A four-seat camper with a facing dinette that drives, flies, and dives. Back to the [README](../../README.md).

## Getting one

- Combine an SUV item, a bed, a chest, and an oak door at a crafting table
- The camper is not given as a starter item
- Use the item on a block's top face with 7 x 7 x 4 clear cells around it
- Each player keeps one camper; placing another removes their previous camper
- Hit an empty camper 3 times without a dye to break it back into the item

## Controls

Use the same controls as the SUV: see the [SUV controls table](suv.md#controls). Seat 0 is the front-left driver; seats 2 and 3 face each other at the dinette.

## How it moves

The camper has the SUV's ground top speed of 0.9 blocks per tick, with gentler acceleration and slower turns for its long body. It flies and dives with the same controls as the SUV. Its long body stops at a wall at both the front and the rear bumper, and it still climbs one-block steps.

The 16 dyes recolor the window band, the stripe with its W, and the lower skirt, leaving the white body unchanged. Headlights light the road up to 27 blocks ahead.
