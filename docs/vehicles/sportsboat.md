A four-seat wake boat that drives on water and lava, including the Nether's lava seas. Back to the [README](../../README.md).

## Getting one

- Craft the **Sports Boat** item at a crafting table from 5 iron ingots and an oak boat
- Use the item on water or lava to place the boat at the liquid surface: tap the bed under it, the liquid itself (up to 12 blocks away), or the side of a shore block that has the liquid next to that side. Tapping the side of a block with no water or lava next to it places nothing
- On land, use the item on the top of a block, as with the SUV
- Hit an empty boat 3 times to get its item back
- The **headlight switch** is crafted from a lever and glowstone dust

## Controls

| Action | PC (keyboard) | Mobile (touch) |
|---|---|---|
| Get in | Right-click the boat | Press the ride button |
| Get out | Shift (sneak) | Sneak button |
| Accelerate / reverse | W / S | Push the stick forward / back |
| Steer | A / D | Push the stick left / right |
| Headlights on / off | Right-click with the switch in hand (driver only) | Tap with the switch in hand (driver only) |
| Repaint | Left-click the boat with a dye | Tap the boat with a dye |

The boat has no handbrake, flying, or diving controls.

The boat seats 4. Aim at a free seat to choose it; the front-right seat drives. A passenger stays in the chosen seat when another rider gets out.

## How it moves

- **On water or lava.** The boat floats on either surface and speeds up, coasts, and turns. The same controls and motion settings apply on both liquids
- **Fire protection.** The hull and dock seats resist fire. Players seated in the boat or its dock seats receive fire resistance and are extinguished while aboard. The short effect expires after they leave. Standing on the deck gives no fire protection, so stepping off into lava is dangerous
- **Deck.** A boat that floats still with no driver for a second holds its place and lays an invisible floor, so players can walk on its deck. Choose a seat by right-clicking it on PC or long-pressing it on mobile. When the deck driver pushes the stick, seated players board the boat in their chosen seats and it sets off. Only the deck driver can set off. Driving off clears the floor and deck seats. The deck floor cannot be broken, not even in Creative, and a docked boat puts back any of it that a command removes within a second
- **Wake and attitude.** While it moves faster than 2 blocks a second, bubbles and foam stream from the jet nozzles under the stern. The bow lifts while the boat gains speed and settles once it reaches a steady speed, and the hull leans into turns, more at higher speed
- **Engine sound.** While someone rides, a V8 burbles with its exhaust bubbling under the water, and the revs and the hiss of the water rise in eight steps with the speed
- **Shores.** It cannot drive forward onto land: shores stop it. On land, whether it was placed there, pushed there by a piston, or left there when the liquid drained, it can only reverse, slowly, so you can back it into the liquid. It cannot climb a block in either direction
- **Ramming.** It damages mobs it hits at speed. The boat and its seated riders take no collision damage
- **Headlights.** Two beams light the water up to about 60 blocks ahead
- **Paint.** The hull starts black. Each of the 16 dyes repaints it in that dye's color, and brown dye turns it back to black; black or brown dye on a black hull does nothing and keeps the dye; each repaint uses one dye
