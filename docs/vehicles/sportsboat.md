A four-seat wake boat that drives on water. Back to the [README](../../README.md).

## Getting one

- Craft the **Sports Boat** item at a crafting table from 5 iron ingots and an oak boat
- Use the item on water to place the boat at the water surface: tap the bed under the water, the water itself (up to 12 blocks away), or the side of a shore block that has water next to that side. Tapping the side of a block with no water next to it places nothing
- On land, use the item on the top of a block, as with the SUV
- Hit an empty boat 3 times to get its item back
- The **headlight switch** is crafted from a lever and glowstone dust

## Controls

| Action | PC (keyboard) | Mobile (touch) |
|---|---|---|
| Get in | Right-click the boat | Press the ride button |
| Get out | Shift (sneak) | Sneak button |
| Accelerate / reverse | W / S | Push the stick forward / back |
| Steer | A / D | Push the stick left / right |
| Headlights on / off | Right-click with the switch in hand (driver only) | Tap with the switch in hand (driver only) |
| Repaint | Left-click the boat with a dye | Tap the boat with a dye |

The boat has no handbrake, flying, or diving controls.

The boat seats 4. The first player to get in drives from the front-right seat and the others ride as passengers. When the driver gets out, a passenger moves up to the driver's seat.

## How it moves

- **On water.** The boat floats on the surface and speeds up, coasts, and turns on the water
- **Deck.** A boat that floats still with no driver for a second holds its place and lays an invisible floor, so players can walk on its deck. Choose a seat by right-clicking it on PC or long-pressing it on mobile. When the deck driver pushes the stick, seated players board the boat in seat order and it sets off; a passenger may move to a lower seat if lower seats were empty. Only the deck driver can set off. Driving off clears the floor and deck seats. The deck floor cannot be broken, not even in Creative, and a docked boat puts back any of it that a command removes within a second
- **Wake and attitude.** While it moves faster than 2 blocks a second, bubbles and foam stream from the jet nozzles under the stern. The bow lifts while the boat gains speed and settles once it reaches a steady speed, and the hull leans into turns, more at higher speed
- **Engine sound.** While someone rides, a V8 burbles with its exhaust bubbling under the water, and the revs and the hiss of the water rise in eight steps with the speed
- **Shores.** It cannot drive forward onto land: shores stop it. On land, whether it was placed there, pushed there by a piston, or left there when the water drained, it can only reverse, slowly, so you can back it into the water. It cannot climb a block in either direction
- **Ramming.** It damages mobs it hits at speed. The boat and the people inside take no damage
- **Headlights.** Two beams light the water up to about 60 blocks ahead
- **Paint.** The hull starts black. Each of the 16 dyes repaints it in that dye's color, and brown dye turns it back to black; black or brown dye on a black hull does nothing and keeps the dye; each repaint uses one dye
