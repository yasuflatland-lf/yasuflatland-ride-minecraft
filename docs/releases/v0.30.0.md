## Summary

This release turns the bulldozer into a building machine with the new Building Tools, adds a guide for playing on a Realm, and makes riding more robust when a vehicle disappears under its rider. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.30.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Select an area from the bulldozer.** Hold the new Selection Wand while driving the bulldozer. Hit a block to set the first corner and use it on a block to set the second; the box between them is drawn as an outline. Sneak and use it at the air to clear the selection.
- **Fill, replace, copy, paste, and undo.** Hold the new Build Tool while driving the bulldozer and use it to open a form with Fill, Replace, Copy, Paste, Undo, and Clear selection. Paste puts a copy back at the same offset from where the bulldozer stands now, with a preview outline that follows the bulldozer. Turn on "Skip air" to leave the blocks under a copy's gaps alone. Each player can undo their last 10 edits.
- **See the whole site while you build.** While you drive the bulldozer and hold the Selection Wand or the Build Tool, the camera moves to the third-person view. It switches back a moment after you put the tool away or at once when you get off.
- **Play on a Realm.** A new guide, [Installing on a Realm](https://github.com/yasuflatland-lf/yasuflatland-ride-minecraft/blob/main/docs/installing-on-a-realm.md), shows how to apply the packs to a world and upload it to a Realm. Players who join need nothing installed.

### Fixes

- Riding no longer breaks when the vehicle under a rider is removed. The vehicles and the camping gear read the rider's mount safely, so a missing mount cannot stop driving or a switch from working.

### Before you update

- The Bulldozer Switches group in the Items tab is gone. The Blade Up/Down switch now sits in the new Building Tools group, the last group in the Items tab, with the Selection Wand and the Build Tool. It keeps its ID, so switches you already have keep working.
- An edit covers at most 100,000 blocks and a copy at most 32,768. Nothing is consumed in Survival, and undo gives nothing back. Containers, signs, spawners, command blocks, vehicles, tents, and the add-on's own blocks are never edited.
- Selections, copies, and the undo history are kept only in memory, so they are lost on reload and when you leave the world.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
