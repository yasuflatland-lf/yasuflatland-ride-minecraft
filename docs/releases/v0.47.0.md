## Summary

The Build Wand can now build round shapes that it could not make before and reshape terrain in one step: clear everything above an area, or fill the hollows below it. Your own edits also stop leaving you stuck inside the blocks they place. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.47.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Build a sphere.** Select a box or a flat horizontal area and choose Sphere, then Fill for a solid ball or Hollow for a shell. On a flat area, you aim the height first. Undo restores the cells as with other builds.
- **Lay a cylinder on its side.** Select a box or a flat horizontal area and choose Sideways cylinder for pipes, tunnels, and beams. The cylinder runs east-west or north-south, whichever is closest to the way you are facing, and offers Fill, Hollow, and Tube. Undo works here too.
- **Clear everything above an area.** Remove above is on every shape menu. It keeps the lower point's layer as a level floor and clears everything from the next layer up to the top of the world. Each side can be up to 32 blocks. A confirmation form shows the size and height first, because this edit cannot be undone and things above can fall.
- **Fill the hollows below an area.** Fill below is also on every shape menu. Using the material you tapped first, it fills connected air and liquids from the higher selected point down, without building outside the selected area or into separate caves it does not connect to. Each side can be up to 32 blocks, and materials that would fall or break, such as sand and gravel, are refused. A confirmation form appears first, because this edit cannot be undone.

### Fixes

- An edit that placed blocks where you stood used to leave you buried. When it finishes, it now moves you straight up to the first spot with room to stand, unless you are riding something. You can still take suffocation damage while the edit runs.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
