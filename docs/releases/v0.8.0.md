## Summary

You can now walk on a sports boat that floats still and pick your seat from its deck, and the SUV has a new four-door body under a black hardtop. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.8.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Walk on a parked sports boat.** Leave a sports boat with no driver on the water, and after about a second it stops bobbing and docks. Step onto it and walk around the cockpit and the bow at deck height. Walking off the edge drops you into the water.
- **Choose your seat from the deck.** While the boat is docked, use a seat to sit in it: right-click it on PC, or long-press it on touch. Each player can pick a different seat.
- **Set off together.** When the player in the driver's seat pushes the stick, everyone sitting on the deck moves into the boat and it drives off. Players still standing on the deck fall into the water as the boat leaves. A rider may end up in a lower seat than the one they picked when a seat in front of them was empty.
- **Hit nothing by accident.** Hits from a player standing on the deck no longer break the boat, so tapping around on touch is safe. You can still pick up a docked boat from the water or the shore, and you can still paint it with a dye from the deck.
- **Drive a reworked SUV.** The SUV now has four doors under a black hardtop, a windshield that leans back toward the driver, trapezoid over-fenders with the tires standing out from the body, detailed eight-spoke wheels, angled bumpers, door handles that swing with the doors, and a more detailed dashboard and hood. The rear wheels sit further back under a new cargo area. The license plates are gone.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definitions and the Resource Pack changed and the packs add a new block and a new entity.
- The SUV's body is a little longer and wider, so it bumps into walls and other mobs slightly sooner than before.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
