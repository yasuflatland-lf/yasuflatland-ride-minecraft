## Summary

This release lets the bulldozer cut through rock under water and dig a shaft when it rises, and gives the sports boat a smoother, glossier hull, flat slanted glass, and a rear-view mirror. The camper gets a louvred vent lid. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.22.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Cut under water with the bulldozer.** The submarine bulldozer now clears the rock in front of its blade while it travels under water, so it can tunnel along the seabed.
- **Dig a shaft upward.** When the bulldozer rises, it clears the column above it so it can surface through solid ground.
- **See a smoother sports boat hull.** The sides and the bow outline are built from long flat planks, so the stepped, jagged edges are gone, and the bow's inner deck outline is smooth too.
- **Get a glossy hull with Vibrant Visuals.** The sports boat hull has a clear-coat finish that shows sharp sky and sun reflections in every paint color.
- **Look back in the boat's mirror.** The sports boat's rear-view mirror now reflects its surroundings with Vibrant Visuals.
- **See through the new glass.** The windshield and side glass are flat, slanted panes instead of blocks, and the windshield pillars are gone.
- **Louvred camper vent.** The camper's roof vent now has a slatted lid with an aluminium edge.
- **See cleaner bulldozer bodywork.** The blade support triangle plate meets the push arm without a gap, and its lower edge is trimmed to fit.

### Fixes

- A moored sports boat or camper no longer stays visually tilted after a failed mooring update; the tilt resets once the update goes through.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new pack versions and entity definitions load.
- The glossy hull and the mirror reflections only show with Vibrant Visuals turned on.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
