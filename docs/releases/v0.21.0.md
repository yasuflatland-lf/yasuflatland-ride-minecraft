## Summary

This release adds a two-seat camp table and a folding fire pit, lets the sports boat travel on lava, and improves the bulldozer's bodywork. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.21.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Set up a camp table.** Craft and place the bamboo-colored folding table outside a vehicle. Two players can sit side by side, and three quick hits fold an empty table back into its item.
- **Light a fire pit.** Craft and place the folding stainless fire pit, then tap it with flint and steel to light it. Tap it again to put it out. The flames and smoke are visual; the pit does not cook food or spread fire. Its polished metal reflects its surroundings with Vibrant Visuals.
- **Boat across lava.** Place and drive the sports boat on lava, including Nether lava seas. Seated riders are protected from fire while aboard. Standing on the deck does not give that protection.
- **See smoother bulldozer panels.** The rear side covers and blade supports now have flat, enclosed surfaces instead of visible stepped edges and gaps.

### Fixes

- The headlight switch now controls the parked camper's headlights from its wheel seat. Other seats continue to control the ceiling lights.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new pack versions and entity definitions load.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
