## Summary

The first release of MyCar: a drivable SUV for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It works on PC (keyboard) and mobile (touch), and needs no experimental toggles. Open `mycar-0.1.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Craft and place an SUV.** Craft the "SUV" item at a crafting table from 5 iron ingots, 1 iron block, and 2 coal blocks, then use it on the ground to place the vehicle. It will not place under a low ceiling.
- **Ride with up to four players.** The front-right seat is the driver's seat; the other three are for passengers. When the driver gets out, passengers stay in their seats instead of moving into the driver's seat. The door of the seat you leave swings open with a sound.
- **Drive with arcade-style handling.** The SUV accelerates, coasts with inertia, and turns right away. Use the handbrake to slide. On mobile, pushing the joystick halfway forward gives full throttle, the same as a keyboard.
- **Climb steps up to two blocks high.** The SUV looks ahead and hops onto two-block steps, head-on or at an angle, without stopping.
- **Ram mobs.** Mobs in front of the SUV take damage based on speed and are knocked aside. Riders are never hurt: crashing into a wall or falling while riding costs no hearts, and the SUV does not hit the occupants of any vehicle.
- **See it move.** The wheels spin, the body tilts, and smoke comes out of the muffler while you ride.
- **Pick it back up.** Punch an empty SUV three times to break it and get the item back. An SUV with someone inside does not break.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
