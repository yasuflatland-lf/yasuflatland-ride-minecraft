## Summary

This release adds a bulldozer: a one-seat ride that pivots on its tracks, hops, flies, and dives like the other vehicles, and clears hills and tunnels with its blade as it drives forward. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.16.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Get a bulldozer.** Every player receives one bulldozer item on joining, including players who already have their starter SUV. You can also craft one on a crafting table from an SUV item, an iron block, and an iron shovel.
- **Place it.** Hold the item and tap the top of a block with a clear 9 by 9 area, three blocks high. Each player keeps one bulldozer; placing another removes your older one. Hit an empty bulldozer three times with an empty hand to take its item back.
- **Drive it.** Push forward or back to drive straight. Push the stick sideways (A or D on PC) to pivot: from a stop it turns in place, and at speed it slows down while it turns. Its top speed is half the SUV's.
- **Hop, fly, and dive.** Tap Jump to hop one block. Double-tap Jump to take off or land, then hold Jump to climb and pull back to sink, in the air and under water. Sneak to get out.
- **Clear the way.** While driving forward, the blade cuts away the blocks it touches ahead, from just above the ground up to the first gap of air. In flight it cuts a tunnel as tall as the bulldozer. Water, lava, chests and other containers with their contents, item frames, campfires, and the add-on's own blocks are left alone, and the cut does not litter the ground with dropped items.
- **Switch on the lights.** Use the Headlight Switch while driving to light the headlights, the rear beacon, and the road ahead.
- **Paint it.** Hit the bulldozer with any dye to recolor its yellow panels. Brown dye brings back the original yellow.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- The blade cuts in every game mode, including Adventure, and it also cuts blocks such as bedrock and barriers. Keep it away from builds you want to protect.
- The bulldozer's color is not kept when you take it back: the item you get is the plain yellow bulldozer.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
