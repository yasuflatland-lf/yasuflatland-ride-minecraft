## Summary

This release reworks the roof tent. It has a new olive green soft-shell look, the SUV tent now sits on the left of the roof with a ladder that matches, and sleeping inside is more comfortable. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.41.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See a new roof tent.** The roof tent has a new soft-shell design with an olive green default fabric, mesh windows, and a front flap. The ground tent and the camper tent use the same design.
- **Climb the ladder on the left.** On the SUV the tent now sits on the left side of the roof, and the ladder reaches the ground at the same place you enter.
- **Stay dry and hidden.** While you are inside, you sit on a seat in the tent, your head stays inside the shell, and a temporary ceiling keeps the rain off you.
- **Open the menu again.** Use the roof tent item while seated, even without sneaking, to reopen the sleeping bag menu. On mobile, the dismount button now gets you out.

### Fixes

- Boarding a vehicle that already has a roof tent no longer shows the obstacle message.
- The SUV rearview mirror now sits close under the roof edge instead of hanging on a long stay.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
