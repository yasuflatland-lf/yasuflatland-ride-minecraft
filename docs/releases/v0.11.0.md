## Summary

This release polishes the camper and the sports boat and fixes several driving problems on walls, brakes, and slow ground. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.11.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Hit the camper anywhere along its front bumper.** Breaking the camper or painting it with a dye now works across the whole width of the front bumper, not only near its middle.
- **Get hints at the camper.** Standing near the front bumper shows that you can break the camper or change its color with a dye. Standing near the driver's seat of a parked camper shows that you can press and hold the seat to drive.
- **Look into a parked camper.** From outside, the windows now show the cabin instead of the scenery behind the camper.
- **Ride a calmer sports boat.** The bow rises half as much as before when the boat speeds up.

### Fixes

- Hitting a wall at an angle now slides the vehicle along the wall instead of stopping it almost at once.
- Hitting a wall no longer turns a vehicle's forward speed into reverse, which used to make the throttle brake and the steering turn the wrong way for a moment.
- Pulling the stick back a little now slows the vehicle at least as much as letting go of it.
- The SUV and the camper drive at a sensible speed on strongly slowing ground such as soul sand instead of crawling.
- When the driver gets out of a flying SUV or camper, a passenger who moves into the driver's seat can keep flying instead of the vehicle starting to land.
- A sports boat that has run aground can reverse back into the water.
- A sports boat leans the right way into turns while reversing.
- Black dye on a new sports boat, which is already black, no longer uses up the dye.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
