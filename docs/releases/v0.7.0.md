## Summary

The sports boat now looks, moves, and sounds like a real wake boat: a rebuilt cockpit, a wake behind the jets, a bow that lifts as it speeds up, a hull that leans into turns, and a V8 engine that revs with its speed. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.7.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Sit in a detailed cockpit.** The boat has a U-shaped rear lounge, wraparound helm seats, a round three-spoke steering wheel, a dashboard display with cupholders, a bow sun pad, and a rear-view mirror. The seats are in the same places as before.
- **Leave a wake.** Bubbles and foam pour out of both jets while someone rides the boat and it is moving.
- **Feel the boat move.** The bow lifts while the boat gains speed and settles once it holds its speed, and the hull leans up to 20 degrees into turns at full speed.
- **Hear the engine.** The boat rumbles like a V8 with a wet exhaust and the hiss of water along the hull, and the revs climb in eight steps as it speeds up.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definitions and the Resource Pack changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
