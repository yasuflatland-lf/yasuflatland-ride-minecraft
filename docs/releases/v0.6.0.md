## Summary

MyCar is now **Yasuflatland Ride**, and it adds a second vehicle: a sports boat that drives on water. The packs also get their own icon in the pack list. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.6.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Craft a sports boat.** Put an oak boat between two iron ingots on the top row and three iron ingots on the bottom row of a crafting table.
- **Launch it on water.** Use the boat item on the water, the bed under it, or the side of a shore block next to the water, and the boat appears at the surface.
- **Drive on water.** The boat seats a driver and three passengers and steers like the SUV. It floats and bobs on the waves, keeps its speed through turns, and stops at the shore instead of driving onto land.
- **Paint it and light it up.** Repaint the hull with any of the 16 dyes (brown dye brings back the black default), and switch on two headlight beams with the headlight switch.
- **Ram mobs.** A boat at full speed damages mobs it hits, and keeps its riders safe.
- **Spot the packs.** The packs show a flying SUV as their icon in the pack list instead of the default placeholder.

### Before you update

- The add-on is renamed, and every ID now starts with `yasu_ride:` instead of `mycar:`. Existing vehicles and vehicle items disappear from your worlds, because the old IDs are not carried over.
- Switch the headlights off on every vehicle before you update. Light blocks from a vehicle whose headlights are on stay in the world after the vehicle disappears.
- Every player receives a free SUV item again on their next join. Get more with the recipe or `/give @s yasu_ride:suv_item`.
- On a dedicated server, delete the old MyCar pack folders from `behavior_packs` and `resource_packs` before you copy in `yasu_ride_bp` and `yasu_ride_rp`. The old and new folders share their UUIDs, so keeping both leaves the server with two packs under one UUID.
- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definitions and the Resource Pack changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
