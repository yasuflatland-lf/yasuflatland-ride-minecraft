## Summary

This release adds a Dutch oven to the camping gear and gives the sports boat a rebuilt helm. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.24.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Cook with a Dutch oven.** Craft the Dutch oven, then stand it on a fire pit or on a camp table. On a fire pit it simmers, and the fire pit shows a grill while something stands on it.
- **Take the Dutch oven back.** Pick it up from its spot to get the item again. A camp table cannot be folded while the Dutch oven stands on it.
- **Drive a sports boat with a new helm.** The console now has a tilted steering wheel with a round hub, a wide 16:9 display set at an angle with a glossy bezel, a throttle dial, a controller pad with a knob, and a slanted switch plate. The dash and frame have a clear-coat shine, and the speaker pods are rounder.

### Fixes

- A Dutch oven stays on its host while the chunk around it is unloaded, instead of being lost.
- The sports boat roof's side skirts are rounder, and their open end triangles are closed.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
