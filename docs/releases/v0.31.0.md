## Summary

This release gives the bulldozer a wide blade and a road paver, adds an upper bunk to the camper, lets you set Dutch ovens and Gas Lanterns anywhere on the camp table, and lights more of the camper with its headlights. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.31.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Cut wider with the Wide Blade.** The new Wide Blade switch in the Building Tools group doubles the bulldozer's blade: it cuts 11 columns instead of 5, and the ram reaches the same width, raised or lowered.
- **Lay a road with the Road Paver.** Turn on the new Road Paver switch while you hold a block, and the bulldozer lays that block behind it as a road at the blade's width, on land, over liquid, and in flight.
- **See your first selection corner.** When you have set only one corner with the Selection Wand, that block is outlined with end rod particles that only you can see. The selection outline takes over once the second corner is set.
- **Sleep in the upper bunk.** The camper now has an upper bunk over its bed. Each level is its own bed and sets your spawn point at its own spot on the floor.
- **Set things anywhere on the camp table.** Aim at a spot on the Camp Table to put a Dutch oven or a Gas Lantern there. The table holds up to 4 items, and items cannot overlap. A Dutch oven can still stand on a Fire Pit.
- **See the camper's roof and tail lamps glow.** Turning on the headlights now also lights the five front roof lamps with their domes, the four red rear roof lamps, and the two round red tail lamps.

### Fixes

- A Dutch oven on a Camp Table or a Fire Pit now disappears with no drop when its host is broken, instead of closing its lid and falling to the ground.

### Before you update

- The Camp Table can no longer be sat at. It is now a surface for placing things.
- A Gas Lantern standing on a Camp Table disappears with its light and with no drop when the table is broken or removed. A lantern on the ground is never touched.
- Dutch ovens saved on a table before this update stay at their old spot.
- The Wide Blade and Road Paver switches sit in the Building Tools group of the Items tab.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
