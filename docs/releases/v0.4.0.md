## Summary

Painting works from outside the SUV on a phone now: tap it with a dye and it changes color. The ride button gets you in whatever you hold. Open `mycar-0.4.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Paint by tapping.** Standing next to the SUV, tap it with a dye on a phone, or left-click it with a dye on PC. It turns the color of the dye and uses one dye.
- **Get in with a dye in hand.** The ride button on a phone, or right-click on PC, gets you in even while you hold a dye. It no longer repaints the SUV.

### Fixes

- On a phone, tapping the SUV with a dye from outside did nothing and counted as a hit toward breaking it. It now repaints the SUV and never breaks it.
- Tapping quickly with one dye no longer uses more than one dye for a single color change.

### Before you update

- To break an empty SUV back into its item, hit it three times without a dye in hand. Hits with a dye only repaint.
- On a phone, a rider can no longer repaint the SUV they are sitting in. Get out and tap it instead. On PC, a rider can still left-click to repaint.
- Only the Behavior Pack changed; the Resource Pack is the same as in 0.3.0. Restart the server after installing so it loads the new Behavior Pack.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
