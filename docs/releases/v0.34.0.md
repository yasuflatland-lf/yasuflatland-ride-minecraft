## Summary

This release makes the SUV larger so a seated head fits under the roof, and lets the bulldozer cut rock that sticks out ahead of its body. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.34.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Sit in a roomier SUV.** The whole SUV is about 12 percent bigger with its proportions unchanged, so the seated head no longer sticks out above the roof.
- **Dig on without stopping.** With the blade lowered on the ground, the bulldozer now also cuts rock that sticks out ahead of it, up to the height of its body, even when there is a gap below it.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.
- The SUV is bigger, so it no longer fits the 3 x 3 space it used to; leave a little more room when you park it.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
