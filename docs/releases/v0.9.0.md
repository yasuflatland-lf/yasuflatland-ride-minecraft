## Summary

The vehicle limit now counts each player's vehicles on their own, so placing a vehicle never removes one that belongs to someone else. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.9.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Keep your own vehicles.** Each player keeps up to 10 SUVs and 1 sports boat. Placing one more removes only your own oldest vehicle of that kind, so your SUVs stay when you place a second boat, and other players' vehicles are never touched.

### Fixes

- Until now the world held at most 10 vehicles in total, so one player placing vehicles could remove vehicles another player had placed. Now the limit applies per player.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- Vehicles placed before this update, and vehicles summoned with a command, have no owner. They share one world-wide limit of 10, as before, and placing new vehicles from items does not remove them.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
