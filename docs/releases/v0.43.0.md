## Summary

This release gives the SUV a new look modeled on a rugged off-road 4x4, with a matching cabin, and makes it lean more smoothly in turns. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.43.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Drive a reshaped SUV.** The body and wheels follow an off-road 4x4 shape, with a hardtop, a flatter nose, and mirrors. The headlights no longer sit on a separate plate, and the gaps around the rear pillar are closed.
- **Sit in a refitted cabin.** The interior and seats are fitted to the new body.
- **Fold the SUV cleanly in flight.** Each wheel now folds out straight along its axle and clears the body.
- **Mount the roof tent on the new roof.** The roof rack bars sit on the hardtop.

### Fixes

- The SUV leans into turns with a smooth ease instead of a sudden step.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
