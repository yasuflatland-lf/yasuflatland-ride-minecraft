## Summary

This release reshapes the SUV and makes it bigger. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.27.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Drive a reshaped SUV.** The SUV has a longer wheelbase, a longer hood, a windshield laid further back, a higher ground clearance, shorter side windows under a lower roof, and low bar bumpers. It keeps its colors and its eight-slot grille.
- **Ride with your head under the roof.** The whole SUV, tires included, is 1.3 times bigger, so a seated player's head stays inside the roof in every seat.
- **See the whole SUV while flying.** The camera behind a flying or diving SUV sits further back, so the bigger body fits on screen.

### Before you update

- Placing an SUV now needs three blocks of free space above the ground instead of two.
- The bigger SUV needs deeper water to dive as a submarine, and it takes more room to pass through gaps and tunnels.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
