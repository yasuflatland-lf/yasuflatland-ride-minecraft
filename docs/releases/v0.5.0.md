## Summary

The SUV becomes a submarine: drive it into deep water and it dives, with sealed windows, bronze propellers, and a sonar ping. In flight the folded wheels now hang on arms, and a world keeps at most ten vehicles. Open `mycar-0.5.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Dive.** Drive into water at least as deep as the SUV is tall and it turns into a submarine: the windows seal blue, and the rear wheels turn backward on gunmetal arms around spinning bronze propellers.
- **Steer under water.** The submarine handles like the flying SUV and banks into turns. It holds its depth; hold Jump (Space on PC, the jump button on a phone) to rise, and pull the stick back (S on PC) to sink.
- **Breathe freely.** Riders do not run out of air while the submarine is under water.
- **Leave the water.** Hold Jump until the whole body is out of the water to take off and fly, or drive onto a shore to become a car again. An empty submarine floats up to the surface.
- **Hear the sonar.** A ridden submarine plays a quiet sonar ping under water.
- **See the wheels on arms in flight.** The folded wheels now hang out from the body on gunmetal arms instead of lying flat against it.

### Fixes

- The flying SUV rolls into turns again. During development the body stopped banking, and the rear propellers showed on the ground; both are fixed.

### Before you update

- A world now keeps at most 10 vehicles across all players. Placing an eleventh removes the oldest one without dropping an item, and riders of the removed vehicle are dismounted. A world that already has more than 10 vehicles loses the oldest ones down to 10 after the update.
- Breaking a vehicle frees its slot. A vehicle removed another way, such as with `/kill`, keeps its slot until newer vehicles push it out.
- Land any flying SUV before you update. Its flight state was renamed, so an SUV in the air when the server restarts comes back as a car.
- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definition and the Resource Pack changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
