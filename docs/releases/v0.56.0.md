## Summary

This release gives the scooter seated rider poses, new wheel and body detail, and new engine sounds, and fixes several scooter riding problems. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.56.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Ride the scooter seated.** The driver and the passenger now sit in their own poses, with the legs forward and the arms no longer swinging while riding.
- **Hear new engine sounds.** The scooter plays new idle and rev recordings, with rev loops that join without a gap.
- **See a more detailed scooter.** The wheels have hub nuts, rim screws and a tread pattern, and the underbody, floor and stand are refined.

### Fixes

- The scooter rider sits at the right height, and the stand, plate and model textures are corrected.
- The scooter stand animation now loads on clients.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
