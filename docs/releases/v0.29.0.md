## Summary

This release adds the outdoor Gas Lantern to Camping Gear (Outside), removes the inside Lantern, and smooths and tidies the sports boat. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.29.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Put out a Gas Lantern.** Camping Gear (Outside) has a new Gas Lantern. Place it on the ground; it is crafted, and hitting it three times takes it back. Hold the Headlight Switch and press or tap it to turn it on and off. While lit it lights up its surroundings.
- **See smoother sports boat headlights.** The lenses on the bow are built from finer panels, so their top and bottom edges now read as smooth curves.
- **Sit behind the wheel.** The sports boat's driver and front passenger sit a little further back, and the two front buckets move with them.
- **Read "Drive" on the driver's seat.** A docked sports boat shows "Drive" on the driver's seat button. Passenger seats keep "Sit".

### Fixes

- With the icebreaker ON, the sports boat no longer digs into the bed. It cuts a level tunnel from its hull floor upward, so it stays at the water surface when it meets a mountain instead of sinking into a pit.
- The Sports Boat Equipment group in the Items tab lists only the Icebreaker Switch. The Equipment Box is hidden from the menu but still arrives as the gift when you first place a sports boat.

### Before you update

- The inside Lantern is removed from Camping Gear (Inside). A camper saved with a Lantern or an Oil Lamp loads without it, and nothing is refunded.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
