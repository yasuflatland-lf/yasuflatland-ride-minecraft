## Summary

This release gives the SUV a detailed new look, with a steel-painted body, round headlights, detailed tail lamps and a rebuilt dashboard. It also lets you switch the camper's Gas Lantern and toggle the Dutch oven lid with a press of any item. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.33.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See the SUV's steel body.** Every paint shows panel lines, metallic flake and door handle recesses, and the hardtop is matte. Each paint also has its own shine and texture set.
- **See the SUV's round headlights and tail lamps.** The headlights are round textured lamps behind a cutout bezel. The tail lamps and the third brake light are detailed and light up with the headlights.
- **Sit in the SUV's new dashboard.** The dashboard is grained black plastic with vents, a clock, a centre stack and a passenger tray. The gauge cluster is detailed and the steering wheel has a leather finish.
- **Switch the Gas Lantern with any item.** Press it with whatever you are holding; no light tool is needed.
- **Toggle the Dutch oven lid with a press.**

### Fixes

- The camper no longer has roof rails.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
