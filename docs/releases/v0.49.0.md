## Summary

The Build Wand is easier and safer to use when pasting and rotating structures. Pasting now needs a deliberate sneak press, the paste preview sits on the ground, and a new rotate mode lets you turn a structure with taps. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.49.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Paste on purpose.** A plain press opens the paste menu, and you paste only by sneaking and pressing. This stops accidental pastes.
- **See where the paste will land.** The paste outline is drawn in horizontal and vertical layers, and it snaps to the highest ground under the structure.
- **Rotate with taps.** After you choose Rotate, tap the wand to turn the structure a quarter turn each time, then finish to keep it.
- **Undo up to ten edits.** One undo history now covers every kind of edit, so you can step back through ten mixed edits.
- **Aim the height more precisely.** The height step now picks the layer that contains the point you are aiming at.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
