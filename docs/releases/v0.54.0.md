## Summary

This release adds the scooter, a light two-seat ride, and a warp block and tool for travelling between named points in your world. The Build Wand paste now turns to follow the way you are facing. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.54.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Ride the scooter.** A classic two-seat scooter that drives on the ground and across the water surface at the SUV's speed. It leans and steers into turns, stands on its centre stand when empty, and lays its wheels flat like hover discs when it flies. Riders hold the handlebar and lean with it. It can be repainted with any of the 16 dyes and has a headlight switch.
- **Warp between named points.** Place a warp block and give it a name. The warp tool lists every point in the world, and choosing one teleports you there.
- **Paste in the direction you face.** A pasted Build Wand structure now turns to follow your gaze instead of always keeping the direction it was copied in.

### Fixes

- Warp landings now check the floor correctly, so you arrive on solid ground.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.
- Each player keeps at most 10 scooters; placing another removes that player's oldest one.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
