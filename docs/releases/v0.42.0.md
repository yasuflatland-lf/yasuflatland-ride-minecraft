## Summary

This release gives the tents a new trapezoid shape with entrance and mesh details, and makes tents safer to fold and close. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.42.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See a reshaped tent.** The ground tent is now a trapezoid with a tarp, a framed front with a mesh entrance door, mesh side windows, an inner tent, and a lean that follows real tents.
- **Close the flap without getting stuck.** If you stand in or next to the flap when it closes, you are moved out instead of the flap refusing to close.

### Fixes

- Tents placed in an older layout are folded safely when their chunk loads, instead of being left behind.
- The tiny collision box of the ground tent no longer traps players.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.
- Tents placed with an older version are folded when they load. Place them again afterward.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
