## Summary

This release adds Stairs and Terrace to the Build Wand and makes the wand keep your selection between edits. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.60.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Build stairs.** Select a straight row at one height, choose Stairs, look at the goal and press. The stairs run from the row to the block you look at, up or down, at up to 45 degrees. Pick Passage (steps, fill below and clear the space above), Fill below, or Steps only. A stair block as your first point gives real stairs. Undo restores everything.
- **Cut the terrain into steps.** Choose Terrace to remove the ground above the stair surface without placing blocks. It is undoable.
- **One-wide stairs.** Tap one cell twice and confirm height 1 to build a one-wide staircase, for example indoors.
- **Keep building from the same selection.** A confirmed box stays selected without opening a menu, and a build leaves the result selected. Set the height live by gaze, adjust it later from the selection menu, and use Copy and Cut straight from the selection.
- **Rotate with Paste Rotate.** Paste Rotate turns the selection itself. The separate Rotate box entry is gone.
- **Build roofs with stairs.** Roofs made from a stair material now slope at 45 degrees outward.
- **Ram in reverse.** The camper and the bulldozer can ram while reversing.

### Fixes

- Copies no longer include Warp Blocks, and Rotate and Cut refuse a box that holds one.
- Fill below now always stops at the higher of your two tapped points.
- An aimed end point is kept inside the world height range.
- A seat held for a rider stays reserved when they mount without a request.
- A failed deck seat spawn no longer skips the rest of the vehicle's tick.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
