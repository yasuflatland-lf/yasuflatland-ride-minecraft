## Summary

This release adds a two-room tent to the camping gear: pitch it on open ground near where you park, walk in through its flaps, sleep on its two mattresses, switch on its hanging lantern, and paint it with any dye. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.15.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Craft a tent.** On a crafting table, put wool of any color in the top middle and both middle sides, a stick in the center, and string in the two bottom corners. The tent is also in the Camping Gear group in Creative.
- **Pitch it.** Hold the tent and tap the top of a block. The tent opens toward you: a tarp in front, a front room behind it, and a bedroom at the back. It needs a flat, solid 7 x 12 patch of ground with 4 blocks of clear space above it; grass, flowers, and snow layers in the way are cleared. When it cannot go up, a message tells you why, for example when it is too close to a vehicle, or when a player or an animal stands where it would go, and you keep the tent item. A world holds up to four tents in all.
- **Walk in and out.** The front room has openings at the front and on both sides, and the bedroom has its own door. Tap a flap to roll it up or drop it; each flap moves on its own, and a flap will not close while someone stands in it. All flaps are closed when you pitch the tent.
- **Sleep in it.** The bedroom has two mattresses. Long-press (or right-click) one at night to skip to morning. Long-pressing a mattress at any time sets your spawn point there.
- **Switch on the lantern.** Stand in either room and use the Headlight Switch, the same item that turns on the camper's ceiling lights.
- **Close up for the night.** The walls and closed flaps are built to keep monsters out, and a faint light from the ceiling stops them spawning inside the rooms.
- **Sit and build inside.** Place Low Chairs and ordinary blocks in the tent and under the tarp.
- **Paint it.** Hit the tent with any dye to recolor its outer fly, its tarp, and its three outer flaps; the inner tent stays white. Brown dye brings back the original tan.
- **Take it down.** Hit the tent three times in a row from outside to fold it back into its item; each hit shows how many are left. It will not come down while someone is in either room. Chairs and blocks you placed inside stay where they are.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- A tent's color is not kept when you take it down: the item you get back is the plain tan tent.
- You cannot place a vehicle on ground a tent covers, including under its tarp.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
