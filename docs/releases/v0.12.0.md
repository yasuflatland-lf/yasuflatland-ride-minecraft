## Summary

This release makes a parked camper a place to live in: sleep in its bed, decorate it with camping gear, and use its shower and toilet. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.12.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Sleep in the camper's bed.** In a parked camper, long-press the bed at the rear left to set your spawn point there. At night the screen fades to black and the night is skipped to the morning; during the day you see "You can sleep only at night". The bed is solid, so you can stand on it.
- **See new furniture in the camper.** Along the right side there is now a tall shelf, a counter with a stove and a sink, a wall cabinet above it, and a shower-toilet room in the rear corner. A small hook hangs from the ceiling above the table. You can walk through the furniture.
- **Decorate a parked camper with camping gear.** Nineteen 1970s-style items, such as a potted plant, a percolator, a lava lamp, and a macrame hanging planter, go on the dashboard, the table, the dinette seats, the shelf, the stove, the counter, the wall cabinet, and the ceiling hook. Hold an item and long-press inside the parked camper to place it on the nearest free spot that fits it. Long-press a placed item with an empty hand to take it back. Placed items come back every time you park, and return to you when you break the camper back into its item.
- **Get the camping gear.** The first time you place a camper, you get a Camping Gear Box; use it to unpack all 19 items. In Creative, the items are in the Camping Gear group at the end of the Items tab.
- **Use the shower.** With an empty hand, long-press the floor of the shower-toilet from outside to open or close its door, and from inside to run the shower for 5 seconds.
- **Sit on the toilet.** In a parked camper, long-press the toilet to sit on it, and sneak to stand up.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- Decor appears only while the camper is parked; it is not visible while driving.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
