## Summary

This release makes the parked camper's kitchen and storage work the way players expect: open the wall cabinet and keep things inside, put two items on each shelf board and on the table, and look out of a new window over the stove. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.13.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Open the wall cabinet.** In a parked camper, tap a door of the cabinet over the counter with an empty hand to open it; tap the open door again to shut it. The two doors open separately. Each compartment holds two items: hold an item and press inside an open compartment to put it there. Items inside are hidden while the door is shut, and the doors shut when you drive off.
- **Put two items on each shelf board and on the table.** Press the end of a shelf board or the table where you want the item. Tap an item with an empty hand to take back the one at that end.
- **Look out from the kitchen.** A new window on the door side, over the counter and the stove, matches the window ahead of the door.
- **Cook on one burner.** The stove now has one burner in the middle of its top, and the item you put on the stove stands on it.
- **Stand up inside.** Standing up from a seat in a parked camper leaves you in the aisle next to that seat, instead of outside the camper.

### Fixes

- The camper door opens or closes once per tap; a single tap no longer opened and then closed it.
- Items go where you press: a press on the side of the stove, a shelf, or the table no longer picks a spot you did not aim at.
- The dashboard sits just under the windshield, shaped like the reference photos, and the front roof cap, the stove, and the rear windows look like the real camper.
- Decor shows in the parked camper, the hanging planter hangs from its hook, and the seats you can sit on while parked say "Sit" instead of "Ride".
- Pressing the shower-toilet itself opens its door from outside and runs the shower from inside.
- A press on a taken dinette seat no longer puts your item somewhere else.
- Players can no longer break the parked camper's room, and lost room blocks come back.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- Items saved on a stove burner come back on the single burner. Items saved on top of the wall cabinet in 0.12.0 go to the nearest free spot, because the cabinet now keeps its items inside.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
