## Summary

This release makes the vehicle lights glow on devices with Vibrant Visuals, tidies the camper and bulldozer look, and sorts the camping gear into inside and outside groups. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.20.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **See the lights glow.** With Vibrant Visuals on, the lit headlights of every vehicle, the camper's ceiling lights, and the bulldozer's rear beacon shine at full strength while the lights are on. Without Vibrant Visuals the lamps look as before, and the camper and sports boat headlights now also shine full-bright when lit.
- **Find the camping gear in two groups.** The Creative inventory now lists the gear for inside the camper and the gear for outside (the low chair and the tent) separately.
- **Notice the camper's new details.** The amber lamp that sat in the middle of the body is now a pair of small amber lamps at the rear corners, the door handle sits just under the stripe, and the stripe no longer has a small step where it meets the W. The rear-right toilet window is now a vent cover with a mesh.
- **Step on a yellow mesh.** The step beside the bulldozer's cab is now a yellow wire mesh.

### Before you update

- This release needs Minecraft 1.21.120 or later. Devices on an older version refuse the Resource Pack.
- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- The glow only shows with Vibrant Visuals, and it has not been checked on a Vibrant Visuals device yet.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
