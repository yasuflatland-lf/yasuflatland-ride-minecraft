## Summary

This release adds touch-friendly building with the Build Wand, new paste and undo controls, and smoother rendering for every vehicle. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.62.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Build by tapping on touch devices.** A tap target appears in front of you, and pressing it moves the Build Wand through its steps without aiming a crosshair. Long presses also work in the height step and in the selected flows.
- **Re-aim a paste.** Paste Place now works from idle or from a selection, ends after one paste, and selects the pasted box so you can adjust it.
- **Turn a paste by its tapped span.** Paste Rotate turns the span you tapped right after the second tap.
- **Step back with Build Undo.** Undo now steps back a fresh selection endpoint, and shows messages when the start or end is cleared.
- **See the selection more clearly.** The outline particles are larger, horizontal edges are denser, and endpoint markers also show on ground-level blocks. The shape menu opens as soon as you confirm the height.

### Fixes

- Vehicles, camping gear, the tent, the sports boat, and the bulldozer draw fewer hidden faces, so they render more smoothly. The bulldozer blade edges are also cleaner.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
