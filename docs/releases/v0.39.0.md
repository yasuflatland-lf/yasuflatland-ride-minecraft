## Summary

This release adds cylinders and cubes to the Build Wand, shows dimensions while you aim at a height, and gives the camper a flatter front. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.39.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Build cylinders and cubes.** Select a horizontal rectangle with the Build Wand, choose Cylinder or Cube, and aim at the height or use the slider. Choose Fill for a solid shape, Hollow for a closed shell, or Tube for walls with an open top and bottom. Cylinder is also available on a box, using its existing height; a rectangular footprint makes an oval cylinder.
- **See the size before building.** While aiming at the height of a roof, cylinder, or cube, the Build Wand shows the width, depth, and height. After an edit, the completion message explains how to open the utility menu and Undo it.
- **A flatter camper front.** The camper's front is squared off, and its two grilles have a shallow fold.

### Before you update

- On the iPad, a short tap on air sends nothing to the game. Use a long press of about one second for air actions with the Build Wand, including confirming a height and opening the utility menu. Tapping a block works as usual.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
