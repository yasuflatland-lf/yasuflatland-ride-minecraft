## Summary

The SUV can fly now, light the road at night, and come in three colors, and it is much easier to drive from a phone. Every player also gets an SUV the first time they join, so they can drive right away. Open `mycar-0.2.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Fly.** While driving, double-tap Jump to take off: the wheels fold flat, glow blue underneath, and the SUV rises about 1.5 blocks. Drive forward and look up or down to climb or sink; look level, or stop, to hold your altitude. Double-tap Jump again to land gently without losing hearts. If the driver gets out in the air, the empty SUV sinks slowly to the ground.
- **Switch on the headlights.** Craft a headlight switch from a lever and glowstone dust. While you drive, use it (right-click or tap) to make the lamps glow and light up the road up to 27 blocks straight ahead at night. Use it again to switch them off; no light is left behind.
- **Repaint your SUV.** Use green or red dye on a placed SUV to repaint it, the way you dye a sheep. One dye is used each time (none in Creative), and holding a dye does not make you get in.
- **Start with an SUV.** Each player gets one SUV item the first time they join the world, so nobody has to craft one before driving. Players who joined before this update get it on their next join. Crafting still works for more SUVs.
- **Enjoy a rounder look.** The wheels, spare tire, steering wheel, and gauges are built from small rotated blocks, so they look round instead of square.

### Fixes

- On a phone, tapping the ground with the SUV item now places the SUV, and the tap no longer also opens a chest or toggles a door.
- On a phone, pressing Jump while riding no longer throws you out of the SUV, so the handbrake and flight work from touch.
- On a phone, holding the stick straight left or right from a stop now creeps forward while turning, instead of backing up and turning the wrong way.
- Doors swing outward when you get out, instead of into the cabin.

### Before you update

- When the driver gets out, a passenger now moves into the driver's seat and can drive. In 0.1.0 passengers stayed in their seats.
- A quick double tap of the handbrake on the ground takes off. Space out handbrake taps if you want to stay on the ground.
- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definition and the Resource Pack changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
