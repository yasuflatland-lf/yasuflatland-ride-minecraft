## Summary

This release makes the scooter rider's arms look right in first person. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.59.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See your hands while driving the scooter.** In first person you now see the vanilla hands, and both arms are drawn even when you hold nothing.
- **Hold the handlebar in third person.** The driver's arms are posed on the handlebar, and the first-person view keeps its own pose.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
