## Summary

This release adds the camper: a four-seat vehicle that drives, flies, and dives like the SUV, and parks as a small room you can walk around in and build on. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.10.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Craft a camper.** Combine an SUV item, a bed, a chest, and an oak door at a crafting table, then use the camper item on the ground with a 9 x 9 x 4 clear space. Each player keeps one camper; placing another removes their previous one.
- **Drive, fly, and dive.** The camper uses the SUV's controls and reaches the SUV's top speed on the ground, with gentler acceleration and slower turns for its long body. Its driver sits on the right, as in the SUV, and the front seats sit high enough to see the road over the dashboard.
- **Sit at the dinette.** Seats 2 and 3 face each other at a table on the left side of the camper.
- **See the whole camper in flight.** While the camper flies or dives, the third-person camera pulls back far enough to show the whole camper. The SUV and the sports boat keep the usual distance.
- **Park and walk inside.** Stop on flat ground and leave the controls alone for a second. The camper parks, and you can walk inside, sit at the dinette, and place blocks on the floor and the roof. Tap the door from either side to open or close it. Grass, flowers, and snow layers in the way are cleared; anything else shows "Can't park here".
- **Drive off with everyone aboard.** Sit in the driver's seat and push forward or back. Players standing inside or on the roof take a free seat, or step outside the door when no seat is free. While a block you placed is still inside or on the roof, the camper stays parked and asks you to clear it first.
- **Paint it and light the way.** The 16 dyes recolor the window band, the stripe, and the lower skirt, and the headlight switch lights the road up to 27 blocks ahead. Hit the front bumper of an empty camper 3 times without a dye to break it back into its item.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
