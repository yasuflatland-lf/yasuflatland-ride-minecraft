## Summary

This release adds the roof tent: a tent you set on top of the SUV or the camper, climb into, and sleep in. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.40.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Get a roof tent.** Every player in Survival or Adventure mode receives one roof tent item the first time they join the world. Respawning does not give another one.
- **Set it on a vehicle.** Use the roof tent item on the SUV or the camper to fold it out on the roof. The SUV carries it on the left side with a ladder that reaches the ground. You can also place it on a block on the ground. The vehicle must be parked and empty of a driver.
- **Climb in and sleep.** Sneak next to the foot of the ladder, or sneak at the tent, to crawl in. Interact with the tent again to open the sleeping bag menu, where you choose Sleep or Get out. Sleeping at night sets your spawn point to the tent. One player fits in a tent at a time.
- **Get out.** Choose Get out in the menu or sneak again. You are placed on the ground beside the vehicle. You are also placed there if you leave, die, or change dimension.
- **Paint it.** Dye the roof tent the same way as your other camping gear.
- **Fold it away.** Hit the tent three times to fold it back into an item.

### Before you update

- A roof tent cannot be placed on a vehicle that is being driven or is moving. If the vehicle starts moving or is removed, the tent folds into an item that returns to its owner, and anyone inside is placed on the ground first. Removing a tent also clears the spawn point it set.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
