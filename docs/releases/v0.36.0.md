## Summary

This release gives the SUV driver a clearer view of the horizon and makes the hover glows under the SUV round. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.36.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See the horizon from the driver's seat.** All four SUV seats sit a little higher, so looking straight ahead you see over the hood and dashboard while your head stays under the roof.
- **See round hover glows in flight.** The glows under the SUV's wheels are now round discs instead of flat diamond-shaped slabs.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
