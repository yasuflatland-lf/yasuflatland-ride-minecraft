## Summary

This release brings back persona skins, makes building and camping gear behave more predictably, and tidies up a lot of internals. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.64.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Copy taller structures.** The Build Wand can now copy, cut, and rotate boxes up to 384 blocks tall (it was 256). The width and depth limits stay at 64.

### Fixes

- Your persona or Character Creator skin shows again in worlds with this add-on, instead of Steve, and you can change your appearance without a Molang error.
- After Rotate, the tap targets follow the rotated box, and Build Undo of a rotation restores both the selection and the tap targets.
- Cut and Rotate now say "stay close so the box is loaded" when part of the box is not loaded, instead of saying the box is too large.
- A vehicle is taken back only after three hits from the same player. Hits from different players no longer add up.
- A sofa, dining chair, dining table, toilet, washstand, or beach chair can no longer be placed on top of another one.
- A roof tent's ceiling blocks in an unloaded area are now cleared once the area loads, instead of being left behind.
- After a script reload, a camper or boat that drives away right away no longer stays marked as docked, and a restored camper only lays its room where parking is allowed.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
