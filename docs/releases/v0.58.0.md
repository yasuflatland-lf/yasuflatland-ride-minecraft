## Summary

This release makes the scooter's smoke, lamps, and paint look better while using less texture memory, and fixes recipes that did not unlock for some camping items. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.58.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See bigger exhaust smoke.** The scooter's exhaust smoke is larger, denser, and trails behind it.
- **Watch the riders lean more naturally.** The scooter driver and passenger lean by the total body roll of the scooter.
- **See the scooter lamps light up.** The dome lamp glows orange and the brake lamp lens glows red.
- **Paint the scooter with a uniform finish.** Paint colors now share one metal and gloss setting across the scooter body.

### Fixes

- The fire pit, gas lantern, Dutch oven, and toilet recipes now unlock in the recipe book.
- The scooter uses far less texture memory, so it no longer risks running out of memory on small devices.
- The warp block is renamed with a hit and broken with three taps.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
