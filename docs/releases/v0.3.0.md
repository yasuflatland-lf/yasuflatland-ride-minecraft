## Summary

Flying feels more like an airplane now: the SUV banks into turns, the camera pulls back so you can see it, and you climb and sink with the same buttons as Creative flight. The SUV also has engine and wind sounds, sixteen colors, a detailed interior, and it climbs one-block steps again with a driver aboard. Open `mycar-0.3.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Climb and sink like Creative flight.** While flying, hold Jump (Space on PC, the jump button on a phone) to climb, and pull the stick back (S on PC) to sink. This works while hovering in place too. Looking up or down no longer changes your altitude, so you can look around freely.
- **Bank into turns.** In the air the SUV rolls into turns like an airplane, up to 30 degrees, and sinks a little while it is banked.
- **See your SUV in flight.** When the SUV leaves the ground, everyone aboard switches to the third-person camera. Your own camera comes back when you land or get out.
- **Land on the wheels.** When you come down to land, the wheels start to unfold about four blocks above the ground. Sinking onto the ground with the stick also lands the SUV right away.
- **Hear the SUV.** A slow diesel beat plays while you drive, and rushing air plays while you fly. Both change with your speed.
- **Paint it any of sixteen colors.** Every vanilla dye now repaints the SUV the color of its name. Brown dye brings back the original khaki.
- **Sit in a detailed cabin.** Pleated black leather seats, a floor shift lever in a rubber boot, pedals, and an instrument panel with a large speedometer and small gauges.

### Fixes

- With a driver aboard, the SUV climbs one-block steps again instead of stopping dead.
- The SUV no longer hops over flowers, grass, carpets, or torches.
- Riders sit higher, so their legs no longer show under the floor.

### Before you update

- In flight, looking up or down no longer climbs or sinks; use Jump and the stick instead. Pulling the stick back in the air sinks instead of braking or reversing.
- SUVs painted green or red in 0.2.0 keep their color.
- Restart the server after installing, because the entity definition and the Resource Pack changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
