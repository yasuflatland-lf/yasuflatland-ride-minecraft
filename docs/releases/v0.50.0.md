## Summary

Two new Resort Gear pieces join the set: a Toilet you can sit on and a Washstand with a running tap. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.50.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Sit on the Toilet.** Place it on the floor and interact to sit down. The lid lifts as you sit, and when you stand up it closes and flushes with a recorded flush sound.
- **Run the Washstand tap.** Interact with the Washstand and water runs for a few seconds with the sound of running water, then stops on its own.
- **Craft and recover them.** Both pieces have crafting recipes and are picked up again by hitting them three times, like the other Resort Gear.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
