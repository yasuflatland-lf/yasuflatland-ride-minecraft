## Summary

This release gives the bulldozer its own diesel sounds and lets its driver step out beside the cab door. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.18.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Hear the bulldozer.** The engine idles with a deep, slow diesel thump and works harder as you drive and as the blade pushes into the ground. The tracks clank while it moves, and the hydraulics whine when you raise or lower the blade.
- **Step out beside the door.** When the driver gets off the bulldozer, they stand outside the left cab door instead of behind the bulldozer. If that spot is blocked, has no ground under it, or is in water, the game picks the spot as before.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
