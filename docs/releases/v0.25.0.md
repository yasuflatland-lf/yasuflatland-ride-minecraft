## Summary

This release swaps the Oil Lamp for a Lantern and removes the Lava Lamp from the camping gear. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.25.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Light up the camp with a Lantern.** The Lantern replaces the Oil Lamp. It has a glass globe, a wire cage, a cap with a knob, and a side loop, and it fits on the table, on a shelf board, in the wall cabinet, and in the fridge.

### Fixes

- A camper saved with an Oil Lamp now shows a Lantern in the same spot.

### Before you update

- The Lava Lamp is gone from the camping gear. In a camper saved with a Lava Lamp, that cell shows the empty furniture and the lamp is not handed back.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
