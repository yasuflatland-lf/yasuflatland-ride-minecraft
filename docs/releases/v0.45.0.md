## Summary

The camper looks much closer to a real classic motorhome, the SUV's rear corner is reshaped, and switches now confirm themselves in the chase camera. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.45.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **See the reshaped camper front.** The grille leans and folds to a shallow point, the windshield stands upright with its centre forward, and the roof overhang runs down to a V above it.
- **See the camper's new side details.** The entry door has an aluminium frame with hinges and a latch plate, the fold-out step is made of thin panels, and the water fill and the louvred storage hatches have their full detail.
- **See the SUV's new rear corner.** The rear bumper wraps the corners, the spare sits in a notch, and the fuel door is on the vehicle's left.
- **See your switches work in third person.** Switching the headlights on the SUV or camper, or the blade, wide blade, road paver, or headlights on the bulldozer, shows a short message while you are in the chase camera.
- **Fly the bulldozer with a closer camera.** Its chase camera now sits nearer, like the SUV's.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
