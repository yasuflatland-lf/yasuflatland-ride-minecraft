## Summary

This release adds Sports Boat Equipment and an icebreaker mode for the sports boat. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.28.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Get the Sports Boat Equipment Box.** Place a sports boat for the first time and you receive the box once. Open it to get an Icebreaker Switch. Both are in the new Sports Boat Equipment group of the Items tab, and the switch can be crafted from a lever, an iron ingot and packed ice.
- **See the switch on the dash.** While you drive, hold the Icebreaker Switch and the screen ahead of the helm shows its icon and OFF or ON. Use it to switch the mode. Holding anything else, or nothing, shows the usual screen. Only the driver can switch it, and the state survives a server restart.
- **Break through ice and walls afloat.** With the icebreaker ON, pushing forward against a blocked bow clears the boat's size plus three blocks to each side, above, below and ahead, on water or lava. Water and lava stay in place, and so do chests, barrels and blocks laid by this add-on.

### Before you update

- The icebreaker never cuts while the boat is beached, reversing, creeping sideways or has no driver, and a new boat starts with the mode OFF.
- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
