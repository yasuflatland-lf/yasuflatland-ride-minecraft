## Summary

The Build Wand can now cut a selected range and makes Undo much easier to find, especially on touch devices. Undo also respects other builders' work, and several cases where a failure lost an item or left blocks behind are fixed. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.46.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Cut with the Build Wand.** Every shape menu has a new Cut action, just before Copy. It clears the selected box, even when it contains hand-placed blocks, and lets you paste what you cut somewhere else. Protected blocks such as chests stay, and Undo restores the cleared cells.
- **Open the utility menu without sneaking.** When no selection is in progress, a plain long-press on the air opens the wand's utility menu, so Undo is within reach on an iPad or phone. Sneaking and long-pressing the air still works.
- **See how to undo.** The message after an edit now names the gesture that works on touch, and your first edit in a session also leaves a one-time reminder in the chat.
- **Read the height more easily.** While you aim the height of a roof, Cube, Cylinder, or Copy, the top and bottom of the particle outline read as a continuous ring.

### Fixes

- Undo used to overwrite blocks that another builder had changed after your edit. It now leaves those blocks alone and tells you how many it skipped: "Restored 10 blocks; skipped 2 blocks."
- A vehicle that failed to spawn used to take your item with it. You now get the item back, unless part of the vehicle could not be removed from the world.
- Removing a roof tent used to release the respawn point of only the last player who slept in it. It now releases everyone who set their respawn point there, while a player who has since set one at another bed keeps it.
- When the game failed to clean up after a vehicle that was no longer loaded, its light blocks, dock cells, or seats could stay behind for good. The cleanup now tries again until it succeeds.
- A failed paint sound could leave a vehicle or tent repainted without using up the dye. The dye is now used first, and the sound plays last.
- A failure in one player's Build Wand edit could stall everyone else's edits or record the same edit twice in Undo. Each edit now fails on its own and is recorded once.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
