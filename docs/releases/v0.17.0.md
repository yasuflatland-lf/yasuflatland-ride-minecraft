## Summary

This release lets the bulldozer's driver raise and lower the blade, shows the blade's position on the cab monitor, and gives the bulldozer a more detailed body. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.17.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Raise and lower the blade.** Craft the new Blade Up/Down switch on a crafting table from a lever and an iron ingot, or take it from the new Bulldozer Switches group of the Items tab. Hold it while driving the bulldozer and use it (right-click on PC, long press on mobile) to raise or lower the blade. Holding the press switches it once.
- **Cut only when you want to.** With the blade lowered, the bulldozer cuts the blocks ahead as before. With the blade raised, it cuts nothing and climbs steps up to two blocks high, like the SUV and the camper.
- **Read the cab monitor.** While the driver holds the Blade Up/Down switch, the monitor in the cab shows the switch's icon and UP or DOWN. It changes the moment you press the switch, and players outside can see it through the windshield.
- **See a more detailed bulldozer.** The lift cylinders now push the blade up and down from the front of the cab, the cab door opens from a handle at its front edge, and the hood shows its access doors, hinges, latch, intake, exhaust stack, and sensor.

### Fixes

- A bulldozer dropped from a height no longer dies when it lands.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- A newly placed bulldozer starts with its blade raised, so it does not cut until you lower the blade with the switch.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
