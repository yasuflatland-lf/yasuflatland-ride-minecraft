## Summary

This release adds the Nav, an item that gives riders an explorer map to a structure of their choice, and a radar that reports nearby places while you ride. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.66.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Get a map from the Nav.** While riding any vehicle in the Overworld, the driver or a passenger can use the Nav to open a menu of ten destinations: buried treasure, woodland mansion, ocean monument, village, jungle temple, swamp hut, trial chambers, pillager outpost, shipwreck, and ocean ruins. Pick one and an explorer map to the nearest one is added to your inventory, or dropped at your feet when it is full. The action bar shows "Creating a map to ..." while the map is made. The Nav is available from the creative inventory.
- **See how to open the Nav.** Selecting the Nav while riding, or tapping with it, shows how to open the menu on your controls: long-press on touch, right-click with a mouse.
- **Hear about places on the radar.** While you ride, the action bar reports villages, pillager outposts, ocean monuments, woodland mansions, and witch huts that are hinted at by mobs within about 128 blocks, with a compass direction and a distance, for example "NE 120 m: Village". Every rider gets their own reports, and each place is reported once per ride.
