## Summary

The Build Wand can now copy, cut, and paste whole structures, including their chests, signs, doors, and beds, and rotate what you are building. This release also adds resort furniture: a beach parasol, a beach chair, and a dining set. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.48.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both packs.

### What you can do

- **Copy, cut, and paste structures.** Copy and Cut now take the whole structure with its contents and block states, and Paste places it back exactly. Undo restores the area to how it was before the edit, including blocks a paste laid.
- **Rotate a selected box.** Rotate is now on the shape menu. It turns the selected box a quarter turn in place, with one Undo step.
- **Rotate a paste.** The paste menu has a repeating Rotate entry, so you can turn the structure before you place it. The turn is kept while you keep pasting.
- **Set up a beach.** The Beach Parasol gives shade and keeps rain off, and you can paint it. The Beach Chair folds, lets you lie down, and holds a table. The Dining Table, Dining Chair, and Sofa furnish a room, and the table can hold items on top.
- **Camper.** The Camper has plated mirrors and a simpler exterior.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing so the new Resource Pack version loads.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.120 or later
