## Summary

This release fills the parked camper with more to use and fixes how players get in and out of the vehicles: switch on ceiling lights, keep food in a two-door fridge, park on grass, sit in the sports boat's back seats, and set up a folding camp chair outside. Open `yasuflatland-ride-0.14.0.mcaddon` on a device to import both the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.

### What you can do

- **Switch on the ceiling lights.** In a parked camper, use the light switch inside to turn on the two lights over the aisle.
- **Use the fridge.** The camper's shelf is now a two-door fridge. Tap a door with an empty hand to open or shut it; the two doors open separately. Hold an item and press inside an open door to put it there. Items inside are hidden while the door is shut.
- **Park on grass.** The camper parks on grass, dirt, and other natural ground, not only on placed blocks, and you can get back in afterward.
- **Sit in the sports boat's back seats.** Look at the seat you want and get on; a second player can sit in the back instead of being put in the front.
- **Sit on a camp chair.** Craft a folding low chair from leather, sticks, and iron ingots, place it outside, and press Sit to sit on it. Hit it three times to pick it up.
- **Drive the SUV more comfortably.** The front seats sit further back, and the steering wheel leans toward the dashboard.

### Fixes

- Hitting the front bumper of a parked camper three times turns it back into an item again, whichever way the camper faces.
- The open shower-toilet door closes from a tap on either side of it, not only from outside.
- The camper's rear bumper is the same white as the front one, the stove has a single knob in the middle, and the W on its side is symmetric with straight lines.
- A vehicle removed while its chunk was unloaded is no longer remembered as still being in the world.

### Before you update

- Restart the server after installing, because the pack versions changed.
- Items saved on the camper's shelf move into the fridge.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.80 or later
- Developed and tested on a Bedrock Dedicated Server on a home LAN
