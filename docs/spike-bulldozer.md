# Bulldozer spike: findings

The spike is throwaway code on `spike/bulldozer`. It finds out what a bulldozer that levels hills should look like and how it should feel. This page collects the owner's decisions and what the spike showed on the dev BDS on 2026-10-23.

## Decisions by the owner

- A new ride, a crawler bulldozer, for clearing hills and mountains before building a house.
- It clears everything in front of the blade from the height of its own feet up to the sky. The cleared blocks vanish without drops. It does not fill holes or dips.
- In flight it clears only its own height, so it tunnels through hills and trees it flies into.
- Top speed and acceleration are half the SUV's.
- It does not steer while driving: a sideways stick stops it and turns it on the spot, the tracks running opposite ways, and forward or back drives straight.
- Mobs it runs into take damage and a push as on the SUV and the camper; the machine takes none.
- One seat, the driver's.
- Up: a tap hops one block; a second tap within 8 ticks takes off, as on the SUV. In flight, holding Up climbs, pulling the stick back sinks, and touching down ends flight; a flying machine that loses its driver lands. The tracks do not change in flight. While flying, blue glow comes out of the round cover between the rear tracks.
- Under water it dives: a propeller comes out of the same cover and turns, gravity is off, holding Up rises and pulling the stick back sinks.
- In flight and under water the driver sees the machine from behind with the camper's pulled-back chase camera; on the ground they keep their own view.
- The left door opens when the driver gets in or out.
- No rear-view mirror in the cab. A true reflection cannot be drawn with stable APIs; a scrolling landscape was considered and dropped.
- Headlights light the road like the SUV and the sports boat: three lamps on the roof front, two on the grille.
- Seen from above the cab is a trapezoid, its front corners cut back at about 45 degrees to the doors' rear posts (7.9 px), as in the owner's top-view drawing. Each door is that angled pane, hinged at the center post. The center window between the two posts is 5.7 px wide. No mesh on the rear window.
- The air cleaner, the exhaust stack, and the intake precleaner stand behind the center posts as seen from the seat.
- The switch and knob covers sit on the pillars between the doors and the rear side windows; the front corner pillars are plain black frames.
- Look: a D5-class dozer from the owner's photos, with no maker name or logo. Sizes follow the dry-ground spec with a creeper (1.7 blocks) standing for 1.8 m, except the blade, which is 1.5 times the spec width. The owner approved the outside and the cab.

## Findings

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Model | About 3,200 cubes, generated by `tools/gen_bulldozer.py`; previews by `tools/spike_render_bulldozer.py` and the driver's view by `tools/spike_cockpit_view.py` |
| Driver's eye | A rider's eye is about 1.22 blocks above the seat position; a seat at y 1.4 puts the eye just under the headliner |
| Facing and jump | Without `minecraft:input_ground_controlled` the rider's view does not follow turns; without `minecraft:can_power_jump` and a zero `minecraft:horse.jump_strength` the jump button dismounts the rider |
| Stale entities | A bulldozer summoned before those components were added kept dismounting its rider on Jump through every later definition change (seat height, collision box, seat count, even a full copy of the SUV's behavior file). A freshly summoned one kept its rider at once. Entities saved before a jump-related change have to be killed and summoned again |
| Jump on touch | On the SUV a held Jump reaches the script every tick. On the stale bulldozer it arrived for one tick before the dismount, which first looked like a limit of the API; on a fresh bulldozer a held Jump in flight climbs as on the SUV |
| Clearing | `Dimension.fillBlocks` per column with a block filter that keeps air, water, lava, and bedrock clears hills as the owner wanted |
| Turning | `minecraft:body_rotation_always_follows_head` keeps the model turning with the driver's view; without it the body lagged behind the view on a pivot turn |
| Ramming | The SUV's ram rule (ADR 0006, SPEC) over the blade's width hit and pushed pigs and cows |
| Tracks | Three grouser phases a third of a link apart, switched by an entity property per side, plus wheel bones turned by another |
