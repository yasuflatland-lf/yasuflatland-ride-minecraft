## Context

The owner wants the photographed teak lounger beside the Beach Parasol. One item must serve as a reclining chair and a low table. The reference photos remain outside the repository. The design document in the main checkout is `docs/designs/2026-11-09-beach-chair-design.md`.

Issue #1444 supplied the assets. Scripted placement, recovery, the chair menu, and form switching are now implemented. Player poses (#1448) and lantern and oven hosting (#1449) remain pending. The player definition override and its compatibility with vanilla animations still require a separate implementation and verification.

## Decision

- On 2026-11-09 the owner decided to widen the bed to one block (16 px) and rescale it to the photo proportions, approximately 2.86:1 length to width: 16 by 46 px. Cross sections follow K=16/12=4/3, using the specified rounded dimensions. The bed top is round(7 x 4/3) = 9 px; the photos put the seat height at about 0.18 to 0.2 of the length, 8.3 to 9.2 px on a 46 px bed, so 9 px keeps the photo ratio. Colors, textures, shapes, parts, and bones stay unchanged.
- Keep both forms within a 1 by 3 block footprint, with the origin at ground level at the center of the middle cell. Model -z is the foot end and +z is the backrest end. The pressed cell is the foot cell (-z); the next two cells along the facing direction are the middle and head cells. Placement is later work; the shorter folded form remains inside that footprint.
- The chair uses eight straight square posts and the table uses four, all 1.55 by 1.55 px, with their outside faces flush with their own rails. Every chair post ends at y=7.2 and every table post at y=6.63; a 0.024 px dark seam separates it from the rail above. Near-black ungrained pads occupy y [0, 0.27], inset 0.11 px on each side. The exposed chair post wood is 6.906 px high, about 4.46 post widths; table posts expose 6.336 px. Main and rear posts have outside faces at x=+-8; stub posts at x=+-7.05. Main legs begin at z=-11.5 and 12.95, stub legs at z=-22.5, and rear legs at z=21.45. Lengthwise, the stub runs z [-23, -15], the pale band z [-15, -11.5], the main bed z [-11.5, 9.5] to the hinge, and the main rails z [-11.5, 14.5], past the hinge; the short open rear frame runs z [14.5, 23]. All apron planks are 0.55 px thick through the wood. Main aprons span y [7.2, 9] (1.8 px deep); stub and rear aprons span y [7.2, 8.76] (1.56 px deep). The inset stub rails slide 1.55 px under the main rails at the junction so an oblique view cannot expose an open side gap. The rear is an open rectangle with a single head cross rail and no middle rail. Thirteen bed and backrest slats are 0.7 px wide with 0.48 px gaps; twelve half-pitch stub slats run between them. Stub, band, and bed tops are all exactly y=9, with 0.73 px slat thickness so the gaps read as deep slots; the inset stub rail alone sits lower. The pale band uses 0.53 px main fingers and 0.21 px stub fingers. Backrest slats are 13.5 px long, so the lowered backrest ends at z=23 during the fold, and 0.08 px thick, with only the downward head crossbar. Two steel supports are 0.16 px wide and 0.11 px thick, joining the backrest at 40 percent of its length to the rear rail center at y=7.98, z=22.2. Their slope is 26.76422011152607 degrees and length 9.339824532327768 px. There is no steel crossbar. The head crossbar spans x [-7.73, 7.73], starts 1.07 px before the backrest end, is 0.87 px deep with bottom y=8.13, and retains its -20 degree tilt. The head cross rail spans z [22.2, 23]. The 144 by 32 px atlas takes its colors from the studio reference photo, divided by the preview face shading so rendered faces match the photo means (frame side about a38164, legs 9b7052, slat tops d0b79c, band e6d6be): honey-brown legs (236, 172, 126), lighter apron rail sides (255, 202, 158), yellow-tan slat and rail tops (240, 212, 178), a clean cream band (252, 240, 214) whose finger sides keep the band color, light grey steel (206, 210, 210), a mid-brown teak table top (196, 156, 116), near-black pads (34, 26, 21), dark slat walls (70, 44, 26) on the inner bed slat sides and undersides so the gaps read deep and dark, and darker end grain (190, 138, 96) on the board ends. Rows 0-15 hold grain running along v and rows 16-31 the same grain running along u; every face takes the copy whose lines follow its longer side, so rails, slats, and legs show fine lengthwise grain (one-texel lines, figure lines, darker arrises) instead of blocky patches. Cross rails stop at the side rails' inner faces so no coplanar faces z-fight at the leg joints. The outermost slats use the rail color on their sides.
- The folded table is a low coffee table, as in the folded reference photo: its top is x [-8, 8], z [-10.5, 10.5] px, y=9 (`TABLE_TOP_PX`, the same height as the chair bed), with underside y=8.76. Ordinary blocks are not placed on it; only hosted Gas Lanterns and Dutch Ovens stand on it. Its 21 px length equals the main bed from the stub junction at z=-11.5 to the backrest hinge at z=9.5, excluding the stub and the rear frame, because the real stub slides under the main frame and the backrest folds flat. Its width is 16 px. It is centered on the entity origin (`TABLE_Z_CENTER` 0), so tabletop hosting needs no center offset, and it spans the middle cell z [-8, 8] and portions of the foot and head cells z [-24, -8] and [8, 24]. The top is one continuous rectangle with twelve separate 0.48 px square holes across its midpoint, closed slat joints, four corner legs at z=-10.5 and 8.95 of the same 1.55 px square section as every chair leg, and 0.55 px thick aprons at y [6.63, 8.76] (2.13 px deep, so the short legs read as a low table). It has no stub, pale band, or extra legs.
- Retain the required `root`, `frame`, `backrest`, and `prop` bones. Parent the chair geometry under `chair_form`; put the separate rectangular table frame under `table_form` with a `table_top` child. Static animations set the visible group's scale to one and the other group's scale to zero. The fold/unfold animations rotate the backrest flush and stow the prop, then switch groups using discontinuous pre/post scale keys at 0.5 seconds. This removes every chair-only part from the table without stretching the footprint or leaving dark overlapping surfaces. The preview renderer applies the same rotations and hides zero-scaled groups including descendants.
- Use one client-synchronized boolean property, `yasu_ride:folded`, default false. Fold and unfold over 0.5 seconds (10 ticks). An initial controller state selects the current static chair or table pose without playing a transition on load.
- The generated chair top and table top are both 9 px above the origin; table half extents are 8 px across x and 10.5 px along z, centered on the entity origin. The bed outline is x [-8, 8], z [-23, 23] px. The reclining hip seat is `seatOffset = [0, 0.5625, 0.3125]` blocks. The backrest hinge is [0, 9, 9.5] px and the chair backrest angle is 37 degrees above the bed. These values are the handoff to #1445.
- Define one script-controlled player seat without interaction text, persistence, immunity to damage, gravity, no pushing, and the `inanimate` family. Three overlapping custom hit boxes have width 1.0 and height 1.125 blocks, with pivots [0, 0.5625, z] for z=-0.9375, 0, and 0.9375. Together they cover z [-1.4375, 1.4375] blocks, the full 16 by 46 px outline. The square physics collision box is 1.0 blocks wide and 0.5625 blocks high.
- Put Beach Chair immediately after Beach Parasol in Resort Gear. Craft one from planks, sticks, and an iron ingot using rows `P P`, `PPP`, `SIS`. Do not grant a starter chair or add paint.
- Subsequent behavior opens a two-choice menu on a chair: lie down in the player's own skin with straight legs and a reclined upper body, or fold into a table. Refuse folding while occupied. A table press unfolds without a menu unless anything is on top.
- The table lays no blocks: ordinary blocks cannot be placed on it, and taps and hits on the table reach the chair entity through its own hit boxes, as in chair form.
- Host Gas Lanterns and Dutch Ovens through the shared tabletop host registry, with a top surface of half extents 8 by 10.5 px at y=9 px, no clearance base, and a limit of four items, like the Camp Table. The 16 by 21 px top fits two Dutch Ovens (11.5 by 10 px each) end to end, one oven and two Gas Lanterns, or up to six lanterns (7.5 by 6.5 px each) in a two by three grid; the overlap check refuses what does not fit, and the limit of four caps the lanterns. Anything on top means a hosted lantern or oven; it prevents unfolding and recovery.
- Three non-dye hits within the shared hit window return one chair item in either form. Refuse recovery while someone lies on it or something stands on the table, without changing the counter.

## Form hit boxes and pose hold

The folded table overrides `minecraft:custom_hit_test` in the
`yasu_ride:beach_chair_table` component group alongside disabled collision and
gravity. Bedrock uses the same width along x and z, so two boxes of width 1.0
and height 0.5625 blocks, at pivots [0, 0.28125, -0.15625] and
[0, 0.28125, 0.15625], cover exactly the 16 by 21 px table, 9 px high.
The chair retains its three base boxes covering 16 by 46 px.

The `yasu_ride:beach_chair_lounger` group explicitly restores the chair's
collision, gravity, and three hit boxes on unfolding; this avoids depending on
the engine to restore a base component after an overriding group is removed.
Folding removes the lounger group and adds the table group; unfolding does the
reverse. Fresh chairs use the unchanged base components without either group.
After folding, the hold rebuilds the placed pose from the entity position and
quarter-turn yaw, clears velocity, and teleports to the middle cell's centre
at floor height. The low table lays no blocks and receives taps and hits itself.

## Consequences

The asset contract allows later work to use measured seat and tabletop dimensions without changing the model. The folded table lays no invisible blocks, so nothing tracks or restores table cells, and only hosted gear can stand on it. Static previews and pack tests cannot establish engine behavior, multiplayer pose synchronization, or compatibility of a future player animation override. Owner visual approval remains pending and is required before merge.
