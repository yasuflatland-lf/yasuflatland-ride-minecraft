# 0009. Vehicle model conventions, and accepting a square collision box

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

We want to start with a placeholder vehicle model generated by a script and later replace it with a model made in Blockbench. We want to avoid fixing scripts or animations every time the model is replaced.

In the spike, the model was built with the front at +z, so the body was shown back to front. In Bedrock, the front of an entity is the model's -z side.

Bedrock's collision box (`minecraft:collision_box`) can only be square when seen from above. The SUV body is about 3.3 blocks long and about 2 blocks wide.

## Decision

- Camper door details stay on the existing bones: `door_entry` and its child `door_glass` keep hinge `[-24, 0, -7]`, and `shower_door` keeps `[-8, 0, 54]`. Interior curtains are static cubes on `door_entry`, not new animated bones.

- The front of the entity is the model's -z side
- Standardize the bone names as follows: `root`, `body`, `wheel_fl` / `wheel_fr` / `wheel_rl` / `wheel_rr`, `door_fl` / `door_fr` / `door_rl` / `door_rr`, `seat_0`–`seat_3`
- The camper extends this contract with a static `steering` child of `body` containing the 115-cube, 0.8-scale wheel, spokes, half horn ring and plain hub. Its pivot is (-14.29, 38.4909, -42.8), with bone rotation [60, 0, 0] giving a 30-degree wheel plane. The dark-dash column shaft, steel sleeve and static 30-cube gear lever stay on `body`. There is no steering animation. Compound wheel, spoke and lever rotations follow 0167.
- The camper also has `drawer_top` and `drawer_bottom` children of `body`, each with ten cubes and a closed-pose pivot. Their open-top boxes are 10 x 12.9 x 4.5 px with 0.2 px walls; two 5.2 px routed cream fronts have a 0.2 px seam. The carcass is hollow behind them. Their independent position animations follow the matching float entity properties along model +x by at most 6.6 px (0114), leaving 9.62 px of the 16.22 px closed aisle for a 9.6 px wide player. The boxes and closed-pose pivots remain unchanged. A second geometry in `camper.geo.json` repeats the animation skeleton with `drawer_top_item_<gear>` / `drawer_bottom_item_<gear>` children under the corresponding drawer bones. These stable names select one allowlisted item per drawer through client-synced integer properties (0121). Its 2,002 added cubes reuse gear geometry and UVs with the separate 512 x 512 camping gear atlas and render controller. Item bones follow their parent slides throughout opening and closing; the combined block renders only counter decor
- Put each wheel bone's pivot at the center of its axle and each door bone's pivot at its hinge (front edge). Mark the exhaust position with the locator `exhaust`
- Make the collision box a 1.8-wide, 1.6-high square, and accept that the front and back of the body stick out and appear to sink into walls
- Give the grille eight slots, with no logo or vehicle name. A seven-slot grille is a registered trademark of Jeep, and this repository is public

## Consequences

- Any model that follows these conventions can be used by swapping only the `.geo.json` and the texture
- Fixing the front and back clipping needs a separate mechanism, such as adding collision with helper entities. The driving core does not address it
