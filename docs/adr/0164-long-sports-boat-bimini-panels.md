# 0164. Long sports boat bimini panels

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-26
- Extends: [0051](0051-sports-boat.md) (bimini construction)

## Context

The bimini's transverse 2 px slices show stair steps from the side and above. The owner wants a gently domed soft top, a rolled front lip, and one darker hatch. The roof series runs in order: 1/3 #647 (top panels), 2/3 #648 (main side skirts), then 3/3 #649 (rear corner skirts and removal of the slice loop). Each later branch starts on the previous branch. Only #639's rotation-aware `pair()` and generator `--check` are prerequisites. The hull series (0161-0163) is already on this branch's main base.

Before this step the Bimini block contains 75 cubes, excluding the tower and speakers: ten slices with one top, two ribs and four rolled-edge cubes each, plus five hatch boxes. The complete starting model has 1166 cubes.

## Decision

- Keep `B_REAR = -10`, `B_FRONT = 9`, `B_HALF = 19`, `B_TOP = 36`, and `B_THICK = 2` in the generator's build frame, where +z is the bow. Define `dome(z) = B_TOP - 1.5 t + 0.6 (1 - (2t - 1)^2)`, with `t = (z - B_REAR) / (B_FRONT - B_REAR)`; the old discrete drop term is removed.
- Declare ordered `ROOF_PANELS` entries `(name, z0, z1, y0, y1)`: `ROOF_REAR` spans -10..-3 with dome endpoints; `ROOF_MAIN` spans -3..6 with dome endpoints; `ROOF_LIP` spans 6..9 from `dome(6)` to `dome(9) - 2.4`. `roof_top(z)` linearly interpolates the applicable panel, clamping outside the nominal range. It replaces every `bimini_top()` call, including the speakers' underside height, without changing their shapes.
- Build each top as one canvas cube from x -16 to 16, without the former corner trim, with 2 px thickness square to the surface and nominal length `hypot(z1 - z0, y1 - y0)`. Pivot at the rear top edge `(0, y0, z0)`. Rotate about x only, using `atan2(y1 - y0, z1 - z0)` degrees in the build frame. This corrects the opposite sign in #647's explicit formula: under 0051's in-game convention a negative x turn tips +y toward +z, so a surface falling toward +z needs a negative x turn. `turn_around()` negates it for the shipped geometry. Keep even the rear panel's small rotation (about 0.048 degrees) for exact endpoints. The preview renderer's `collect_boat()` applies `game_turn()` to account for the opposite raw x/z signs (0051, 0058); device verification is still required.
- Extend each panel's local length at each internal joint by `max(0.25, B_THICK * tan(abs(angle change)) / 2 + 0.05)`, using radians for the tangent. Both neighbours receive that allowance; outer ends get none. These overlaps use the same canvas swatch. The nominal top heights remain fixed at panel endpoints.
- Replace the five hatch boxes with one canvas_panel cube, x -9..9, 0.25 px thick above the main surface. Use the main panel's pivot and x rotation, with local distances that project its bottom edge to build z -2..5. The hatch stays inside the main footprint.
- Delete all 20 underside ribs and all 40 original rolled-edge cubes, without replacement ribs. The final roof uses only the panels below and the hatch.

| Panel | Build z range | Cubes | Construction |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `ROOF_REAR` | -10..-3 | 1 | 2 px canvas top, nearly level (height change below 0.01 px) |
| `ROOF_MAIN` | -3..6 | 1 | 2 px canvas top carrying the hatch |
| `ROOF_LIP` | 6..9 | 1 | 2 px canvas top, front endpoint lowered by 2.4 px |
| Hatch | -2..5 | 1 | 0.25 px canvas_panel sheet on the main top |
| `SKIRT_MAIN` | -3..6 | 6 | Three 0.8 px canvas chords per side at 15, 30 and 45 degrees, x 16..19, total drop 2.6 px |
| `SKIRT_REAR` | -10..-3 | 2 | 0.8 px canvas sheets, x 16..18, outer edge 3.6 px lower |
| Main end closures | -3.25..-2.75 and 5.5..6 | 8 | Two overlapping transverse canvas steps per side at each end |
| Rear end closures | -10..-9.5 | 4 | Two overlapping transverse canvas steps per side |

- Refine the shipped v0.23.0 main skirts over z -3..6 from one 40.9-degree sheet per side to three chained sheets at 15, 30 and 45 degrees. Their horizontal runs are 0.300, approximately 0.427 and 2.273 px; each drop is its run times the tangent of its angle. This preserves the 3 px reach and 2.6 px total drop exactly. The large drop relative to the reach requires a longer outer chord. Start at `(16, roof_top(6) - 0.3, 1.5)` on the right; pivot each next sheet at the preceding nominal endpoint. The shoulder remains inside the top panel's thickness at both ends.
- Extend both neighbours at each roll joint by `max(0.25, 0.8 * tan(angle change) / 2 + 0.05)` along their local widths, giving 0.25 px per neighbour. Each sheet uses canvas and 0.8 px local thickness, with only a z rotation. Retain the negative right build-frame turn and mirrored left turn. The outermost endpoint remains x 19; thickness extends inward.
- Close the transverse end cross-sections at build z -3, 6 and -10 on both sides. The previous two buried 40.9-degree chord caps only overlapped the skirt skins; they did not fill the triangular area below those skins and outboard of the top panel's x 16 side face. The enlarged above/side preview reproduces the owner's marked opening at the main skirt's z 6 end beside the lip. The rear skirt's z -10 end has the same exposed folded cross-section; the shared z -3 end also needs a full closure rather than another sheet along the fold.
- Replace the two chord caps with twelve thin transverse canvas cubes, using `pair()` and no rotation. Each section uses two overlapping rectangular steps because a single box cannot follow a triangle while staying inside the silhouette. Main steps span x 15.8..16.95 up to y 34.1 and x 16.7..17.9 up to y 33.2, both starting 0.1 px below the main skirt's nominal outer top edge (about y 32.056). Place them at z -3.25..-2.75 and 5.5..6. The shared -3 steps also close the rear skirt's inner end. Rear steps span x 15.8..16.8 up to y 34.1 and x 16.6..17.5 up to y 32.9, starting 0.1 px below the rear skirt's nominal outer top edge (y 32.0), at z -10..-9.5. All plates are 0.5 px deep, overlap their neighbour and the existing skirt skins, and tuck into the top panel at x 15.8..16. Their outer upper corners remain buried in the sloping skirts. No step reaches x 19 or below the existing outer hems. The lip's outer end at z 9 is already closed by its own 2 px top-panel end face and needs no extra skirt.
- Build each rear corner skirt as one level sheet, because the rear top changes height by less than 0.01 px. Its right pivot is `(16, roof_top(-10) - 0.3, -6.5)`, its z-only angle is `atan2(3.6, 2.0)` (60.9 degrees), and its local width is `hypot(2.0, 3.6)`. The outer edge sits 1 px inboard of the main skirt and drops farther, making the rear corner a visible steeper step. For both skirts, the build-frame turn is negative on the right to lower the +x end; `pair()` negates it on the left, and `turn_around()` keeps the written z angle (0058).
- Do not build `LIP_EDGE`: the lip's own 2 px side face supplies the rolled edge. The regenerated top, rear and afloat previews and an untracked front-left roof close-up show no additional see-through lip wedge requiring a hanging `LIP_EDGE`. Confirm on device as well as in the generated preview. The Bimini totals twenty-four cubes including the twelve transverse closure pieces.
- Remove the entire roof slice loop and its corner-width locals. Keep `SLICE` for the hull and `roof_top()` for the unchanged speakers. Do not change the tower, board racks, mirror, bow rails, speaker shapes, textures, swatches, or gameplay.
- Set the Resource Pack header and module and the Behavior Pack's Resource Pack dependency to 0.1.153 (0015), the next free patch above this branch's 0.1.152 after fetching `origin/main` (still 0.1.151). Earlier roof steps used 0.1.148, 0.1.149 and 0.1.150.

## Consequences

Step 1/3 reduces the Bimini block from 75 to 44 cubes: three top panels, one hatch, and 40 rolled-edge cubes. The complete model drops from 1166 to 1135 cubes. Step 2/3 removes 16 rolled-edge cubes and adds two main skirts, taking the block from 44 to 30 cubes and the model to 1121 cubes. Step 3/3 removes the remaining 24 rolled-edge cubes and adds two rear skirts: 30 - 24 + 2 = 8 Bimini cubes, and 1121 - 24 + 2 = 1099 model cubes. That shipped v0.23.0 baseline is seven canvas panels plus one hatch. The initial smoothing refinement replaced two main skirts with six and added two joint caps: 8 - 2 + 6 + 2 = 14 Bimini cubes, and 1099 - 2 + 6 + 2 = 1105 model cubes. Closing the actual end faces replaces those two caps with twelve stepped transverse panels: 14 - 2 + 12 = 24 Bimini cubes and 1105 - 2 + 12 = 1115 model cubes. Raise the enforced cap only from 14 to 24. The final roof has twenty-three canvas cubes plus one hatch, excluding the unchanged speakers, tower and racks.

The rear and main chords deviate from the dome by about 0.081 and 0.135 px at their midpoints. Automated geometry checks pin the in-game rotation signs, thickness, nominal endpoints, joint allowances and intersecting joint volumes, hatch placement, mirrored skirt drops, outward-steepening roll angles, intersecting roll joints, mirrored transverse steps, rays through the triangular end interiors, closure overlap and buried outer corners, rib and slice removal, single-axis rotations, and the twenty-four-cube cap. Device checks B25 and B26 in [the manual checklist](../manual-test.md) remain pending on PC and mobile: compare the regenerated `docs/images/sportsboat-top.png` and `docs/images/sportsboat-rear.png`, then inspect the roof from the side, above and rear-left for the three top panels, darker hatch, rolled front lip and rounded three-sheet side rolls with a steeper rear corner step, closed end cross-sections at z -10, -3 and 6 and no projecting steps or lip hole (bow toward the viewer's left). Previews and server tests cannot establish client rendering or sorting.
