## Context

The owner wants the SUV to look like a lifted four-door off-roader, the photographed Wrangler Rubicon, and to be ridden by friends and family. The old exterior is discarded without backward compatibility. A throwaway spike (kept outside the repository) built the shape from rectangular cubes, rendered it against 19 photos and measured it in units of the road tyre's outside diameter D: wheelbase about 3.0, height 2.07, hood 1.485, windshield rake about 36 degrees, axle height 0.5, body half width 0.885, wheel outer edge 1.244. The spike had 1,888 cubes: 1,223 for the body and interior placeholders, and five wheels of 133 cubes.

The spike's cube list is a reference, not a format to ship. The generator has to stay the one path to the model (`pnpm model`), keep the bone contract of 0009 and 0192, and map the new parts onto the swatch and paint system of 0031 and 0192.

## Decision

1. **Replace the exterior.** The body, the hood air intake, the hardtop with ribs, the mirrors, the rock rails, the open wheel arches with overfenders, the bumpers and the rear spare come from the spike's construction, ported to `tools/suv_hull.py`, `suv_front.py`, `suv_cab.py`, `suv_rear.py` and assembled in `suv_body.py`. They are written in units of D on the spike's frame (front at -z) and converted to construction pixels by `suv_kit.py`. The old `suv_shell.py` is removed.
2. **New size anchor.** The height of the roof ribs (2.064 D) stays at the old model's 26.1 construction pixels, so the roof tent anchor, the roof margin above the collision box and the placement clearance keep their meaning. That makes D = 26.1 / 2.064 = 12.645 px, 1.146 blocks at `SIZE_SCALE` 1.45, which does not change. All lengths follow from D in `tools/suv_dims.py`.
3. **Body width.** The half width is 0.885 D behind the windshield base (z 0.80). The hood front edge is 0.755 D wide, and the body widens as a trapezoid between z -0.59 and 0.80. Panels inside that zone are single cubes turned about y, so the outer face follows the slanted line. The overfender centre is 0.96 D with 0.37 D thickness at the front, and 0.185 D wide against the body at the rear.
4. **Wheels.** Every wheel, the spare included, is the 133-cube construction of the earlier SUV, drawn at radius 6 and half tread 2, then scaled by D / 12 across and by 0.43 D / 4 along the axle. The wheel stays the same design with the same part counts. Nothing reaches past the hub cap, 2.5 axial units out.
5. **Spare.** The spike's spare intersected the rear window glass, the tailgate and the rear bumper. The spare sits at z 3.80 D, y 1.35 D (front face 3.585 D, bottom 0.85 D), so it clears all three. It is the road wheel turned a quarter about y, with its face to the rear. The tail lamps stay clear.
6. **Doors and windows.** The side wall has an opening behind each door. A door bone holds a thick outer skin, the seam outline, the handles, the window frame and, for the front doors, the mirror. Window openings are open frames in the base pass. Opaque plates in `windows_sealed` (windshield and side windows) and `rear_glass` (rear window) fill them in flight and under water, in the existing `rear_glass` swatch (0026, 0040).
7. **Lamps and grille.** The grille has eight slots and no logo, vehicle name or lettering (0009). The headlight lens and bezel panels keep the painted faces of 0192 at the new position and size. The front marker lens (clear section, with an amber turn-signal section that wraps the corner), the fog lamp lens and every other `light` swatch cube go to `headlights`. The tail lamp faces and the third brake light go to `tail_lights`. The fog pocket, front strip, resin block and bumper reflector pieces of 0192 are dropped.
8. **Paint.** The body, hood, doors and fenders use the `body` swatch, so all sixteen paints still repaint them. The hardtop and its ribs use `hardtop` and `hardtop_rib`. The default paint does not change. A new `vent` swatch (grey, 88 96 106) carries the mesh of the side vents. The painted steel block and its panel-line relief are removed from the atlas because they described the old shapes. Both relief maps are flat, and the texture sets keep naming them.
9. **Interior.** The interior is unchanged and fitted in a later pull request. It must still build: seats stop at x 9.2 so they stay inside the side windows and clear the rear tyres, the dashboard sits 1.3 px behind the cowl's rear edge because the windshield foot is ahead of it, the floor console and pedals follow the dashboard, and the tall shift lever starts further back because the floor is higher.
10. **Seat and head clearance.** Seat positions and heights do not change. The tests that keep a grown head under the roof and behind the leaning windshield are not relaxed. The front axle at construction z 17.2 puts the windshield far enough forward, and the bumper stands 0.055 D further back than in the spike so the winch plate stays within `SUV_SPEC.bodyFront`.
11. **Spinning and folded wheels.** Under water the rear wheels now move 8 px out instead of 6 (`SUB_OUT`), so the larger wheel clears the overfender. The suspension links and the axles stop at the tyres' inner faces, and the rear bumper end caps stand further back, so no spinning tyre touches the body. Flight folding keeps `FOLD_OUT` and `FOLD_DROP`.
12. **Side-view targets.** `tools/suv_proportions.py` measures the new landmarks (the windshield frame's rails, the rock rails, the low bumper cubes beyond each axle, the largest side window plate). The thirteen rows are re-recorded from the model: wheelbase 3.007 D, front overhang 0.974, rear overhang 0.826, ground clearance 0.679, overall height 2.064, belt 1.495, side window 0.460, hood 1.461, hood length 1.748, front bumper 0.287, rear bumper 0.340, bumper height 0.357, rake 35.78 degrees.
13. **Out of scope.** `SUV_SPEC` (collision box, probes, seats, roof tent anchors), the interior and seats, the flight axle and the bank are not changed here (issues #1278, #1279 and #1280).

## Consequences

The generated SUV has 2,071 cubes: body 1,220 (which includes the 133-cube spare and the old interior), 133 for each road wheel, 118 for the four doors, 28 for `headlights`, 29 for `windows_sealed` and 1 for `rear_glass`, with the rest in the props, glow discs, arms, lamp bones and tail lamps. The model measures 2.68 blocks ahead of its centre, 3.10 behind it, 2.376 high and 1.34 half wide for the shell, with the complete model inside the 3.4 footprint.

The old collision box, probes, seats and roof tent anchors are unchanged. The new size anchor keeps the roof tent at the old roof height, and the roof ribs stay 0.056 blocks above the collision box, within the 0.30 allowance of 0170. The collision box is still square and narrower than the new body, whose overfenders reach 1.16 D and wheels 1.244 D from the centre line. The next pull request derives `SUV_SPEC` from the new body. The interior looks placed for the old cabin until it is refitted: the seats are narrower, and the dashboard fittings sit under the new windshield.

The spare reaches about 1.05 D behind the rear axle against 0.74 D in the photos, because it must clear the tailgate, the rear glass and the bumper. The spike's other known differences remain: planar body and hood surfaces, a slightly wide roof (about 1.45 D) and mirror span (about 1.99 D), a vent mesh that is a band instead of a round pocket, and stripes at the front arch seams. The render, the head clearance and the first-person view still need the owner's check in game on PC and on an iPad with Vibrant Visuals.

Revisit this decision if the owner finds the spare position, the 8 px submarine offset or the fixed interior unacceptable in game.
