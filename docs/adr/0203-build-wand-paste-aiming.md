# 0203. Build Wand paste aiming

- Status: Proposed
- Acceptance condition: the input spike for the paste aiming event mapping is done and the owner reviews the open assumptions (#1114)
- Date: 2026-11-01
- Supersedes in part: [0202](0202-build-wand-copy-and-continuous-paste.md): Decisions 6, 8, and 9, and the Consequences bullet on Stop pasting

## Context

ADR 0202 starts continuous Paste from the utility menu, asks for Skip air before aiming, and pastes on a block tap. Its Stop pasting entry and item switching leave the clipboard available. The redesigned interaction enters paste aiming after Copy, uses the gaze for both the outline and the target, and offers paste controls on a plain press. Paste Rotate and Paste Place are separate, always-available items alongside the wand. Touch input and the event mapping need an input spike before the interaction can be verified.

## Decision

Paste is a wand state (`pasteAim`) entered automatically, without a menu step. `AimReader` finds the first solid block within `BUILD_SPEC.reach` (64) and its face. Liquids and passable blocks do not stop the ray. The paste outline has blue horizontal edges (`minecraft:blue_flame_particle`) and orange vertical edges (`minecraft:basic_flame_particle`), redrawn every `BUILD_SPEC.outlineInterval` (10) ticks; the paste uses the live aim at press time. Orange flame is emissive for dark caves and complements the blue horizontal edges in daylight; device visibility remains unverified. Ground contact is dense: the bottom ring and the lowest block of each vertical edge use `BUILD_SPEC.denseOutlinePerBlock` samples per block. The top ring and upper vertical edges use one sample per block. The combined layers stay within `BUILD_SPEC.outlineMaxPoints`: upper vertical edges yield first, then the top ring, with proportional largest-remainder allocation within each tier. A one-block-tall box draws only the bottom ring.

1. **Entry.** Copy and Cut record the player's cardinal facing at the menu choice, including utility Copy of a confirmed box. Height-step confirmation retains that facing even if the player looks elsewhere. The adapter uses horizontal view direction, then body yaw for vertical or unavailable view direction, then north when neither is available or the player is unknown or invalid. Completing Copy from the shape menu, Copy height step, or utility menu stores the clipboard and immediately enters paste aiming while the player holds the wand. A copy exceeding `BUILD_SPEC.maxCopyVolume` (32,768 cells) is refused without changing the flow or previous clipboard.
2. **Outline.** When the gaze hits a block, the outline shows the clipboard volume snapped to the highest solid ground across its footprint and follows the gaze. The initial anchor cell is one cell outside the viewed face; orientation determines which box corner it occupies (Decision 10). Each footprint column scans down from that initial minimum Y inclusively for at most `BUILD_SPEC.groundSearchDepth` (64) cells, fewer on footprints wider than 512 columns so one snap reads at most `BUILD_SPEC.groundProbeBudget` (32,768) cells, skipping air, liquids and replaceable cover (including grass, flowers, vines and snow layers). Unloaded or throwing cells make that column groundless. Ignore groundless columns; set the final minimum Y to the maximum ground Y plus one, preserving X/Z and size. The anchor equals the snapped box minimum. The outline reuses the last snap for an unchanged box and dimension for up to `BUILD_SPEC.groundCacheTicks` (20) ticks; a press always re-probes. Top-face aims on flat ground keep their height; wall-side and cave-underside aims drop to the floor below. If all columns are groundless, draw no paste outline. With no block within reach, no outline is drawn and pasting is impossible. Every outline interval, an action-bar mode line shows clipboard size, block count, effective rotation in degrees (0, 90, 180, 270), and the menu and sneak-to-paste gestures, even without a target. Paste Rotate immediately refreshes this line after resolving live gaze facing, including without a block target. A separate translated sneak prefix is visible while sneaking.
3. **Paste.** A plain accepted wand `itemUse` always opens the paste menu, even while viewing a block or when no aim is available, and never writes the world. The wand commits on sneak plus press. At the application boundary, `PressPasteAim` also accepts `intent: "place"` to use the same commit path regardless of sneaking, without opening a menu. Place outside paste aiming or without a clipboard reports `pasteInactive` and does nothing; omitted intent retains wand behavior. Paste Place uses the shared paste debounce with sneak-wand commits, so a held press counts once within the debounce window. With no block target, a commit shows "Aim at a block to paste." and keeps aiming. The outline and press share the application use case `ResolvePasteTarget`, which reads the session clipboard and gaze facing and passes the box from `aimedPasteTarget` through `snapToGround` using `GroundProbe` in the player's dimension. A block hit with no ground refuses with `noGround`, starts no paste, and keeps paste aiming; protection and undo snapshot checks in `PasteAt` use the snapped box. PC right click produces `itemUse`; on iPad a long press of about one second produces repeated `itemUse` events, while a short tap on air sends no event, for both the wand and control items. A short iPad block tap sends only the first `playerInteractWithBlock`. For Paste Rotate and Paste Place, block interaction is canceled and the first event schedules a press with `system.run`, outside the read-only before-event. The callback re-checks player validity and the currently held control item. Both block taps and `itemUse` call the same press entry point: Paste Rotate uses `rotateDebounce` with `BUILD_SPEC.rotateDebounceTicks`, and Paste Place uses `pasteDebounce` with `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks`. One physical click counts once even if both events arrive, and held repeats count once per window; every attempted press updates its window, including rejected repeats. A control block tap outside paste aiming shows "Copy something first." without world writes. The wand retains its `itemUse` routing during paste aiming. Separate per-player windows use `BUILD_SPEC.pressDebounceTicks` for ordinary presses and `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks` for sneak presses during paste aiming. A menu press does not suppress a following commit, and repeated commits within the window are suppressed across different anchors. Each attempted press updates its own window, including rejected repeats.
4. **Menu.** A plain press opens a menu in this order: Undo when history exists, New copy, End pasting, Cancel. Cancel and dismissal keep paste aiming. Rotate is absent; selected-box rotation belongs to the shape menu. Undo reverts the last paste, keeps the clipboard and paste aiming, and clears the same-anchor-and-turn repeat window.
5. **End.** End pasting, holding neither the wand nor either paste control item for `modeLeaveTicks` (6), death, and a dimension change end paste aiming and discard the clipboard and confirmed box. Another paste requires another copy.
6. **New copy.** New copy returns to normal selection (`idle`): select start and end points, then choose Copy. The old clipboard remains until a successful copy replaces it and automatically starts paste aiming. No paste outline or mode line appears during selection. A refused oversized copy, Clear selection, or shape-menu cancel keeps the old clipboard and stays in selection. Switching items, death, or a dimension change before completion discards the clipboard.
7. **Unchanged rules.** A paste overlapping a protected cell is refused as a whole (`pasteBlocked`) and aiming continues. An accepted sneak press while a paste job runs shows the busy message and does not queue. A second press on the same anchor with the same turn within `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks` (10) is ignored; a refused paste does not start that window. Each paste creates one undo record, and the job checks protection again per cell.
8. **Mode display.** `BuildFeedback.showMode` supports paste-ready dimensions and block count, and a rotate variant with degrees 0, 90, 180 or 270. The rotate variant renders the repeated-tap mode described in ADR 0245. The paste-ready mode says "Paste ready %sx%sx%s (%s blocks) | Press: menu | Sneak + press: paste | Rotate / Place items" and adds a "[Sneaking]" prefix while the player is sneaking. Skip air is removed by ADR 0243: paste replaces the whole box, including air.
9. **Removed.** Remove Paste and Stop pasting from the utility menu, the pre-paste Skip air form, and wand block-tap pasting. The normal selection utility menu keeps Copy of the confirmed box, Undo, and Clear selection.

10. **Orientation follows facing.** Copy-time facing is captured at the Copy or Cut menu choice as in Decision 1. During paste aiming, `turnBetween(clipboard.facing, currentFacing)` computes clockwise quarter turns, `(currentFacing - clipboard.facing) mod 4` in north/east/south/west order. With no manual offset, the side facing the player at copy time keeps facing the player. Odd turns swap X/Z size. Preview and press both resolve facing from the live ray, retain it in `PasteAim.facing`, and use the same hysteresis state: after the first facing, yaw must move more than `BUILD_SPEC.pasteFacingHysteresisDegrees` (10 degrees) past the current facing's 45-degree boundary before switching. An unresolved vertical gaze (horizontal length below `BUILD_SPEC.facingMinHorizontal`, `1e-3`) retains the last paste facing, or the clipboard facing before the first resolved gaze. The copy-time adapter falls back to body yaw and then north, as in Decision 1. For `up` and `down` faces, `anchorCell` is the near-left corner as seen by the player and the box grows forward and right. For side faces the back is against the wall: the box grows along the face's outward normal and to the right as seen looking into the wall, never covering the aimed block; rotation still follows the player's facing. Y grows upward, then uses the existing ground snap. Placement uses the resulting box minimum. `PasteAt` suppresses a repeat only when both minimum anchor and turn match inside `pasteRepeatTicks`; changing facing retains the last-paste record. The separate press debounce still filters held input before target resolution. Paste Rotate advances `PasteAim.manualTurn` by one clockwise quarter turn; the effective turn is `(gazeTurn + manualTurn) mod 4`. Its per-player debounce is shared with selected-box rotation and uses `BUILD_SPEC.rotateDebounceTicks` (7), so held input counts once within that window. Preview and press use `session.pasteTurn` after updating gaze facing. `RotatePasteAim` advances that offset once without world writes, or reports `pasteInactive` outside paste aiming or without a clipboard. The immutable rotation keeps facing and the last-paste record. Completing Copy or Cut starts the offset at zero; leaving paste aiming discards it. Consecutive pastes retain it. Box growth still follows gaze or the hit face; only the rotated footprint includes the offset. The `pasteat x y z face [sneaking] [facing]` test hook uses the given facing vector, defaulting to east independently of the player's view.

## Alternatives considered

- **Rotate entries in the paste menu.** These require reopening a form for frequent orientation changes. Gaze-driven orientation keeps the outline and placement aligned while leaving Undo / New copy / End pasting / Cancel unchanged.
- **Tap-count gestures on one wand.** Double or triple taps need a delay window and can conflict with other gestures; a short iPad tap on air sends no event, so it cannot reliably produce them. Separate items also allow paste without Sneak, which descends during Creative flight.
- **Plain press rotates, with the menu moved to another gesture.** This changes the established PC and touch input mapping and makes the menu harder to discover. Plain press remains the menu gesture and sneak plus press commits the currently outlined orientation.

## Open-for-veto assumptions

- Gaze on the outline means a solid block is viewed within reach, rather than a ray intersection with the drawn box. Liquids and passable blocks, including water and tall grass, count as air. Live aim can differ from the last outline by up to 10 ticks.
- Undo clears the same-anchor-and-turn repeat window.
- Clear selection or shape-menu cancel during New copy stays in selection and keeps the old clipboard.
- Death and dimension change end pasting and discard the clipboard.
- `endPasting` clears the confirmed box as well as the clipboard and returns to idle.

## Input spike event table

Device probe logs from 2026-11-17 establish the iPad event sequences below. The control-item outcomes still need owner verification on iPad and PC.

| Device and action                             | Event and result                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| iPad: short tap on air                        | No event for the wand or control items; no action                                                                                        |
| iPad: short block tap with a control item     | First `playerInteractWithBlock` only; canceled, then `system.run` presses Rotate or Place                                                |
| iPad: long press in air for about one second  | Repeated `itemUse`; control items press through their debounce; plain wand press opens the menu and sneak press pastes at a viewed block |
| PC: right click                               | `itemUse` presses the control item; plain wand press opens the menu and sneak press pastes at a viewed block                             |
| Control-item block interaction plus `itemUse` | Block interaction is canceled; both routes share the debounce, so one click presses once                                                 |
| Wand block interaction during paste aiming    | Canceled; any resulting `itemUse` handles the wand press                                                                                 |

## Consequences

- Copy leads directly to repeatable paste aiming, and a plain wand press opens the menu, sneak plus press or Paste Place commits, and Paste Rotate adjusts the orientation.
- Ending paste aiming discards the clipboard and confirmed box, so another paste requires another copy.
- The outline can lag the live target by up to 10 ticks. Manual checks must cover a fast gaze change as well as touch input and action-bar readability.
- The input event mapping and the assumptions above remain subject to the spike and owner review before acceptance.
