# 0203. Build Wand paste aiming

- Status: Proposed
- Acceptance condition: the input spike for the paste aiming event mapping is done and the owner reviews the open assumptions (#1114)
- Date: 2026-11-01
- Supersedes in part: [0202](0202-build-wand-copy-and-continuous-paste.md): Decision 8 originally specified the paste menu

## Context

The original paste interaction started from the utility menu, asked for Skip air before aiming, and pasted on a block tap. The live-aim interaction enters paste aiming after Copy, uses the gaze for both the outline and the target, and opens the paste menu on a plain press. Paste Rotate, Paste Place and Build Undo are separate, always-available items alongside the wand. Touch input and the event mapping need an input spike before the interaction can be verified.

## Decision

Paste is a wand state (`pasteAim`) entered automatically, without a menu step. `AimReader` finds the first solid block within `BUILD_SPEC.reach` (64) and its face. Liquids and passable blocks do not stop the ray. The paste outline has blue horizontal edges (`minecraft:blue_flame_particle`) and orange vertical edges (`minecraft:basic_flame_particle`), redrawn every `BUILD_SPEC.outlineInterval` (10) ticks; the paste uses the live aim at press time. Orange flame is emissive for dark caves and complements the blue horizontal edges in daylight; device visibility remains unverified. Ground contact is dense: the bottom ring and the lowest block of each vertical edge use `BUILD_SPEC.denseOutlinePerBlock` samples per block. The top ring and upper vertical edges use one sample per block. The combined layers stay within `BUILD_SPEC.outlineMaxPoints`: upper vertical edges yield first, then the top ring, with proportional largest-remainder allocation within each tier. A one-block-tall box draws only the bottom ring.

1. **Entry.** Copy and Cut record the player's cardinal facing at the menu choice, including utility Copy of a confirmed box. The adapter uses horizontal view direction, then body yaw for vertical or unavailable view direction, then north when neither is available or the player is unknown or invalid. Completing Copy from the shape menu or utility menu stores the clipboard and immediately enters paste aiming while the player holds the wand. A copy exceeding `BUILD_SPEC.maxCopyVolume` (32,768 cells) is refused without changing the flow or previous clipboard.
2. **Outline.** When the gaze hits a block, the outline shows the clipboard volume snapped to the highest solid ground across its footprint and follows the gaze. The initial anchor cell is one cell outside the viewed face; orientation determines which box corner it occupies (Decision 10). Each footprint column scans down from that initial minimum Y inclusively for at most `BUILD_SPEC.groundSearchDepth` (64) cells, fewer on footprints wider than 512 columns so one snap reads at most `BUILD_SPEC.groundProbeBudget` (32,768) cells, skipping air, liquids and replaceable cover (including grass, flowers, vines and snow layers). Unloaded or throwing cells make that column groundless. Ignore groundless columns; set the final minimum Y to the maximum ground Y plus one, preserving X/Z and size. The anchor equals the snapped box minimum. The outline reuses the last snap for an unchanged box and dimension for up to `BUILD_SPEC.groundCacheTicks` (20) ticks; a press always re-probes. Top-face aims on flat ground keep their height; wall-side and cave-underside aims drop to the floor below. If all columns are groundless, draw no paste outline. With no block within reach, no outline is drawn and pasting is impossible. Every outline interval, an action-bar mode line shows clipboard size, block count, effective rotation in degrees (0, 90, 180, 270), and the menu and sneak-to-paste gestures, even without a target. Paste Rotate immediately refreshes this line after resolving live gaze facing, including without a block target. A separate translated sneak prefix is visible while sneaking.
3. **Paste.** A plain accepted wand `itemUse` always opens the paste menu, even while viewing a block or when no aim is available, and never writes the world. The wand commits on sneak plus press while aiming at a block. At the application boundary, `PressPasteAim` also accepts `intent: "place"` to use the same commit path regardless of sneaking, without opening a menu. With a clipboard in `idle` or `selected`, Place resumes aiming with a fresh manual turn, shows the ghost and prompts the player to choose a position. The next Place or sneak-wand press commits. Place during an unfinished selection step or without a clipboard reports `pasteInactive`. Omitted intent retains wand behavior. Paste Place uses the shared paste debounce with sneak-wand commits, so a held press counts once within the debounce window. With no block target, a wand commit shows "Aim at a block to paste." and keeps aiming. The outline and press share the application use case `ResolvePasteTarget`, which reads the session clipboard and gaze facing and passes the box from `aimedPasteTarget` through `snapToGround` using `GroundProbe` in the player's dimension. A block hit with no ground refuses with `noGround`, starts no paste, and keeps paste aiming; protection and undo snapshot checks in `PasteAt` use the snapped box. PC right click produces `itemUse`; on iPad a long press of about one second produces repeated `itemUse` events, while a short tap on air sends no event, for both the wand and control items. A short iPad block tap sends only the first `playerInteractWithBlock`. For Paste Rotate, Paste Place and Build Undo, block interaction is canceled and the first event schedules a press with `system.run`, outside the read-only before-event. The callback re-checks player validity and the currently held control item. Both block taps and `itemUse` call the same press entry point: Paste Rotate uses `rotateDebounce` with `BUILD_SPEC.rotateDebounceTicks`, Paste Place uses `pasteDebounce` with `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks`, and Build Undo uses a separate undo debounce with the same interval. One physical click counts once even if both events arrive, and held repeats count once per window; every attempted press updates its window, including rejected repeats. Outside paste aiming, Paste Rotate turns a fixed selection in place. With neither a selection nor a clipboard, either paste control shows "Copy something first." The wand retains its `itemUse` routing during paste aiming. Separate per-player windows use `BUILD_SPEC.pressDebounceTicks` for ordinary presses and `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks` for sneak presses during paste aiming. A menu press does not suppress a following commit, and repeated commits within the window are suppressed across different anchors. Each attempted press updates its own window, including rejected repeats.
4. **Menu.** A plain press opens a menu in this order: New copy, End pasting, Cancel, regardless of history. Undo is available through the Build Undo item, not this menu. Cancel and dismissal keep paste aiming. Undo keeps the clipboard and resumes paste aiming when undoing a paste whose destination is still selected in the same dimension, as specified in ADR 0202. One press of Build Undo invokes the same use case directly in any flow and never opens a menu. Its first block interaction is canceled and schedules the press with `system.run`, re-checking the player and held control item; `itemUse` shares the same per-player undo debounce using `BUILD_SPEC.pasteRepeatTicks`. Busy, empty-history, success, partial, blocked and failed feedback use the existing action-bar messages. With no session, it reports `nothingToUndo` without creating one.
5. **End.** End pasting, holding none of the wand, either paste control item, or undo for `modeLeaveTicks` (6), death, and a dimension change end paste aiming and discard the clipboard and confirmed box. Successful placement also ends paste aiming, selecting the destination and retaining the clipboard (ADR 0202).
6. **New copy.** New copy returns to normal selection (`idle`): select the start and end points, confirm live height for a rectangle or box, then open the selection menu and choose Copy. The old clipboard remains until a successful copy replaces it and automatically starts paste aiming. No paste outline or mode line appears during selection. A refused oversized copy, Clear selection, or shape-menu cancel keeps the old clipboard and stays in selection. Switching items, death, or a dimension change before completion discards the clipboard.
7. **Refusal and history.** Paste preflights protection across the entire target (`pasteBlocked`), then checks for a running edit (`busy`). Either refusal keeps the player aiming. A successful placement records one undo snapshot and selects the pasted box (ADR 0202).
8. **Mode display.** `BuildFeedback.showMode` shows paste-ready dimensions, block count and effective rotation in degrees (0, 90, 180 or 270). The paste-ready mode says "Paste ready %sx%sx%s (%s blocks) | Rotation %s° | Press: menu | Sneak + press: paste | Rotate / Place items" and adds a "[Sneaking]" prefix while the player is sneaking. Skip air is removed by ADR 0243: paste replaces the whole box, including air.
9. **Removed.** Remove Paste and Stop pasting from the utility menu, the pre-paste Skip air form, and wand block-tap pasting. The normal selection utility menu keeps Copy of the confirmed box, Undo, and Clear selection.

10. **Orientation follows facing.** Copy-time facing is captured at the Copy or Cut menu choice as in Decision 1. During paste aiming, `turnBetween(clipboard.facing, currentFacing)` computes clockwise quarter turns, `(currentFacing - clipboard.facing) mod 4` in north/east/south/west order. With no manual offset, the side facing the player at copy time keeps facing the player. Odd turns swap X/Z size. `TrackGaze` resolves facing from the live ray each tick and retains it in `PasteAim.facing`. Preview and press use the same hysteresis rule: after the first facing, yaw must move more than `BUILD_SPEC.pasteFacingHysteresisDegrees` (10 degrees) past the current facing's 45-degree boundary before switching. An unresolved vertical gaze (horizontal length below `BUILD_SPEC.facingMinHorizontal`, `1e-3`) retains the last paste facing, or the clipboard facing before the first resolved gaze. The copy-time adapter falls back to body yaw and then north, as in Decision 1. [0262](../adr/0262-tap-to-place-the-build-wand-paste-box-and-stair-goal.md) Decision 3 places the rotated point-1 corner on the bottom layer at the cell adjacent to the aimed face, then snaps the footprint to ground. Placement uses the resulting box minimum. The press debounce filters held input before target resolution. Paste Rotate advances `PasteAim.manualTurn` by one clockwise quarter turn; the effective turn is `(gazeTurn + manualTurn) mod 4`. Its per-player debounce is shared with selected-box rotation and uses `BUILD_SPEC.rotateDebounceTicks` (7), so held input counts once within that window. Preview redraws use the tracked facing, and a press resolves its own fresh aim using the tracked facing for hysteresis. Immediate redraws can project the live aim without writing back to the session. When Paste Place resumes aiming, the immediate redraw uses the copied facing and leaves the position prompt visible until the next scheduled HUD report. `RotatePasteAim` advances that offset once without world writes, or reports `pasteInactive` outside paste aiming or without a clipboard. The rotation keeps the current facing. Entering fresh paste aiming starts the offset at zero. Leaving paste aiming discards it. The `pasteat x y z face [sneaking] [facing]` test hook uses the given facing vector, defaulting to east independently of the player's view.

## Alternatives considered

- **Rotate entries in the paste menu.** These require reopening a form for frequent orientation changes. Gaze-driven orientation keeps the outline and placement aligned while keeping New copy / End pasting / Cancel in the menu.
- **Tap-count gestures on one wand.** Double or triple taps need a delay window and can conflict with other gestures; a short iPad tap on air sends no event, so it cannot reliably produce them. Separate items also allow paste without Sneak, which descends during Creative flight.
- **Tap Paste Place again to undo.** A placement control and an undo control keep those actions independent.
- **Confirm after every paste.** One press commits the live target and selects the pasted box.
- **Sneak plus tap for quick undo.** Rejected because Sneak means descend in Creative flight.
- **Plain press rotates, with the menu moved to another gesture.** This changes the established PC and touch input mapping and makes the menu harder to discover. Plain press remains the menu gesture and sneak plus press commits the currently outlined orientation.

## Open-for-veto assumptions

- Gaze on the outline means a solid block is viewed within reach, rather than a ray intersection with the drawn box. Liquids and passable blocks, including water and tall grass, count as air. Live aim can differ from the last outline by up to 10 ticks.
- Clear selection or shape-menu cancel during New copy stays in selection and keeps the old clipboard.
- Death and dimension change end pasting and discard the clipboard.
- `endPasting` clears the confirmed box as well as the clipboard and returns to idle.

## Input spike event table

Device probe logs from 2026-11-17 establish the iPad event sequences below. The control-item outcomes still need owner verification on iPad and PC.

| Device and action                             | Event and result                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| iPad: short tap on air                        | No event for the wand or control items; no action                                                                                        |
| iPad: short block tap with a control item     | First `playerInteractWithBlock` only; canceled, then `system.run` presses Rotate, Place or Undo                                          |
| iPad: long press in air for about one second  | Repeated `itemUse`; control items press through their debounce; plain wand press opens the menu and sneak press pastes at a viewed block |
| PC: right click                               | `itemUse` presses the control item; plain wand press opens the menu and sneak press pastes at a viewed block                             |
| Control-item block interaction plus `itemUse` | Block interaction is canceled; both routes share the debounce, so one click presses once                                                 |
| Wand block interaction during paste aiming    | Canceled; any resulting `itemUse` handles the wand press                                                                                 |

## Consequences

- Copy starts paste aiming. A plain wand press opens the menu, sneak plus press or Paste Place commits and selects the destination, and Paste Rotate adjusts the orientation before or after placement.
- Choosing End pasting discards the clipboard and confirmed box. Successful placement retains the clipboard.
- The outline can lag the live target by up to 10 ticks. Manual checks must cover a fast gaze change as well as touch input and action-bar readability.
- The input event mapping and the assumptions above remain subject to the spike and owner review before acceptance.
