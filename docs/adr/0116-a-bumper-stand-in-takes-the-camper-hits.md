# 0116. A bumper stand-in takes the camper's hits

- Status: Proposed (to be Accepted once the phone check under Consequences passes)
- Date: 2026-10-22
- Supersedes in part: 0084 (the three boxes fixed at z 3.7 on the camper), 0068 (the bumper hitbox on the camper entity)
- Extends: 0086 (the bumper hints)
- Extended by: [0119](0119-park-the-camper-over-natural-ground.md) (Proposed; the stand-in rises out of the ground)

## Context

0084 put three 1 x 1 x 1 `minecraft:custom_hit_test` boxes at x -1, 0, and 1, y 0.5, z 3.7 in the camper's base components (#446). Two findings from the dev BDS, tested from an iPad on 2026-10-22:

1. The boxes do not turn with the entity's yaw. With a camper parked facing north (yRot -180), taps on its front bumper landed on the front `camper_floor` room block, and three hits on its rear bumper broke it into its item. A hitbox pivot stays in world axes, so the boxes cover the front bumper only at yaw 0, the rear bumper at yaw 180, and the air beside the body at yaw 90 and -90.
2. Swapping the boxes by heading does not work. A first fix kept four component groups, `yasu_ride:bumper_0`, `bumper_90`, `bumper_180`, and `bumper_270`, each holding the boxes turned to its quarter, and had the drive send the group's event when the heading changed. The server applied `yasu_ride:bumper_180`, also when forced with `/event entity`, but the client kept the base yaw-0 boxes: taps on the front bumper still did nothing and the rear bumper still broke the camper. This confirms 0068's limit that a hit test changed after spawn never reaches the client, for moved boxes as well as shrunk ones. That fix is removed.

The other limits stay as 0068 and the [camper spike](../spike-camper.md) found them: hitboxes take taps only while the entity's base components hold a `minecraft:collision_box`; a separate stand-in entity whose hit box was set from spawn let every tap inside the parked camper reach the blocks; and an entity with only `minecraft:interact` never showed an interact button on a phone, while the deck seats' `minecraft:rideable` does (0063).

## Decision

- A separate invisible stand-in entity carries the bumper boxes. Since its boxes never turn either, it comes as two entity types with fixed boxes in their base components: `yasu_ride:camper_bumper_x`, with the three boxes of 0084 (1 x 1 x 1, centers at y 0.5) at x -1, 0, and 1, and `yasu_ride:camper_bumper_z`, with them at z -1, 0, and 1. Each row is centered on the entity's feet. Neither has component groups or events
- The camper keeps one stand-in, standing `CAMPER_SPEC.bodyFront` (3.7) blocks ahead of its feet along its heading, at its feet's height, of the type whose row lies across the heading: `_x` facing north or south, `_z` facing east or west. Parked, the yaw is snapped (0068), so the boxes cover the bumper exactly as 0084 intends: the whole 3-block width, 1 block high at the bottom of the body, reaching half a block into it, in the row of cells in front of the floor. While driving the row follows the nearest quarter, but keeps its axis until the yaw is `CAMPER_SPEC.bumperTurnMargin` (5) degrees past the 45-degree line, so steering along a diagonal does not respawn it every tick
- The stand-in has a 0.2 x 0.2 collision box in its base components, which its hitboxes need (0068), and blocks a placed block only in the one cell under its middle box. It has no gravity and no physics collision, cannot be pushed or knocked back, takes no damage of any kind, drops nothing, and has no despawn or leash component. Its family is `yasu_ride_stand_in` and `inanimate`, so the ram query skips it and the fleet never counts it. Its client entity draws a model with no cubes
- It has a one-seat `minecraft:rideable` for players with the camper's interact text, only so that a phone shows the interact button. Every interaction with it is cancelled, so nobody rides it; the script also ejects anyone found on it
- The camper's own `minecraft:custom_hit_test` is a single box `CAMPER_SPEC.hiddenHitboxSize` (0.1) wide and high on its feet, centered under the body. Parked, the feet are at the bottom of the center floor cell (0068), so the box lies inside that full `camper_floor` block, under its top face: no tap from inside or outside reaches it, and taps inside the body reach the room blocks as before. The box stays from spawn, since a hit test cannot change later, and the 3 x 4 collision box stays in the base components for the body's collision
- The core declares the stand-in kind-neutrally: a profile's optional `standIn` rule (`name`, `ahead`, `turnMargin`) and `standInAxis` and `standInPose` in `domain/vehicle/standIn.ts`. Each tick the vehicle returns the pose, from its hold pose while held and its location before the move while driving, and `DriveWorld.keepStandIn` keeps it after the deck seats. The SUV and the sports boat have no rule
- Lifecycle, in `infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/standIns.ts`: the stand-in is spawned with `dimension.spawnEntity` next to the camper, as the deck seats are (0063), and named on both sides, `yasu_ride:stand_in_of` on the stand-in and `yasu_ride:stand_in` on the camper, so a camper rebuilt after `/reload` or a restart finds its own instead of spawning another. It is teleported only when more than 0.05 blocks off its spot, and replaced when the axis or the dimension changes. A spot in an unloaded chunk is skipped until the next tick. The first time a camper is kept after a reload, any other stand-in naming it within reach is removed. Breaking the camper into its item, retiring it (0066), and unloading it release the stand-in with the deck seats. Every tick any loaded stand-in whose camper is not loaded is removed, and a stand-in that loads is removed unless its camper is loaded near it and names it
- Forwarding: a player's hit on a stand-in goes to its camper as a hit on the camper would, so a dye paints it (0036) and other hits break it (0008). A player's interaction with it boards them into the camper with `addRider`, unless they are sneaking (the camper's `crouching_skip_interact`) or already ride the camper; a rider holding the headlight switch switches the camper's headlights (0020, 0024). Mobs and projectiles get nothing: the hit handler takes only players, and the damage sensor stops all damage
- The bumper hints of 0086 already measure the bumper in the camper's own frame from `CAMPER_SPEC`, not from any entity, and stay as they are

## Consequences

- A parked camper takes taps on its front bumper whichever way it faces, and its rear bumper and the air beside it take none, if the client honors a stand-in's spawn-time boxes the way the spike found
- Check on a phone before this ADR is Accepted: park the camper facing each of the four directions, and for each, tap the left, middle, and right of the front bumper (three taps break it into its item), paint it with a dye there, long-press it to get in, and tap the rear bumper and the body's sides (nothing happens). Inside the parked camper, tap the floor, the door, the bed, and the furniture as before. While driving, hit the bumper with a dye
- Checked so far (iPad, dev BDS, 2026-10-22): with the camper parked facing north, three hits on the front bumper broke it into its item, and hits on the rear did nothing. The other headings, painting, getting in, and the room taps are still to check
- While driving, the stand-in trails the body by one tick's move, up to about 1.3 blocks at full speed (0067), and at a diagonal its row covers the bumper only in part, as 0084's boxes did
- Taps on the stand-in's middle box land half a block into the front row of the room, as with 0084; one block cannot be placed in the cell under the middle box
- Each tick costs one query of the stand-in family per dimension and, while driving, a teleport
- The sports boat has the same flaw: its three 2.2 x 1.4 hitboxes sit at z -1.6, 0, and 1.6 in world axes (0051), so at yaw 90 or -90 they cover a strip across the boat's middle instead of its length. It is left for a follow-up, which can declare a `standIn` rule on the boat's profile
