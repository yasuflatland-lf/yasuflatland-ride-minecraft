# 0067. Camper

- Status: Accepted (the hitboxes are superseded by [0068](0068-park-the-camper-with-room-blocks.md), and the seat layout by [0069](0069-camper-right-hand-drive.md); extended by [0091](0091-alpha-blend-only-the-camper-glass.md), [0093](0093-decorate-the-parked-camper.md), and [0145](0145-move-camper-side-lamp-and-door-handle.md) (Proposed))
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

The owner wants a cab-over camper, modelled on their photos of a 1972 motorhome, that they can drive like the SUV and later live in. The [camper spike](../spike-camper.md) showed that a parked camper can be walked through, slept in, and used for storage, and settled its look with the owner. The SUV's forms, flight, submarine motion, landing gear, and bank now live in shared code under `src/domain/vehicle/motion/` with per-kind values, so a second transforming kind can use them (0050, 0055). The engine's collision box is a square, but the camper's body is 3 blocks wide and 7 long.

## Decision

- Add the kind `camper`, entity `yasu_ride:camper`, item `yasu_ride:camper_item`. It drives on land, takes off and lands, hovers, banks, and dives through the shared transforming drivetrain, with its own `CAMPER_SPEC` (never `SUV_SPEC`)
- Ground motion: top speed 0.9 blocks/tick (the SUV's speed, as the owner asked), acceleration 0.012 blocks/tick per tick (about 3.75 s to full speed), reverse 0.15 blocks/tick, turn rate 4 degrees/tick. Flight and submarine values repeat the SUV's numbers, except the body height of 4.0 blocks, a float draft of 3.6 blocks, and a gear probe offset of 1.2 blocks
- Body: 3 wide, 7 long, 4 high. Use a 3-wide, 4-high collision box and three `minecraft:custom_hit_test` boxes along the body. The drive stops the body, and cancels any step jump, when a solid cell lies in any cell its front (3.7 blocks from the center) or rear (3.9 blocks) end passes through during this tick's move, at the end toward travel. It looks ahead by the velocity the engine applies this tick, not the post-friction speed, and samples each lane at most 1 block apart along the travel, from just beyond the current end to the end after the move, so a one-block-thick wall cannot fall between two samples at top speed (about 1.3 blocks per tick on default ground). It checks lanes evenly spaced across the body's width no more than 1 block apart (4 for the camper), so no cell across the width is skipped. In car form it checks from two blocks above the feet, because lower cells are steps for the step probe (0004); in hover and submarine, which have no step probe, it checks from the feet up
- Four seats: seat 0 the driver at the front left (left-hand drive), seat 1 the front passenger, seats 2 and 3 a facing dinette on the left behind the driver, seat 2 facing the rear
- Ram reach 3.7 blocks ahead and 1.5 to each side; headlights with the SUV's reach (0006, 0020)
- One camper per player (0066). Placement on a block's top face needs 9 x 9 x 4 clear cells, because the clearance is axis-aligned, the camper can face any way, and its 7.6-block body with the bumper and ladder reaches 3.82 blocks from the center at a corner when it faces 45 degrees
- Crafted shapeless from an SUV item, a bed, a chest, and an oak door. The starter item stays the SUV (0023)
- The 16 paints recolor the window band, the stripe with its W, and the lower skirt; the white body stays white. Khaki is the default (0031)
- The model is generated by `tools/gen_camper.py` from the spike's approved shape, with bones `root`, `body`, `headlights`, `wheel_fl`, `wheel_fr`, `wheel_rl`, `wheel_rr`, `seat_0`-`seat_3`, `door_entry`, and `steering` (0009)
- Keep the stripe's large W from the photos, at the owner's request, as an exception to the rule against makers' marks in 0009. No maker or model name appears anywhere

## Consequences

- The W is the motorhome maker's mark. Revisit it before the add-on is distributed outside the owner's home server
- The square collision box covers only the middle 3 blocks of the body; the end check in the drive keeps the front and rear out of walls, but a low step can overlap the bumper until the box climbs it
- The end check adds 8 block queries per tick for a moving camper in car form (2 layers x 4 lanes) while the end stays within its next block, and at most 16 when the move spans two blocks; in hover or submarine 16 and 32 (4 layers x 4 lanes). It stops at the first solid hit, nearest samples first. These come on top of the SUV-sized step and gear probes, and there are none while it stands still. A per-tick cache by cell was left out because `solidAt` casts its ray from the exact point inside the cell
- The model has about 3,400 cubes. Revisit it if the camper stutters on slower devices
- Parking, walking inside, the door, the bed, the chest, recording placed blocks, and the ladder come in later ADRs
