# 0063. Walk on a stopped sports boat

- Status: Accepted (extended by [0064](0064-dock-step.md), [0093](0093-decorate-the-parked-camper.md), [0095](0095-sit-on-the-camper-toilet-while-parked.md), [0096](0096-keep-the-dock-blocks-whole.md), and [0101](0101-give-invisible-blocks-a-hidden-cube.md))
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Extends: 0051

## Context

The [boat deck spike](../spike-boat-deck.md) ran on the dev BDS 1.26.52 on 2026-10-12. A custom block whose collision box is h/16 high (one permutation per h from 1 to 16), laid under the hull, held armor stands at boat y + 9.3/16 in the cockpit and y + 11.3/16 in the bow, which matches the model's 9.0 and 11.0 px decks plus 0.3 px of teak. At rest the boat floats at a draft of about 0.45 and bobs by about 0.07 blocks (0051 measured 0.17 while moving), so the deck sits 0.13 to 0.26 blocks above the water and every floor cell is air; no water block is replaced.

The floor moves the boat unless the boat is held. Laid under a floating boat, it made the boat climb onto it (y -60.39 to -59.69). A component group that turns off block collision and gravity, plus a teleport to a fixed pose every tick, kept the boat in place for 150 ticks. Removing that group removed `minecraft:physics` altogether, and the boat sat on the surface with zero velocity; a second group that sets `minecraft:physics: {}` made it float again (y -60.38 to -60.52). With the cell-center rule, a hull turned 30 degrees covered 10 cells without a margin and 13 to 16 cells with a 0.3-block margin; the jagged edge was acceptable for walking.

Getting into a seat from the deck failed through the boat itself. On touch, a short tap on the boat is an attack, so tapping a seat breaks the boat after 3 hits (0008). A player standing on the deck is inside the boat's hit boxes, and a long press never reached the server (`playerInteractWithEntity` did not fire). `rideable.addRider` seats a player, but always in seat 0 first and then in index order (0019), so a seat cannot be chosen through the boat's own `minecraft:rideable`. State kept only in script memory was lost on `/reload` and on restart, which left a docked boat with no hold or a floor with no boat. A player can walk or jump off the edge into the water, including by accident.

## Decision

- **Dock.** A sports boat docks when it is afloat (`isAfloat` in `kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`), has no driver input this tick, and its speed has stayed below `DOCK.maxSpeed = 0.005` blocks/tick for `DOCK.ticks = 20` consecutive ticks (1 s). Docking triggers the entity event `yasu_ride:dock`, sets the entity property `yasu_ride:docked` to true, and fixes a hold pose.
- **Hold pose.** x and z are the boat's location when it docks, yaw is its heading, and y is the top of the topmost water cell in the boat's column minus `DOCK.draft = 0.45` (the measured mean draft at rest). While docked, the boat gets no impulse, and the script teleports it to the hold pose and clears its velocity every tick.
- **Components while docked.** The event `yasu_ride:dock` adds the component group `yasu_ride:docked` (`minecraft:physics` with `has_collision: false` and `has_gravity: false`) and removes `yasu_ride:undocked`. The event `yasu_ride:undock` removes `yasu_ride:docked` and adds `yasu_ride:undocked` (`minecraft:physics: {}`), because removing a group that overrides a base component removes the component.
- **Floor.** While docked, the boat keeps the block `yasu_ride:deck_floor` in every cell its deck covers, and only in cells that were air (or a light block) when laid. The block is invisible, cannot be selected or mined, and has a block state `yasu_ride:height` from 1 to 16 that sets its collision box height in pixels. The covered cells come from the hull's deck parts in the boat's own coordinates (x = left, z = forward, blocks): cockpit |x| <= 0.96, -2.75 <= z <= 0.875, deck at 9.3/16 above the feet; walk-through |x| <= 0.22, 0.875 <= z <= 1.125, at 11.3/16; bow |x| <= 0.69, 1.125 <= z <= 2.5, at 11.3/16. A cell is covered when its center lies within a part grown by `DECK.margin = 0.3` blocks on every side; its y is `floor(holdY + partHeight)` and its height is `clamp(round((holdY + partHeight - y) * 16), 1, 16)`; where parts overlap, the higher part wins. The cells the boat laid are saved on the boat in the dynamic property `yasu_ride:deck_cells`, so a reload or restart can still clear them, and they are cleared when the boat undocks or is gone (as headlight cells are, 0020).
- **Undock.** A docked boat undocks when its driver (the rider in seat 0 of the boat, or the test input when there is no driver) pushes the stick by more than `DOCK.stick = 0.1` (the larger of |moveX| and |moveY|). Undocking triggers `yasu_ride:undock`, sets `yasu_ride:docked` to false, clears the floor and the deck seats, and drives normally from that tick.
- **After a reload.** `docked` is restored from the entity property; a restored docked boat takes its current location and heading as the hold pose.
- **Deck seats.** While docked, the boat keeps one invisible one-seat entity `yasu_ride:deck_seat` at each of its seats that has no rider in the boat itself (same x and z as the boat's seats in `SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE.seats`, at the hold y, rider 0.52 above it like the boat's seats). Deck seat i belongs to boat seat i, so the deck seats are a list of four entries in seat order with an empty entry where the boat seat is taken; the aggregate, which already tracks the occupied boat seats (0019), drops those entries. When a rider gets into boat seat i (for example from the water), deck seat i is removed and its rider, if any, is left standing on the deck. As a result, deck-seat riders plus boat riders never exceed the boat's four seats, and deck seat 0 exists only while the boat has no driver. A player picks a seat by using (long-pressing on touch, right-clicking on PC) that deck seat. The deck seat ids are saved on the boat in the dynamic property `yasu_ride:deck_seats`, and each deck seat stores its boat's id in its own dynamic property `yasu_ride:deck_seat_of`.
- **Setting off from the deck.** When the rider of deck seat 0 (the driver's seat) pushes the stick by more than `DOCK.stick`, the boat moves every deck-seat rider into itself in deck seat order 0, 1, 2, 3, and undocks on the next tick when its new driver's input arrives. Deck seat 0 exists only while boat seat 0 is empty, so its rider lands in seat 0 and becomes the boat's driver. Because Bedrock seats riders by their order in the rider list (0019), a rider can land in a lower seat than the deck seat they used when a lower deck seat was empty; accepted. When the boat undocks for any other reason (its own driver in boat seat 0 drove off), it also moves the deck-seat riders in first, after its existing riders; only the boat's driver drives, and a deck-seat rider's stick does nothing while the boat has one. A rider that `rideable.addRider` still refuses stays on the deck seat until the deck seats are cleared and is then left on the deck, like a player standing on it.
- **Hits from the deck.** A hit on a sports boat from a player standing on a `yasu_ride:deck_floor` block does not count toward breaking it (0008). Hits from anywhere else count as before, so a docked boat can still be picked up from the water or the shore. Painting by hitting with a dye (0036) still works from the deck.
- **Open question, decided after a playtest.** Whether to add a rim that a player has to jump over to leave the deck. Until then, a player can walk off the edge.

## Consequences

- A parked sports boat always has a floor, and a deck seat at each boat seat without a rider, while it floats still, so a parked boat now writes up to 17 blocks and spawns up to four extra entities. The spike measured 13 to 16 cells at a 30 degree heading; sweeping the cell-center rule over every heading and sub-cell offset gives 10 to 17, with 17 at a heading of 0. They are cleared when the boat leaves, breaks, or is removed by the vehicle limit (0038).
- Players standing on the deck when the boat sets off fall into the water when the floor is cleared.
- A seated rider may move to a lower seat on departure (see Setting off from the deck).
- A deck-seat rider who did not fit in the boat is left on the deck when the boat sets off, and falls into the water like a player standing on it.
- Unverified: the spike did not test using a separate entity that sits inside the boat's hit boxes. Every deck seat stands at the hold y inside one of the boat's `minecraft:custom_hit_test` boxes (2.2 wide and 1.4 high from the boat's y, 0051). Unless the deck seat's own box reaches above that, a player on the deck looks down through the top of the boat's box first, so the boat may take the long press or right-click instead of the deck seat, just as a long press on the boat from the deck never reached the server in the spike. Verify on touch and PC in a manual playtest before release: standing on the deck, long-press (touch) and right-click (PC) each deck seat and check that the player sits in that seat and the boat takes no hit. If the boat takes the interaction, revisit this decision, for example by lowering or removing the boat's hit boxes in `yasu_ride:docked` or by raising the deck seats' hit area above the boat's.
