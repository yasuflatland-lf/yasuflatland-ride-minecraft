# 0167. Compound-rotation assemblies as a vehicle-neutral rule

- Status: Proposed
- Acceptance condition: owner decision pending (see #1193)
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Supersedes in part: [0009](0009-vehicle-model-conventions.md): the reading of the one-axis convention
- Supersedes in part: [0166](0166-tilted-two-axis-sports-boat-helm-assemblies.md): the one-assembly limit

## Context

The sports boat helm introduced a rigid compound tilt and a global wheel exception in 0166. Camper, SUV, and bulldozer generators need the same rule without depending on sports boat names. The existing wheel has 146 cubes, 140 with multiple non-zero Euler axes. The original assembly-registration refactor preserved every generated byte. Later geometry changes, including the camper dashboard in 0106, retain the registration rule and update their membership checks.

## Decision

- A cube may carry two or more non-zero Euler axes only when it belongs to a named assembly built through `tilt_assembly` and registered in its generator's `ASSEMBLIES` table. Other cubes retain at most one axis.
- The table shape is `{name: {"owner": generator_name, "cubes": count_or_description}}`. The sports boat declares only `{"wheel": {"owner": "sportsboat", "cubes": 146}}`. Shipped compound cubes that already existed in the other generators are registered as they stand (see below).
- `tilt_assembly(cubes, pivot, axis, degrees, assembly=None, assemblies=None)` requires the name to be a key in the supplied table whenever a result carries multiple axes. The default table is empty; unknown or missing names fail with `ValueError`, including a zero-degree tilt of an already compound cube.
- Every generator checks final model cubes before writing with `assert_axes(cubes, assemblies)`. This checker receives a separate `{name: tilted_member_cubes}` mapping in the final coordinate frame and compares cubes by value. It rejects multi-axis cubes outside those lists, using a non-zero tolerance of 1e-6. The bulldozer monitor is checked too.
- Shipped-model tests share `tests/packs/oneAxis.ts`, with `multipleAxes` and `assertOneAxis`, and declare their own membership tables. The sports boat uses its existing 146 wheel cubes; the SUV, camper and bulldozer pin the counts of their registered assemblies. Property tests exercise both Python and TypeScript checks without running generators.
- `tools/tool_loader.py` exposes cached `load_tool(name)` through `functools.cache` and `importlib`. Generators bootstrap the sibling directory onto `sys.path` because isolated Python (`-I`) omits it. The sports boat uses this loader for cube tilt, tile layout, and preview decoding.
- Preserve the rotation-order assumption from 0166: `Rz(-rz) * Ry(ry) * Rx(-rx)`, z outermost and x innermost. The assembly tilt multiplies on the left. Confirmation on a device remains pending; this refactor provides no new in-game evidence.
- To add a camper, SUV, or bulldozer assembly, add its name, owner, and cube count or description to the generator table; build it through `tilt_assembly(..., assembly=name, assemblies=ASSEMBLIES)`; retain its tilted member cubes through final coordinate conversion for `assert_axes`; and add the corresponding membership entry to its shipped-model test.
- The original registration refactor excluded throttle lever and dial changes in #682/#684 and changed no geometry or Resource Pack files. Later dashboard geometry is built by `tools/camper_dash.py`; any compound dashboard cubes must belong to a named assembly declared in the camper generator, with membership retained through the final mirror into Bedrock coordinates. Do not register unrelated new cubes by a broad rotation predicate merely to pass the check.

## Consequences

Implementation verification found compound cubes outside the sports boat that predate this rule, so the issue's premise that `wheel` is the only registered assembly did not hold. They are registered unchanged, without moving a cube: the SUV `arc` (16 cubes, the ring of cubes turned about two axes), the camper `slanted` (6, all turned `[45, 0, -20]`), and the bulldozer `blade_arms` (7, x turn 30), `yawed_pair` (2, `[-15, 25, 0]`) and `propeller` (4, y turn -18). Their generators collect them with `compound_cubes()` and `assert_axes(cubes, assemblies, table)` also checks that each assembly holds exactly the declared number of cubes. Those original registrations left the generated models byte-identical. Their counts describe that baseline, not a permanent budget for later geometry changes. The camper dashboard replacement removes the old console pieces. Its dash and fittings rotate about x alone, so it adds no compound assembly and leaves `slanted` (6) and `front_panels` (518) unchanged; the approved steering port adds `steering_wheel` (114 cubes: 112 rim, horn and hub cubes plus two body column cubes), `steering_spokes` (3), and `steering_lever` (30). Their compound subsets contain 2, 2 and 29 cubes respectively. All three use the zero-tilt `tilt_assembly` registration path to preserve the approved local rotations. The 115-cube, 0.8-scale wheel uses bone rotation [60, 0, 0], giving a 30-degree wheel plane; the perpendicular 60-degree dark-dash shaft and steel sleeve stay on `body` with the static lever. Ownership is retained by explicit cube slices and the steering area's body membership through the final mirror, so unrelated compound cubes cannot enter these assemblies. The static steering bone has no animation.

All four vehicle generators enforce one rule without a global vehicle-specific exception. Registration records ownership and scope, while the final checker catches compound cubes that escape their assembly. Each new assembly needs matching generator and test membership lists, which must remain in the checked coordinate frame.

A registration-only refactor must leave assets byte-identical under regeneration and the model freshness check. A later intentional geometry change must regenerate the affected model, textures, previews and membership assertions together. If the pending device check finds a different composition order, revisit the shared transform under a separate model change.
