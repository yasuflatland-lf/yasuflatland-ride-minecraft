# 0138. Raise and lower the bulldozer blade with a switch item

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Extends: [0024](0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) (switch items on mobile and their cooldown), [0035](0035-jump-one-block-steps-in-the-script.md) (script step-up), [0134](0134-switch-the-tent-lantern-with-the-headlight-switch.md) (the single switch router), [0136](0136-bulldozer.md) (the bulldozer kind and its bone contract), [0137](0137-clear-block-columns-with-the-blade.md) (when the blade cuts)
- Supersedes in part: [0136](0136-bulldozer.md) (its `stepUp: false` rule: a raised blade climbs steps)

## Context

The owner wants a "Bulldozer Switches" creative group like Camping Gear, holding a "Blade Up/Down" item used like the headlight switch. The limits that shape the design are: one switch router hard-coded to the headlight switch (0134: "No second router exists"); `TransformingOptions.stepUp` is a constructor-time boolean, and a subclass cannot pass a closure over `this` to `super(...)` (TS 17009, issue #525); the creative catalog is written whole by `tools/gen_camping_gear.py`; only `blade` is a bone and the push frame is fixed in `body`.

## Decision

- **Router (1/6).** `registerSwitchRouter(routes: SwitchRoutes)` takes a map from item type to its targets, and an item with no route is ignored. There is still exactly one router (0134).
- **Step-up hook (2/6).** `TransformingDrive` reads step-up through `protected stepUpEnabled(): boolean` (default: the `stepUp` option) at both the car step probe and the submarine rising probe, so a subclass can switch it per tick.
- **Catalog (3/6).** `tools/gen_item_catalog.py` writes the creative catalog from an ordered `GROUPS` list. Camping Gear stays first.
- **Model (4/6).** The bone contract (0009, 0136) gains `blade_rig` (parent `body`, pivot at the push-arm trunnion, holding the whole push frame except the lift-cylinder barrels), `lift_rod_r` and `lift_rod_l` (parent `body`, pivot at each barrel mouth, holding that side's chrome rod). `blade` becomes a child of `blade_rig`. The rest pose is lowered and is today's geometry. The entity property `yasu_ride:blade` (float, 0 lowered to 1 raised, client-synced) drives the drive animation: `blade_rig` moves 3 px up and 3 px rearward times the property, and each rod scales along z by `1 - property * 3 / 12.6` about the barrel mouth, so its front end stays at the moved rod-end bracket. Raised, the cutting edge's bottom is at y 3 px, 3.425 px above the grouser tips. The retraction is exaggerated by the owner's choice over strict kinematics.
- **Drive state (5/6).** `BulldozerDrive` keeps a target (raised or lowered) and eases the shown position one step per tick over `BULLDOZER_SPEC.bladeLiftTicks` = 10 ticks. It restores the blade from the property on rebuild (raised at `>= 0.5`) and resends it after a failed send (0127). The blade cuts only while it is fully lowered (target lowered and the easing finished), in every form; otherwise the 0137 cut path gets nothing. Raised, `stepUpEnabled()` returns true, so the bulldozer climbs 1- and 2-block steps and rises in water like the SUV and camper (0004, 0035). Lowered keeps 0136's no-step-up behavior. On a Jump-tap tick the jump velocity is the larger of the step probe's and the 0.45 hop, so a hop never cancels a step jump; a double tap still takes off. The `Drive` interface gains optional `bladeRaised?()` and `setBlade?(raised)`. `Vehicle.switchBlade(playerId, tick)` lets only the driver switch, with its own `SwitchCooldown` of `SPEC.bladeSwitchCooldown` = `LIGHT_SWITCH_COOLDOWN_TICKS` (10 ticks, 0024), separate from the headlight and ceiling-light cooldowns. A server-only test script event sets the blade for the in-game tests.
- **Item (6/6).** `yasu_ride:blade_switch` ("Blade Up/Down", with a Japanese name in `ja_JP.lang`) copies the headlight switch: stack size 1, `use_duration` 0.1, so a long press on mobile and a right click on PC each flip it once (0024). It is crafted shapeless from a lever and an iron ingot, always unlocked, and is the only item of the creative group `yasu_ride:bulldozer_switches` ("Bulldozer Switches"), the second group after Camping Gear. Its route claims an item use while the player rides a vehicle and a press on the player's own ridden vehicle, never a block press, and plays `random.click` at the vehicle on success. Spawned bulldozers start raised: `BULLDOZER_SPEC.bladeRaisedOnSpawn` true and the property default 1.0.

## Consequences

The README previews stay as they are (rest pose = lowered). In the raised pose the trunnion discs and the rear clamp brackets sink at most 0.2 px into the track's top run and the frame, measured; at rest some clamp brackets already sink 0.1 px. The sign of the animation's z offset follows 0043 and has to be checked in game once 5/6's test event can raise the blade. Revisit the offsets in 4/6 if that check shows the blade moving forward or the rod's front end leaving its bracket. The Resource Pack version goes up (0015). Accept this ADR together with 0136, after the manual checks that 6/6 adds to `docs/manual-test.md` pass on PC and mobile.
