# 0171. Sports Boat Equipment and icebreaker mode

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Extends: 0093 (placement gifts), 0138 (tool switching), 0139 (cab monitors), 0150 (water and lava buoyancy), 0166 (helm display)
- Supersedes in part: 0139 (Decision 9, catalog consistency: a group may hold items that are not monitor tools)

## Context

The owner wants Sports Boat Equipment, a creative group for one vehicle kind like the camper's Camping Gear. Its first tool switches an icebreaker mode on and off, like the bulldozer's blade switch (0138). The screen ahead of the helm must show the driver's held tool and its ON or OFF state, like the bulldozer's cab monitor (0139), and keep its current picture otherwise. On water or lava, a blocked bow must clear the boat's own size plus three blocks on each side, above and below, without removing either liquid. The icebreaker icon must show a bow at the bottom breaking ice.

The owner supplied the Kubernetes icon as a shape reference for the group's helm icon. It is a trademarked logo, so the reference supplies only the idea of a ship's wheel, not its badge, spoke count or colors. The equipment box exists because a creative group's icon must be an item in that group, and the helm is a different picture from the icebreaker tool's bow. A placement gift supplies that icon and gives the driver the first tool without a separate purchase or recipe.

The existing code provides these building blocks:

- `tools/gen_item_catalog.py` writes `packs/yasu_ride_bp/item_catalog/crafting_item_catalog.json` from ordered `GROUPS`; `validate` requires each icon to belong to its group. Today `camping_gear_inside` and `camping_gear_outside` precede `bulldozer_switches`, whose only item and icon is `blade_switch`.
- `PLACEMENT_GIFTS` and `PlacementGift` in `src/domain/vehicle/placement/gift.ts`, together with `VehicleProfile.placementGift`, select the camper's gift (0093). `GIFT_ITEM`, `GIFT_RECEIVED_PROPERTY`, `GIFT_CONTENTS` and `GIFT_LORE` in `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` are already `Record<PlacementGift, ...>` tables. A second gift adds a key to each; it needs no gift-system refactor. `OpenGift` consumes the box before unpacking it.
- `src/domain/vehicle/monitor/monitor.ts` defines `MonitorRule` with a group and ordered `MonitorTool` entries, `monitorScreens`, `monitorScreen` and `BLANK_SCREEN = 0`. `VehicleProfile.monitor` opts in; `Drive.toolState?(item)` supplies the state; `Vehicle.monitorCommands` sends the int property `yasu_ride:monitor`. `bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` reads held items through `riders(vehicle, profileOf(kind).monitor !== undefined)`. Screen 0 draws nothing, leaving the model's screen visible. `tools/gen_bulldozer.py` generates the monitor geometry, atlas and render controller from `tools/monitor_screens.json["bulldozer"]`. ADR 0139 requires moving this generator to a shared module in its own refactor before a second kind uses it.
- The shared switching path still names the blade: `Drive.bladeRaised?`, `Drive.setBlade?`, `Vehicle.switchBlade`, `Vehicle.forceBlade`, `SPEC.bladeSwitchCooldown`, the `SwitchBlade` use case in `src/application/vehicle/switchBlade.ts`, `bladeSwitchTarget` in `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/blade.ts`, and the `[BLADE_SWITCH_TYPE]` route in `subscriptions.ts`. `BLADE_SWITCH_ITEM` is re-exported by `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/index.ts`. The server-only hook is `yasu_ride:test_blade` with message `<id>,raised|lowered`.
- `CutColumn` and `CUT_KEPT_BLOCKS` live in `src/domain/vehicle/cut.ts` (0137, 0155). `DriveStep.cut` reaches the `DriveWorld.cutColumns` port; `BladeCuts` in `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bladeCuts.ts` fills columns with air while preserving `CUT_KEPT_BLOCKS` and `LAID_BLOCK_TAG`. The yaw-rotated rectangle cell selection is inside `bladeColumns` in `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/blade.ts`, inaccessible to other kinds.
- `SportsboatDrive implements Drive` in `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` combines water and lava in `liquidAt` and computes `afloat` (0150). It returns no cut and has no `toolState`. `SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE` has neither `monitor` nor `placementGift`. `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC` in that kind's `spec.ts` sets `ramFront` to 3.0, `ramHalfWidth` to 1.06 and `height` to 1.2 blocks. `tools/gen_sportsboat.py` draws the dash's `"screen"` cube with the `display` tile, `SCREEN_TILT = -45` and `SCREEN_PIVOT`; its face is 6.4 x 3.6 px, 16:9 (0166).

## Decision

1. **Group (5/7).** Add `yasu_ride:sportsboat_equipment` to the Items tab immediately after `bulldozer_switches`. Its en_US name is "Sports Boat Equipment" and its ja_JP name is `\u8239\u306e\u88c5\u5099`. In order, its items are `sportsboat_equipment_box` and `icebreaker_switch`; its icon is `sportsboat_equipment_box`.
2. **Equipment box (5/7).** `yasu_ride:sportsboat_equipment_box` is the second placement gift (0093), given once per player on their first sports boat placement through `SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE.placementGift`. It is not craftable and has stack size 1. Use the existing `OpenGift` flow: consume the box and give one `icebreaker_switch`, with overflow dropped at the player's feet. The player received flag is `yasu_ride:sportsboat_equipment_given`. The unpacked switch carries lore key `yasu_ride.lore.sportsboat_equipment`, with en_US "Sports Boat Equipment" and ja_JP `\u8239\u306e\u88c5\u5099`. The box names are en_US "Sports Boat Equipment Box" and ja_JP `\u8239\u306e\u88c5\u5099\u30dc\u30c3\u30af\u30b9`. Its 16 x 16 icon is a ship's wheel with a round wooden rim, a brass hub and eight spokes extending past the rim as handles. Do not reproduce the Kubernetes logo: no heptagon badge and no blue-and-white logo colors.
3. **Icebreaker Switch (5/7).** `yasu_ride:icebreaker_switch` follows `packs/yasu_ride_bp/items/yasu_ride/blade_switch.item.json`: stack size 1 and `use_duration` 0.1. Its names are en_US "Icebreaker Switch" and ja_JP `\u7815\u6c37\u30b9\u30a4\u30c3\u30c1`. A shapeless recipe uses one `minecraft:lever`, one `minecraft:iron_ingot` and one `minecraft:packed_ice`, yields one switch and is always unlocked, so a lost switch can be replaced. Its 16 x 16 icon shows a ship's bow from the side, pointing down into the bottom rows, with cracked light-blue ice blocks and flying shards under the stem. The monitor uses the same pixel map.
4. **Monitor (6/7).** Set the sports boat's profile to `monitor: { group: "sportsboat_equipment", tools: [{ item: "icebreaker_switch", states: ["off", "on"] }] }`. Labels are OFF and ON. Only the driver's held item counts; passengers do not. Holding anything else, including the equipment box, gives `BLANK_SCREEN`, leaving the existing `display` picture visible. The equipment box is a group member but not a monitor tool: account for that gift when checking catalog consistency instead of giving it a screen. This supersedes in part 0139 Decision 9, which kept the tool list equal to the creative group: the group test compares the tools with the group minus its placement-gift items.
5. **Switching (2/7, 6/7).** One use by the driver advances the held tool to the next entry in its `states` list, wrapping around. Share the switch cooldown, renaming today's `SPEC.bladeSwitchCooldown` to `SPEC.toolSwitchCooldown` in 2/7 and retaining its 10 ticks. The icebreaker state is the bool entity property `yasu_ride:icebreaker`, default false, with no client synchronization needed. Restore it when rebuilding the boat after a reload. A new boat starts with the mode off.
6. **Cut trigger (7/7).** Cut on a tick only when all five conditions hold: the mode is on; the boat has a driver; the existing `afloat` result is true for water or lava (0150); `toDriveInput(raw).throttle > 0`; and the bow is blocked. A beached boat never cuts. The bow is blocked when `ctx.solidAt` is true at either probe height for at least one cell under the yaw-rotated strip from `probeNear` to `ramFront + 1` ahead of the center, with half width `ramHalfWidth`. `probeNear` is 1.1, half the 2.2-wide square collision box in `sportsboat.se.json`, set in `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker`: the engine stops the boat at that box, 1.1 to about 1.56 blocks ahead of the center depending on yaw, well short of `ramFront`, so the strip must start at the box's front to reach the block that stops the boat. Probe at 0.1 and `height - 0.1` blocks above the feet. Sideways creep, reverse and an unblocked bow do not trigger a cut.
7. **Cut volume (7/7).** Select columns under the yaw-rotated rectangle from `-sternReach` to `ramFront + 3` ahead of the center, with half width `ramHalfWidth + 3`. `sternReach` is the hull's rearmost reach behind its center, **measured in 7/7** from `packs/yasu_ride_rp/models/entity/yasu_ride/sportsboat.geo.json`. Each column starts at `floor(feetY) - 3` and has a fixed height reaching up to `ceil(feetY + height + 3)`, as the upper boundary; its `CutColumn.height` is that boundary minus its bottom. The box adds three blocks left, right, above and below, and three ahead of the bow where the obstacle is; it adds no margin behind the stern. Reuse the blade's rectangle cell selection, including its touching-cell boundary convention. Preserve water, flowing water, lava, flowing lava, item holders and air through `CUT_KEPT_BLOCKS`, and laid blocks through `LAID_BLOCK_TAG`. Everything else, including ice, becomes air. Sweep drops through `registerCutDrops` like the blade.
8. **Ownership and order.** Put kind-neutral switching on `MonitorTool` in the shared core (2/7), extract the monitor generator to `tools/monitor.py` (3/7), and extract rectangle cell selection to the shared core (4/7). These three refactors preserve behavior and pack output. Add the group, box and switch in 5/7, the boat's monitor and switch state in 6/7, then `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker` and cut logic in `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/` in 7/7. Keep per-kind values in the kind's `spec.ts` or `profile.ts` (0050); introduce no kind checks in application or infrastructure. Use stable Script APIs only (0001), preserve the layer, feature and kind boundaries (0012, 0046, 0055, 0065), and use property-based tests for pure logic rather than tests that only repeat a value (0010). Only `sternReach` remains to be filled in by measurement; later steps implement these decisions without choosing new behavior.

## Consequences

The shared switch path loses its blade-specific names, including its drive hooks, vehicle methods, use case, handler, route wiring, kind export and test hook. The bulldozer keeps its behavior while the sports boat supplies a second tool through the same path. The gift tables gain entries without a gift-system refactor, and the shared monitor generator supports the tilted helm display while keeping its default picture.

The icebreaker clears a box, not a hull-shaped outline. It runs only while the bow is blocked and stops once the way is clear, even with the mode still on and forward throttle held. The full box can remove solid blocks beside and below the boat as well as ahead. Revisit the cut size if it is too slow on a server, and revisit the recipe if replacing a lost switch proves too costly or too easy.
