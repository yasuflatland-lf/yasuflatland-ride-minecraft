## Rules

### Forms and transition priority

- Applies to: SUV and camper transforming drives; the camper supplies its own `CAMPER_SPEC` through `CAMPER_TRANSFORMING`.
- Rule: `FORMS` declares `car`, `takeoff`, `hover`, `landing`, and `submarine`, mirrored by `yasu_ride:form`. Submergence (`waterDepth >=` the kind's `carHeight`) wins over every other transition. Otherwise a controlled double tap takes `car` to `takeoff`; takeoff advances to `hover` after `takeoffTicks` ticks (the entry tick counts). In hover, ground contact returns directly to `car` before checking a double tap or loss of control; either of those starts `landing` when airborne. Landing returns to `car` on ground contact. In submarine, dry feet with control and Jump held breach to `hover` before the ground check; otherwise dry feet or ground contact below the submergence threshold return to `car`. A submerged submarine stays submarine even on the bed. Double taps are accepted only in car and hover, within `doubleTapTicks`, and a form change clears the pending tap. The SUV definitions are `SUV_SPEC.doubleTapTicks` and `SUV_SPEC.takeoffTicks`; no pitch is part of the input.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0039](../adr/0039-vehicle-forms.md) Decision, "The `car` form has gravity; every other form does not."; [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Apply the following transitions in priority order; otherwise keep the current form."; [0021](../adr/0021-flight-mode.md) Decision, "Takeoff and landing ignore Jump."; [0034](../adr/0034-touch-down-from-flight.md) Decision, "A `flying` vehicle that is on the ground goes straight to `ground` on that tick, without `landing`."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/suv.se.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper.se.json`
- Verification: Existing tests read, not run for this spec: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.test.ts` "submergence wins over every other transition from every other form", "takes off only on a controlled Jump press that follows another press within doubleTapTicks", "stays in takeoff for exactly takeoffTicks ticks, whatever the input", "first enters hover on the call after takeoffTicks is reached", "breaches to hover exactly when dry, controlled, and Jump is held", "returns to car on dry or shallow ground unless breaching to hover", "forgets a pending tap whenever the form changes". Catalog scenarios: `flight_course`, `flight_touchdown`, `submarine_course`, `submarine_shallow`, `submarine_shore`. Not verified: current BDS results or PC/touch input feel.

### Gravity and restored state

- Applies to: SUV and camper, every form.
- Rule: Only car has gravity. A changed form emits `yasu_ride:gravity_on` for car or `yasu_ride:gravity_off` otherwise, before writing `yasu_ride:form`. Gravity off adds `yasu_ride:weightless` and removes `yasu_ride:weighted`; gravity on does the reverse. The weighted group restores `minecraft:physics` rather than merely removing the weightless group. Rebuild reads the restored form once, accepts declared forms and otherwise starts as car; it primes the form latch so an unchanged first tick sends no form command. Bank starts at 0 in every restored form, and gear starts down only when the restored form is car; both are synchronized on the first tick using the resulting state and subsequently when changed. A failed step-command send or tick resynchronizes all three values for retry.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0021](../adr/0021-flight-mode.md) Decision, "The script reads the property only when it rebuilds an aggregate after `/reload`."; [0039](../adr/0039-vehicle-forms.md) Decision, "The `car` form has gravity; every other form does not."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingEntity.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/suv.se.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper.se.json`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingEntity.test.ts` "keeps gravity on only in car form and writes the form after the event (ADR 0021, 0039)"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.test.ts` "keeps the restored form primed on its first unchanged tick", "resync sends gravity and form first, then the current bank and gear"; `tests/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.test.ts` "reads restored properties only when an aggregate is missing", "retries gravity_on after a failed landing send and logs the failure once"; `tests/packs/behaviorPack.test.ts` "switches gravity off with yasu_ride:gravity_off and back on with yasu_ride:gravity_on, and syncs the vehicle form to clients (ADR 0021)". Catalog scenario: `flight_land`. Not verified: reload persistence on a current client.

### Air motion

- Applies to: SUV and camper in takeoff, hover, and landing; SUV tuning names below have camper equivalents.
- Rule: Takeoff maps all controls to neutral. Hover and landing retain steering and forward throttle, omit the handbrake and drop negative horizontal throttle. Sideways input steers and creeps. `stepMediumMotion` uses the kind's air medium: for the SUV, `SUV_SPEC.flightDrag` and `SUV_SPEC.maxSpeed`, with `SUV_MOTION` handling. It pre-divides horizontal target velocity by medium drag and vertical target velocity by `flightVerticalDrag`, then subtracts observed velocity to obtain the impulse. Ground friction estimation and step jumps are absent from the medium step. The per-axis wall check remains, using medium drag and `MOTION.blockedRatio`; it lowers internal speed and preserves motion along a free wall axis. Takeoff targets `takeoffClimb`, hover targets climb command times `maxClimb` less hover bank sink, and landing targets negative `landingSink`; the result is capped to plus or minus `maxClimb`. Ram selection remains enabled by the vehicle core and uses resulting speed.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0021](../adr/0021-flight-mode.md) Decision, "Step climbing (0004) and the ground friction estimate (0003) are off while airborne."; [0021](../adr/0021-flight-mode.md) Decision, "Ram damage (0006) stays on"; [0032](../adr/0032-climb-with-jump-and-sink-with-the-stick.md) Decision, "Takeoff and landing keep their fixed vertical speeds (0021)"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`stepMediumMotion`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleFlight.test.ts` "ignores the controls during takeoff", "holds altitude while cruising with a level view and reaches max speed", "rams a target at most once when it flies into a wall with the throttle held"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.test.ts` "leaves the target vertical velocity after the engine's vertical drag". Catalog: `flight_course` includes its `cruise` and `climb_descend` steps. Not verified: motion on a current BDS or measured drag in this documentation task.

### Climb command

- Applies to: SUV and camper in hover and submarine.
- Rule: `climbInput` computes `clamp((Jump held ? 1 : 0) + min(0, toDriveInput(raw).throttle), -1, 1)`. Holding Jump climbs even without forward speed; pulling back sinks; full pull-back with Jump cancels the climb command, while partial pull-back still climbs. Sideways input does not sink. Missing input gives zero climb, and view pitch is not read. Negative horizontal throttle is discarded outside car form, so pulling back coasts instead of applying reverse or braking. Hover bank sink still applies after the climb command; submarine bank sink does not.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0032](../adr/0032-climb-with-jump-and-sink-with-the-stick.md) Decision, "Holding Jump climbs, pulling the stick back (S on PC) sinks, and Jump with the stick pulled fully back holds altitude; with the stick only partly back, Jump still climbs."; [0032](../adr/0032-climb-with-jump-and-sink-with-the-stick.md) Decision, "The view pitch is not read any more."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.ts` (`climbInput`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts` (`groundInputFor`, `airImpulse`, `submarineImpulse`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.test.ts` "stays within -1 to 1", "climbs fully with Jump held and the stick centered or pushed forward", "sinks with the stick pulled back past the dead zone and never climbs without Jump"; `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleFlight.test.ts` "climbs on held Jump while hovering in place (ADR 0032)". Catalog: `flight_course`, `submarine_course`. Not verified: gesture feel on PC and touch.

### Bank and body roll

- Applies to: SUV and camper motion; SUV client model.
- Rule: In hover and submarine the target bank is the clamped normalized heading change divided by the kind's turn rate, multiplied by its `maxBank`; other forms target level. The angle approaches its target by at most `bankRate` each tick. For the SUV these are `SUV_SPEC.turnRate`, `SUV_SPEC.maxBank`, and `SUV_SPEC.bankRate`. Only hover loses altitude by `abs(bank) / maxBank * bankSink`; a kind with zero maximum bank has zero bank sink. `yasu_ride:bank` is client-synced, initially level, and written on the first tick and changes. The always-running SUV bank animation rotates `root` around z by `-query.property('yasu_ride:bank')`. The exported root pivot is inside the body, scaled from the generator pivot. The collision box and rider seats do not roll. `rear_glass`, a child of `body`, uses opaque blue glass and shows whenever form is not car.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0026](../adr/0026-bank-into-turns-in-flight.md) Decision, "The angle moves toward the target by at most `SPEC.bankRate` (3°) per tick"; [0026](../adr/0026-bank-into-turns-in-flight.md) Decision, "Players cannot be rolled with the stable API"; [0044](../adr/0044-bank-under-water.md) Decision, "`bankSink` stays hover only: a submarine's vertical speed comes from the climb input and buoyancy (0040), and a banked turn under water keeps its depth"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/bank.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/suv.anim.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/suv.ce.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/render_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.rc.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/models/entity/yasu_ride/suv.geo.json`; `tools/suv_palette.py`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/bank.test.ts` "moves at most bankRate per tick and stays within maxBank", "banks toward the turn in hover and submarine form: right side down when the heading turns right (yaw increases)", "sinks only while flying, more the steeper the bank, up to bankSink at full bank"; `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleSubmarine.test.ts` "banks into a turn under water, keeps its depth, and levels out after"; `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "rolls the whole model about its length by yasu_ride:bank", "rolls around a pivot inside the body rather than at the wheels' contact patch". Catalog: `flight_bank`, the `bank` step of `submarine_course`. Not verified: client roll, upright riders hidden by glass, or their appearance from side views.

### Landing gear and probes

- Applies to: SUV and camper, all forms.
- Rule: `yasu_ride:gear_down` is a client-synced bool defaulting to true. Car and submarine put gear down; takeoff and hover put it up. Landing probes only with gear still up, checking the center and four corners at the kind's `gearProbeOffset` along heading-relative axes, at integer downward offsets through `gearDownHeight`. Any solid hit lowers gear, which stays down until the form changes. The SUV uses `SUV_SPEC.gearDownHeight` and `SUV_SPEC.gearProbeOffset`. The engine adapter rejects liquid, air, light blocks, and blocks without a colliding surface. Bank and gear use independent first-tick/change synchronization. Direct hover touchdown skips the landing approach probe, so unfolding starts after touchdown.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0033](../adr/0033-lower-the-wheels-before-touchdown.md) Decision, "The blocks are probed only while landing with the gear up"; [0033](../adr/0033-lower-the-wheels-before-touchdown.md) Decision, "The flag goes to the client through the bool entity property `mycar:gear_down` (default `true`, `client_sync`), written on the first tick of an aggregate and after that only on the ticks it changes, as the bank is (0026)"; [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Keep gear down in submarine, including immediately on entry from hover (0033)."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/landingGear.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts` (`groundNear`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`solidAt`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/landingGear.test.ts` "comes down while landing once the ground is near, and stays down", "probes the ground only while landing with the wheels up"; `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleFlight.test.ts` "lowers the wheels while landing over ground within gearDownHeight under the center or any corner, and keeps them up over deeper ground, in any heading". Catalog: `flight_land`, `flight_touchdown`. Not verified: apparent wheel contact during low landings on clients.

### Driver handover wait

- Applies to: SUV and camper when a driver leaves riders aboard.
- Rule: `Crew` counts ticks since a driver was last present. With no current driver and at least one rider, `awaitingDriver` stays true through `SPEC.driverHandoverTicks` (defined as 6). `Vehicle` forwards that flag through `TickContext`; `TransformingDrive` treats raw input or the wait as controlled. Without raw input the drive receives neutral controls, preventing driverless hover landing and positive submarine buoyancy during the wait. If nobody takes over, hover starts landing on the next tick after the window; no riders means no wait. The wait applies only while riders remain aboard; a hovering vehicle with nobody aboard has no wait and starts landing at once. A rebuilt crew has no remembered driver tick, so passengers alone receive no wait. Ground contact and submergence retain their transition priority.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0078](../adr/0078-wait-for-a-passenger-before-landing.md) Decision, "While it waits, the drive runs with neutral input but counts as controlled: a hovering vehicle holds its altitude and does not start landing, and a submerged one does not float up."; [0078](../adr/0078-wait-for-a-passenger-before-landing.md) Decision, "A vehicle rebuilt after `/reload` with riders but nobody in seat 0 gets no wait, because the core cannot tell when its driver left"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/crew.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/drive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/crew.test.ts` "delegates seating and waits only within the driver handover window"; `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleFlight.test.ts` "keeps hovering while a passenger moves up within the handover ticks, and the new driver climbs (suv)", "starts landing on the tick after the handover ticks when nobody moves up (suv)", "gets no handover ticks when rebuilt in flight with nobody in the driver seat (suv)" (also instantiated for camper). Catalog: `seats_after_driver_leaves` checks engine move-up, rather than the flight grace period. Not verified: a passenger flight handover or submarine handover on the current BDS.

### Water sensing and depth

- Applies to: SUV and camper, sampled at the feet every tick.
- Rule: `waterDepthAt` scans the floored x/z column upward from `floor(feetY)`, sampling cell centers. If that cell is dry and feet are fractional, it starts in the next cell to cover partial-block tops; a dry cell at integer feet returns zero immediately. It counts contiguous water cells up to `ceil(carHeight) + 1`, stops at the first dry cell, and caps surface height minus feet height at `carHeight + 1`. A dry initial cell may require one additional probe before the upward run. Surface cells count as full regardless of flowing level. The adapter accepts `minecraft:water`, `minecraft:flowing_water`, `minecraft:bubble_column`, and `isWaterlogged` blocks. Missing, unloaded or out-of-world cells and lava are dry. Roof-depth entry and fully dry exit give hysteresis; shallow water alone does not transform car into submarine.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Entering submarine requires the roof under water, while breaching to hover requires the body fully out: this gives a car-height band of hysteresis."; [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Ignore flowing levels and count each surface cell as full."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/water.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/forms.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/water.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/water.test.ts` "measures water above a dry partial block the feet rest inside from the feet", "stops at a gap even when water continues above it", "measures arbitrary columns only to their first gap, including feet near cell boundaries", "counts the surface cell as full"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.test.ts` "uses the supplied car height to decide whether the roof is submerged". Catalog: `submarine_shallow`, `submarine_course`. Not verified: every waterlogged block type or flowing-water appearance in game.

### Submarine climb, buoyancy, and shore rise

- Applies to: SUV and camper in submarine; SUV parameter names below have camper equivalents.
- Rule: Vertical priority is controlled finite nonzero climb times `maxClimb`, then a forward shore-step rise at `maxClimb`, then buoyancy `clamp((waterDepth - floatDraft) * floatGain, -surfaceRise, surfaceRise)`. Controlled buoyancy discards positive rise, so idle input holds deep water depth and sinks toward the float line from above; an uncontrolled submarine returns toward the float line from either side. Non-finite climb is no command and non-finite depth is dry. The shore probe needs step-up enabled, positive throttle and nonnegative resulting speed; its look-ahead uses at least `MOTION.stepMinSpeed` and its vertical origin drops by `submarineStepProbeDrop`. It checks one- or two-block steps with body clearance, rising instead of issuing a gravity-dependent jump. The vertical impulse pre-divides by `waterVerticalDrag`. SUV tuning is `SUV_SPEC.maxClimb`, `floatDraft`, `floatGain`, `surfaceRise`, `submarineStepProbeDrop`, `waterVerticalDrag`, `carHeight`, and `hitboxFront`.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Controlled vehicles discard positive buoyancy, so no input holds depth below the float line but sinks back to it from above."; [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Rise at steps instead of jumping (0035), because submarine has no gravity."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/submarine.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/submarine.test.ts` "a controlled finite non-zero climb wins over steps and buoyancy", "controlled buoyancy only sinks above the float line and otherwise holds depth", "uncontrolled buoyancy returns toward the float line"; `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleSubmarine.test.ts` "climbs a shore step fixed in the world from any fractional float height", "looks ahead at least stepMinSpeed's distance when rising from a near stop", "does not rise for a step while still rolling backward from reverse", "still rises while a shore step stops it, as a step jump does on the ground". Catalog: `submarine_course`, `submarine_shore`, `submarine_unmanned`. Not verified: the current engine float line or shore clearance with client riders.

### Submarine horizontal handling

- Applies to: SUV and camper in submarine; the sports boat has its own drive and does not use `TransformingDrive`.
- Rule: Submarine uses the shared medium step with the kind's water medium and ground handling limits, without handbrake or negative horizontal throttle. For the SUV, `SUV_SPEC.waterDrag` pre-divides horizontal velocity and `SUV_SPEC.maxWaterSpeed` caps both internal speed and target velocity, bounded also by `SUV_SPEC.maxSpeed`. Negative throttle commands vertical descent while horizontal motion coasts. Existing negative speed can decay from an earlier car reverse; the mapping does not initiate a new reverse under water. Walls use water drag for the per-axis threshold. Ram damage stays enabled, gear is down, and bank affects appearance without submarine bank sink. Camper values come from `CAMPER_SPEC`, not SUV constants.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Use hover horizontal handling without reverse or handbrake."; [0040](../adr/0040-submarine.md) Decision, "Keep ram damage (0006); bank only in hover."; [0044](../adr/0044-bank-under-water.md) Decision, "`bankSink` stays hover only: a submarine's vertical speed comes from the climb input and buoyancy (0040), and a banked turn under water keeps its depth"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleSubmarine.test.ts` "levels off at water speed and coasts without reversing when the stick is pulled back", "caps speed at water speed so a released throttle slows down at once", "keeps ram damage enabled and lowers speed when a wall stops it", "uses water drag for the per-axis wall threshold"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingPin.test.ts` "the SUV steps tick by tick as today's transforming wiring does", "the camper's drivetrain with its end guard steps tick by tick as today's wiring does". ### Rider camera selection

- Applies to: Player riders of loaded vehicles; form-forced chase view for SUV and camper in every non-car form.
- Rule: `TransformingDrive` reports `riders.thirdPerson` whenever the resulting form is not car. `DriveVehicles` collects those vehicle riders and uses their profile camera: SUV `mid`, camper `far`; sports boat declares `near` and bulldozer `far`. The sports boat reports no form-forced third person. `CHASE_CAMERAS` maps near to `minecraft:third_person`, mid to `yasu_ride:mid_chase`, and far to `yasu_ride:far_chase`. Mid and far inherit `minecraft:follow_orbit`; their preset radius definitions are 8 and 12 blocks respectively. Another feature can request a profile camera for ordinary or deck-seat riders, but bootstrap currently supplies `wantsChaseCamera: () => false`. A changed desired preset switches again; leaving the desired set (landing, getting off, or disappearance) clears only a camera this use case tracked. Tracking is in memory across ticks, not reloads.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0028](../adr/0028-third-person-camera-in-flight.md) Decision, "The use case remembers the players it switched, so each switch is one call, and it only clears players it switched itself."; [0071](../adr/0071-pull-the-camper-chase-camera-back.md) Decision, "`DriveVehicles` remembers which chase camera it set for each player and switches a rider again when the camera their vehicle wants changes, for example a rider who moves from a flying SUV to a flying camper."; [0170](../adr/0170-measure-suv-side-view-proportions.md) Notes, "Add a `mid` chase camera between `near` and `far` (ADR 0071): the pack's `yasu_ride:mid_chase` preset inherits from `minecraft:follow_orbit` with a radius of 8 blocks, and the SUV profile uses it."

### Camera retry and logging

- Applies to: Every requested camera switch or clear.
- Rule: `RiderEffects` updates its remembered preset only after `setChaseCamera` succeeds. Failed set or clear retries on the next tick while still needed. A missing player is a successful no-op in the Bedrock adapter, so a clear removes tracking. Failures log once per player until a successful switch clears the failure marker; a failure does not stop other riders or vehicles. If the failed wish ceases to be needed, its failure marker persists and can suppress logs on a later flight. Reload loses both tracking collections; clearing a camera set by another add-on during flight remains possible.
- Agreement: Accepted

### Water breathing

### Wheel fold, discs, and serialized transforms

- Applies to: SUV client assets; camper hover display details are in `camper-hover-display.md`.
- Rule: `controller.animation.yasu_ride.suv.form` runs `ground` to `deploy` to `hover` to `retract` to `ground` from gear changes. Wheel spin runs only in ground. Each wheel folds oppositely on the two sides by 90 degrees about z, hub down, with translations derived from `FOLD_OUT` and `FOLD_DROP` in `tools/suv_dims.py`, multiplied by `SIZE_SCALE`. Deploy and retract each last one second. Four `glow_*` bones under folded wheels and their `hover_*` locators move outward with the fold; the generated discs use `GLOW_RADIUS` and `GLOW_SEGMENTS` in `tools/suv_running_gear.py`. The SUV base render controller shows discs exactly when gear is up, including deploy; it hides them when gear comes down, including submarine. The hover state alone runs `hover_glow`, emitting downward `yasu_ride:hover_glow` particles at all four locators, and the hover sound animations. The default material is `entity_emissive_alpha`; discs look bright without placing block light. Submarine transforms serialize through `ground`: `ground` chooses `dive` before deploy, then `submarine`, `surface`, and ground; hover-to-submarine retracts first and submarine-to-hover surfaces first. Transition animations finish before the next state; props and arms are pinned by scale in every state.
- Agreement: Accepted

- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.ac.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/suv.anim.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/render_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.rc.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/suv.ce.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/particles/yasu_ride/hover_glow.particle.json`; `tools/suv_dims.py`; `tools/suv_running_gear.py`; `tools/suv_export.py`

### Propellers and sealed windows

- Applies to: SUV submarine model and client animations.
- Rule: `windows_sealed`, a body child, covers front and side windows with the opaque blue rear-glass swatch only in submarine; `rear_glass` covers the back in every non-car form. Rear wheel and prop assemblies translate outward by scaled `SUB_OUT`, then turn 90 degrees about y during the one-second dive; surface reverses the sequence over one second. Front wheels retain their road pose. Props grow from zero to full scale, spin from `query.modified_distance_moved` in the looping submerged animation, and shrink on surfacing. Every other transform state pins them to zero. The nominal hub half-width is 1.4 construction pixels and the bore half-width is 0.6, implemented by turned-square discs rather than a true circular radius. Blade and hub colors are `PALETTE["prop"]` and `PALETTE["prop_hub"]`; the geometry scales at export and stays flat in the wheel plane inside the tire ring.
