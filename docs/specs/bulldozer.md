### Drive and controls

- Applies to: Bulldozer in car, takeoff, hover, landing, and submarine forms.
- Rule: `BulldozerDrive` uses `TransformingDrive` with its own input mapping and horizontal step. Ground forward speed and acceleration are `BULLDOZER_SPEC.maxSpeed` and `accel`, half the SUV values; reverse uses `maxReverse` and `reverseAccel`, and braking, drag, and grip use the corresponding spec fields. A finite sideways input whose magnitude exceeds `BULLDOZER_SPEC.pivotStick` takes priority over throttle, changes yaw by the clamped stick times `turnRate`, and brakes toward zero by `brake` without reversing. Otherwise the vehicle drives straight. There is no creep or handbrake. Takeoff ignores horizontal input. In other non-car forms, back input sinks rather than reversing; horizontal speed is capped by the smaller of the motion limit and the medium limit (`BULLDOZER_AIR` or `BULLDOZER_WATER`). Vertical motion, buoyancy, transition timing, and landing probes use `BULLDOZER_TRANSFORMING`, derived from `BULLDOZER_SPEC`. Bank stays zero; its client property has a nonzero declaration range despite that zero maximum. A ground Jump tap uses at least `BULLDOZER_SPEC.hopVelocity`, preserving a larger step jump, and sets the remaining air-control ticks to `hopTicks - 1`. A second tap within `doubleTapTicks` starts takeoff. Rebuilding reasserts the restored form and gravity on the first step.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0136](../adr/0136-bulldozer.md) Decision, "Implement `BulldozerDrive` on `TransformingDrive`, with its own control mapping and horizontal step supplied through `TransformingOptions` (0089)."
- Basis: [0136](../adr/0136-bulldozer.md) Decision, "Forward and back never steer. There is no creep or handbrake."
- Basis: [0138](../adr/0138-raise-and-lower-the-bulldozer-blade.md) Decision, "On a Jump-tap tick the jump velocity is the larger of the step probe's and the 0.45 hop, so a hop never cancels a step jump; a double tap still takes off."

### Blade lift switch

- Applies to: Bulldozer, all forms; `yasu_ride:blade_switch` (Blade Up/Down).
- Rule: The driver switches a target between up and down through `Vehicle.switchTool`. All monitor tools share `SPEC.toolSwitchCooldown`, independently of light switches. The single switch router accepts item use while riding and an entity press on the player's own ridden vehicle, never a block press; a successful change plays `random.click`. The item has stack size 1 and its short use duration supports PC right-click and mobile long press. New bulldozers use `BULLDOZER_SPEC.bladeRaisedOnSpawn` and the raised property default. Each step moves the shown lift one increment toward the target over `BULLDOZER_SPEC.bladeLiftTicks`, emitting client-synced float `yasu_ride:blade` from 0 lowered to 1 raised. Rebuild treats a numeric saved property at least 0.5 as raised, snaps the shown lift to that target endpoint, and resends it; failed delivery also resends it. The raised target enables both the ground step-up and submarine rising probes immediately, even during easing; the lowered target disables them immediately. Cutting waits until both target and shown lift are fully lowered. `blade_rig` translates upward and rearward using `BLADE_RAISE` and `ROD_RETRACT`; the lift cylinders turn by `LIFT_SWING`, and their child rods shorten by `ROD_SHORTEN / ROD_LENGTH` in the animation. The server-only tool test hook can force the target without role or cooldown checks.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0138](../adr/0138-raise-and-lower-the-bulldozer-blade.md) Decision, "It restores the blade from the property on rebuild (raised at `>= 0.5`) and resends it after a failed send (0127)."
- Basis: [0138](../adr/0138-raise-and-lower-the-bulldozer-blade.md) Decision, "The blade cuts only while it is fully lowered (target lowered and the easing finished), in every form; otherwise the 0137 cut path gets nothing."

### Forward cut gates and cells

- Applies to: Bulldozer with raw input and a fully lowered blade.
- Rule: After the motion step, forward cutting requires no mapped pivot and positive speed strictly above the current blade rule's `minSpeed`. Reverse, stopped motion, airborne car form, a raised target, or unfinished lowering produces no forward cut. The grounded hop-start tick can cut. The rectangle is the current blade rule's `near`, `far`, and `halfWidth`, transformed at the pre-move location and post-step yaw. `rectCells` includes every unit square that touches the rectangle using closed separating-axis comparisons with coordinate-scaled floating-point tolerance. Grounded car form requests run columns with bottom `feetRow(y, true)` and `minHeight = ceil(y + rule.bodyHeight) - bottom`. The adapter fills at least that body range through air gaps, taking the greater of its minimum top and the non-air run scanned from the bottom; that scan stops at its first air or unavailable cell. It does not restart above a gap. Takeoff, hover, landing, and submarine forms request fixed columns ending just below `ceil(y + rule.bodyHeight)`. Their bottom is `feetRow(y, onGround)`: `ceil(y - 0.01)` on ground, including a partial seabed block, otherwise `floor(y + 0.01)`. Thus grounded feet keep their floor row, while a floating body clears the row it occupies. Water and other kept blocks survive; sea plants, coral, and waterlogged blocks have no additional exemption.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0136](../adr/0136-bulldozer.md) Decision, "selecting every unit square that touches it with a closed separating-axis test."
- Basis: [0195](../adr/0195-cut-the-body-height-ahead-of-a-grounded-blade.md) Decision, "Each ground column carries this body height as its minimum height (`minHeight`), and the cut scan treats a gap below it as part of the run."
- Basis: [0155](../adr/0155-cut-under-water-and-dig-a-shaft-when-rising.md) Decision, "any fixed-height cut on the ground follows the same rule."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.ts` (`forwardCut`, `cutReach`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/blade.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/cutArea.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/cut.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bladeCuts.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/blade.test.ts` covers touching columns, floor rounding, minimum body height, fixed height, and wide containment; `drive.test.ts` covers forms, speed, pivot, hop, and seabed gates. `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bladeCuts.test.ts` covers minimum fills through gaps and extensions of an unbroken run. Scenarios include `bulldozer_overhang`, `bulldozer_overhang_clear`, `bulldozer_dirt_path`, `bulldozer_flight_tunnel`, and `bulldozer_sea_cut`. Not verified: actual PC/mobile overhang and submarine behavior, B25 and B75; no test establishes restarting the run above a low air gap, and the adapter does not implement that behavior.

### Cut protection and cleanup

- Applies to: Every bulldozer cut column, including forward cuts and rising shafts, in every game mode.
- Rule: The shared `KEPT_BLOCKS` list combines air and the four water/lava IDs with `PLACE_KEPT_BLOCKS`, the explicit item-holder list: containers and their variants, processing inventories, shelves, frames, campfires, pots, suspicious sand/gravel, and bee homes. Blocks tagged `yasu_ride:laid` also survive. Bedrock, barriers, command and structure blocks, end portal frames, reinforced deepslate, and light blocks are not kept by type. Each column clamps to the dimension height range, checks its bottom with `getBlock`, and uses one air `fillBlocks` with `excludeTypes`, `excludeTags`, and `ignoreChunkBoundErrors: true`. An undefined bottom or thrown column operation skips that column; later columns continue. Application order is step commands, clear, cut, pave, then movement; an uncaught world-call failure aborts the remaining tick for that vehicle inside per-vehicle isolation. Successful fills record boxes in a shared `DropSweep`. It removes only valid `minecraft:item` entities in the same dimension within `DROP_MARGIN` on every axis and `DROP_WINDOW_TICKS` after the fill, including the fill tick; records are pruned on recording and sweeping. The spawn subscriber excludes initialization cause Loaded. Falling blocks are not swept, and unrelated new items inside the same space and time window can be removed. No player inventory or game mode is consulted by this path.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0137](../adr/0137-clear-block-columns-with-the-blade.md) Decision, "Use one `fillBlocks` per column, filtering `excludeTypes` and `excludeTags` with `ignoreChunkBoundErrors: true`."
- Basis: [0137](../adr/0137-clear-block-columns-with-the-blade.md) Decision, "Never sweep entities whose initialization cause is Loaded. Falling blocks may fall. No player or game mode is read, so cuts run in every game mode, Adventure included."
- Implementation: `src/domain/shared/keptBlocks.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bladeCuts.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/dropSweep.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/cutDrops.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/identifiers.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/blocks`
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bladeCuts.test.ts` checks filters, world bounds, failed columns, idempotence, and drop space/time limits; `tests/packs/laidTag.test.ts` checks laid tags. Scenarios `blade_cut_column`, `blade_cut_drops`, and `blade_cut_kept_ids` in `src/test-pack/scenarios/bladeCut.ts` exercise engine filtering, delayed drops, and resolvable kept IDs. ### Rising shaft

- Applies to: Bulldozer with raw input and a fully lowered blade, during takeoff or positive climb input in hover/submarine form.
- Rule: Independently of forward speed and pivot, probe the axis-aligned square of half extent `BULLDOZER_SPEC.footprint / 2`, starting at `floor(y + BULLDOZER_SPEC.carHeight)` and spanning `BULLDOZER_SPEC.shaft.reach` rows. If any probe reports solid, append fixed cut columns over the square expanded by `BULLDOZER_SPEC.shaft.margin` on every compass side. The square follows world axes rather than yaw. The expanded half extent is 3.675 blocks, so `shaftColumns` covers 8 or 9 columns per axis depending on the vehicle's fractional position, at most 9 by 9, each `BULLDOZER_SPEC.shaft.reach` rows high. It is recomputed each rising tick without persistent shaft state. Open air and water alone do not trigger it. A solid kept block can trigger repeated cuts while remaining protected. The same column filters, height clamps, and drop sweep apply.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0155](../adr/0155-cut-under-water-and-dig-a-shaft-when-rising.md) Decision, "The box follows the compass, not the yaw. It is cut every tick the probe finds an obstacle, so the hole follows the climb; it adds no per-vehicle state."
- Basis: [0155](../adr/0155-cut-under-water-and-dig-a-shaft-when-rising.md) Decision, "The forward strip and the shaft are concatenated into one cut list."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.ts` (`shaftCut`, `solidOverhead`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/blade.ts` (`shaftColumns`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts` (`shaft`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.test.ts` checks rising forms, obstacle reach, no-input and raised-blade refusals; `spec.test.ts` pins shaft tuning. Scenario `bulldozer_rise_shaft` checks a pocket and roof in `src/test-pack/scenarios/bulldozer.ts`. ### Wide blade

- Applies to: Bulldozer; `yasu_ride:wide_blade_switch` (Wide Blade), independently of blade lift.
- Rule: The driver switches normal/wide through the same tool router and cooldown as Blade Up/Down. Client-synced bool `yasu_ride:wide_blade` defaults false, is restored, and is resent on construction and resync. `bladeRule()` chooses `BULLDOZER_SPEC.blade` or `BULLDOZER_WIDE_BLADE`, whose only difference is doubled half width. Cut depth, height, gates, and lift stay the same. Every tick, even raised, the drive reports that rule's half width for ram; the core clamps it to the profile's `maxRamHalfWidth`, which is the wide limit, and the target query covers that cap. Paving reads the same selected width. The model draws exactly one of `blade` and `blade_wide`, both children of `blade_rig` with matching pivots. Both use `blade_cubes(half)`; the plate widens while fittings retain their size, bolts retain roughly their pitch, end plates move outward, and back rib count grows. Culling bounds include the wide blade. Placement footprint and clearance stay unchanged, so the wide blade may overlap nearby walls or placements. Holding the switch shows NORM or WIDE after the blade lift screens.

### Road paver input and gates

- Applies to: Bulldozer; `yasu_ride:road_paver_switch` (Road Paver), ground car and hover forms.
- Rule: The driver toggles the paver through the shared tool switch path. Server-only bool `yasu_ride:road_paver` defaults false, restores on rebuild, and resends on construction/resync. Its monitor states are OFF and ON. Paving requires the switch on, `raw !== undefined`, an accepted held block, and absolute speed strictly above `BULLDOZER_SPEC.paver.minSpeed`; reverse paving uses the opposite travel yaw. Blade lift does not gate paving; normal/wide width comes from `bladeRule()`. Infrastructure reads a rider's selected item once as `RiderHeld`: a bare yasu_ride tool ID and, when `BlockPermutation.resolve` succeeds for the item ID itself, that namespaced block ID. The core passes the driver's block, or `testHeld.block` without a driver, to the drive; passengers do not supply it. `isRoadBlock` rejects `KEPT_BLOCKS`, both add-on namespace prefixes, `REJECTED_SUFFIXES`, and `ROAD_REJECTED_BLOCKS` (gravity blocks, multi-cell blocks, listed support-dependent plants/crops, technical non-placeable blocks, and light blocks). Rails, torches, and ladders are not blanket-rejected. The adapter lays the block's default permutation, losing orientation and other nondefault states. No inventory consumption occurs in any game mode.

### Road cells and placement protection

- Applies to: Paver output for grounded car form and hover form only.
- Rule: All tuning is `BULLDOZER_SPEC.paver`. Ground reference is `feetRow(y, true) - 1`. Each candidate column probes solid before liquid, so waterlogged solids count as solid. A solid at reference plus one is paved there unless reference plus two is also solid, in which case it is skipped. Otherwise solid or liquid at reference is paved at reference; an open reference over solid one row down is filled at reference. Deeper open drops get a reference-row bridge only if liquid is found within `liquidSearchDepth` before a solid. The ground trailing strip runs from `-(bodyHalf + trail)` to `-bodyHalf` in travel coordinates. A support zone from `-bodyHalf` to `bodyHalf + lookAhead` contributes only picks classified as over liquid; duplicate columns prefer the trail pick. Hover uses `feetRow(y, false) - 1` and a trailing strip separated from `bodyHalf` by `flightGap`, laying only into open or liquid cells. Both use the blade half width and touching-cell selection. Takeoff, landing, submarine, and airborne car form emit nothing. The application applies `Pave { block, cells }` after cutting and before movement. `RoadPaver` resolves the default permutation and independently skips unloaded cells, cells already holding the road type, laid-tagged cells, `PLACE_KEPT_BLOCKS`, and camp space; each other cell is replaced without drops. Successful replacements enter the same drop sweep as cuts. Broader World Edit data-block protection and vehicle-space protection are not added by this adapter. Roads are ordinary blocks and are not registered as temporary laid cells.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0181](../adr/0181-a-road-paver-on-the-bulldozer.md) Decision, "The drive decides which cells qualify (rules 6 and 7); the adapter lays every emitted cell and only enforces protection"
- Basis: [0181](../adr/0181-a-road-paver-on-the-bulldozer.md) Decision, "Takeoff, landing, submarine, and car form off the ground lay nothing."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/paver.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/pave.ts`; `src/domain/shared/keptBlocks.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/roadPaver.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/dropSweep.ts`

### Cab monitor

- Applies to: Bulldozer profile opt-in `BULLDOZER_MONITOR`, visible to all viewers.
- Rule: The core reserves client-synced integer property `yasu_ride:monitor`, default 0, with range from blank to the count of `monitorScreens(BULLDOZER_MONITOR)`. Screen 0 draws no overlay. Tool/state order gives DOWN 1, UP 2, NORM 3, WIDE 4, OFF 5, ON 6. Only the Crew driver's held tool counts; without a driver the test-held item may supply it. An absent/unknown tool or unknown state blanks the monitor. Blade state is its target, so switching updates the screen before easing finishes; wide and paver states come from the drive's tool table. Every tick the core computes the screen and appends a changed value after drive commands through `SyncedValue`; it is not restored, and the first tick and resync resend it. `tools/monitor.py` builds a separate geometry, atlas, and render controller from `tools/monitor_screens.json` and the bulldozer layout in `gen_bulldozer.py`. Each top-level screen bone has one cube with only its down face textured toward seat 0, slightly ahead of the console. The property selects one screen. The overlay uses `entity_emissive`, alpha-zero glyph/icon texels, and an opaque background, and is drawn before the base controller's blended glass only when the property is positive. ### Track animation

- Applies to: Bulldozer track phase and wheel spin properties.
- Rule: Only car form advances accumulated track travel. Using `BULLDOZER_SPEC.tracks`, right travel adds `speed - degToRad(yawDelta) * halfGauge` and left travel adds `speed + degToRad(yawDelta) * halfGauge`; the yaw change is normalized across angle wrap. `trackPhase` is positive modulo three of `floor(travel / phaseStep)`, and `trackSpin` is positive modulo 360 of travel divided by `radius`, converted to rounded whole degrees. The resulting integer phase selects exactly one grouser bone per side, while spin rotates that side's idlers and sprocket. Outside car form travel freezes. Separate synced values send each phase/spin only when changed and resend after rebuild or failed delivery.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0136](../adr/0136-bulldozer.md) Decision, "Tracks run in car form only. Right travel advances by `speed - degToRad(yawDelta) * halfGauge`, left by `speed + degToRad(yawDelta) * halfGauge`."
