## Rules

### Spec table and profile

- Applies to: Sports boat, driven entity `yasu_ride:sportsboat`.
- Rule: The import-free frozen `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC` owns the boat driving limits, body and footprint, drag estimator, attitude, boarding aim and deck tuning. `SPORTSBOAT_MOTION`, the profile body, `SPORTSBOAT_DRAG`, `SPORTSBOAT_WATER`, `SPORTSBOAT_ATTITUDE`, `DECK_DRAFT`, `DECK.margin` and entity attitude ranges derive from that table. The profile separately defines seats, chosen boarding, headlight beam reaches and lanes, placement clearance and surfaces, and black default paint. The boat has no transforming forms; its drive requests neither an underwater rider effect nor a form camera switch. Seat order and driver choice are covered by [Seats and driver selection](seats-and-driver.md).
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0087](../adr/0087-sports-boat-spec-table.md) Decision, "It is frozen and imports nothing"; [0051](../adr/0051-sports-boat.md) Decision, "Store these in its own profile (0050)"

### Buoyancy and horizontal liquid drive

- Applies to: Undocked sports boat, afloat.
- Rule: `minecraft:buoyant` supplies flotation with gravity enabled and waves disabled. `waterDepthAt` bounds the depth query by `SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE.body.height`; `isAfloat` accepts a positive depth or `waterBelowAt` when the depth query is dry. The latter uses the below-feet offset defined in `waterBelowAt`. While afloat, `stepMediumMotion` drives horizontal velocity with the boat limits and drag compensation; `SPORTSBOAT_WATER.maxSpeed` and the normal forward target come from `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.maxSpeed`. Reverse uses `maxReverse` and `reverseAccel`. At normal depth the drive has zero vertical impulse; deep-liquid surfacing is the 0175 exception (see Open issues), outside this spec's equipment scope.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0051](../adr/0051-sports-boat.md) Decision, "Afloat, the script drives x and z in water with gravity left on and no vertical impulse."
- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`isAfloat`, `SPORTSBOAT_WATER`, `stepHull`), `src/domain/vehicle/motion/water.ts`
- Verification: `tests/packs/sportsboat.test.ts` "floats with minecraft:buoyant, gravity on and no waves"; `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.test.ts` "afloat at full throttle, rises to the boat's top speed above the SUV's and never exceeds it", "queries below the boat only when its depth query is dry". In-game scenarios `boat_float` and `boat_cruise` are defined in `src/test-pack/scenarios/sportsboat.ts`, not run here. Not verified: present BDS speed or owner driving feel (manual B4).

### Water drag estimate and speed checks

- Applies to: Undocked afloat sports boat; estimate retained when beached.
- Rule: `estimateWaterDrag` starts from `SPORTSBOAT_DRAG.initial` or the bounded stored estimate. It projects observed horizontal velocity onto `MotionState.mediumExpected`, skips samples at or below `SPORTSBOAT_DRAG.minGiven` and on a per-axis wall hit, clamps each sample to `.min` and `.max`, and averages with `.smoothing`. The updated estimate divides horizontal motion on the same tick; `Medium.wallDrag` keeps the pre-update estimate for the shared wall decision. A beached tick keeps the estimate. `boat_cruise` checks steady speed relative to `SPORTSBOAT_MOTION.maxSpeed` and turn-speed retention; these assertions are acceptance checks, not a current measurement.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0053](../adr/0053-estimate-buoyant-boat-water-drag.md) Decision, "Each sample is clamped to the bounds before it is averaged."; [0053](../adr/0053-estimate-buoyant-boat-water-drag.md) Decision, "So a tick is a drag sample or a wall hit, never both, and a collision is not taken for drag"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`estimateWaterDrag`, `currentWaterDrag`, `SPORTSBOAT_DRAG`), `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts`, `src/test-pack/scenarios/sportsboat.ts` (`boatDrag`, `boatCruise`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.test.ts` "clamps the sample before averaging rather than only clamping the result", "makes one wall decision per tick: a tick taken as a drag sample never lowers the speed, and a skipped one does", "carries the water estimate afloat and beached, and a higher estimate gives less velocity for the same target". `boat_drag` and `boat_cruise` exist; neither was run here. Not verified: reproducing the historical 0053 measurements on this revision.

### Seated rider fire protection

- Applies to: Sports boat riders and occupied boat deck seats, on any surface.
- Rule: The hull and shared deck-seat entity have `minecraft:fire_immune`. ### Beached reverse-only motion

- Applies to: Undocked boat when neither supported liquid sensing path says afloat.
- Rule: `beachedInput` clamps positive throttle to zero, keeps reverse throttle and steering, and disables creep and the handbrake. `stepMotion` uses the boat's `maxReverse` and `reverseAccel`, and an obstacle callback that always returns zero, so the scripted ground motion requests no step-up. Forward drive cannot propel it inland; reversing on flat land has no distance limit. Ground versus air friction follows the actual `onGround` snapshot.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0080](../adr/0080-beached-sports-boat-can-reverse.md) Decision, "A sports boat that is not afloat"; [0080](../adr/0080-beached-sports-boat-can-reverse.md) Decision, "It still never steps up a block, so a one-block bank stops it in either direction"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`beachedInput`, `stepHull`), `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts`

### Bow rise and signed roll

- Applies to: Undocked sports boat attitude; dock entry levels the hull.
- Rule: `stepTrim` targets `SPORTSBOAT_ATTITUDE.maxTrim` times the forward-speed gain divided by the injected acceleration limit, clamped to the unit interval only while afloat. It approaches the target with `trimRiseRate` and `trimSettleRate`; slowing, reversing, holding speed and being beached target level. `stepRoll` targets `maxRoll` times clamped heading change over turn rate and clamped signed speed over maximum speed. Its sign follows speed times yaw rate, so the same stick leans to the same side forward and in reverse. It approaches by `rollRate`, and targets level aground. Non-finite scale factors target level. Dock entry clears both angles. Synced values send changes and support a resend after failure; the always-on client attitude animation rotates `root` by minus trim on x and minus roll on z, about its generated aft waterline pivot.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0059](../adr/0059-sports-boat-wake-trim-and-roll.md) Decision, "The first tick always sends both, so a rebuilt vehicle clears what the entity kept from before `/reload`, as the SUV does with its bank"; [0061](../adr/0061-lift-the-bow-and-roll-further.md) Decision, "The attitude animation turns `root` by minus `trim` on x, so the bow lifts while the boat gains speed."; [0079](../adr/0079-roll-by-signed-speed.md) Decision, "Afloat, the roll target is `clamp(heading change / turnRate, -1, 1) * clamp(speed / maxSpeed, -1, 1) * maxRoll`, with the signed speed."; [0085](../adr/0085-halve-the-sports-boat-bow-rise.md) Decision, "The rates halve with it"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/attitude.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/entity.ts`, `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/sportsboat.anim.json`, `packs/yasu_ride_rp/models/entity/yasu_ride/sportsboat.geo.json`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/attitude.test.ts` "never rises while holding speed, slowing, reversing, or aground", "flipping the sign of the speed flips the roll"; `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.test.ts` "sends trim and roll only when they change", "restores a docked boat at its first location without dock or attitude commands"; `tests/packs/sportsboat.test.ts` "turns the root bone by minus the trim on x and minus the roll on z in one always-on animation". ### Stopped hold and departure

- Applies to: Sports boat with no raw driver input, afloat and sufficiently still.
- Rule: After `DOCK.ticks` consecutive afloat ticks with raw input absent and absolute internal speed below `DOCK.maxSpeed`, the drive attempts a hold. `holdHeight` scans `WATER_OFFSETS` from highest to lowest and uses the first matching liquid cell's top minus `DECK_DRAFT`; a dry scan postpones docking. Hold x/z and yaw come from the docking pose. The dock event disables collision and gravity; undock restores a full physics component. The adapter holds the same pose and clears velocity every tick. A stick magnitude above `DOCK.stick` on either driver axis undocks, requests deck boarding, and drives in that tick. A restored dock takes its current entity pose and heading. Passenger deck input alone does not depart; driver selection and deck-to-boat seat preservation are in [Seats and driver selection](seats-and-driver.md).
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0063](../adr/0063-walk-on-a-stopped-sports-boat.md) Decision, "While docked, the boat gets no impulse, and the script teleports it to the hold pose and clears its velocity every tick."; [0064](../adr/0064-dock-step.md) Decision, "A dock is present only while a drive holds its vehicle; a kind that never holds never returns one."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/deck.ts` (`holdHeight`), `src/domain/vehicle/motion/dock.ts` (`DOCK`, `heldStep`), `src/domain/vehicle/dock/dock.ts`, `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`holdAt`), `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.test.ts` "returns a complete dock matching its hold on every tick", "resets the still count for any input, so a driver never docks", "undocks on either axis above the threshold and runs normal motion that same tick", "restores a docked boat at its first location without dock or attitude commands". `src/test-pack/scenarios/sportsboat.ts` defines `boat_dock`, not run here. ### Walkable deck cells and deck seats

- Applies to: Docked sports boat.
- Rule: `floorCells` rotates cell centers into boat-local coordinates and covers centers inside any `DECK.parts` rectangle grown by `DECK.margin`. The highest overlapping part sets the surface. It floors the world top to a block row and rounds the fractional height to pixels, clamped by `PIXELS_PER_BLOCK`. `Dock.cells` requests invisible `deck_floor` permutations at those heights. `DockBlocks` places only into air or headlight light, persists the cells in `DECK_CELLS_PROPERTY`, preserves foreign replacements, and clears owned cells on departure or removal with retries for unloaded cells. Optional deck-seat spots follow profile seat positions; occupied boat seats are suppressed by the aggregate. `DeckSeats` saves seat IDs and ownership for restore, replaces missing seats, and removes seats when no longer wanted. There is no added safety rim; walking off the cell edge is possible.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0063](../adr/0063-walk-on-a-stopped-sports-boat.md) Decision, "A cell is covered when its center lies within a part grown by `DECK.margin = 0.3` blocks on every side"; [0064](../adr/0064-dock-step.md) Decision, "The aggregate still blanks `dock.seats[i]` while seat i has a rider, and still masks that seat's deck input."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/deck.ts` (`DECK`, `floorCells`), `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/kinds/camper/dockBlocks.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/deckSeats.ts`, `src/infrastructure/shared/laidCells.ts`, `packs/yasu_ride_bp/blocks/yasu_ride/deck_floor.block.json`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/deck.test.ts` "covers exactly the cell centers within a deck part grown by the margin", "rounds the highest covering surface to a collision height of 1 to 16 pixels"; `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/kinds/camper/dockBlocks.test.ts` "only places in air or light, saves only placed cells and preserves replacement blocks"; `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/deckSeats.test.ts` "restores seat ownership, replaces missing seats, moves drifted seats and clears on removal". ### Deck hit protection and repair

- Applies to: Boat hits from its stopped deck and player breaks of owned dock blocks.
- Rule: `standsOnDeck` tests cells intersecting the player's footprint at feet level and just below it. Foreign blocks are preserved; unloaded cells wait for a later check.
