- Applies to: Successful headlight switches by chase-camera drivers, including SUV, camper and bulldozer.
- Rule: After setting the headlights, show translated Headlights ON/OFF for the new state on the switching player's action bar only when the shared `RiderEffects` tracks that player's chase camera. Ceiling lights and tent lanterns receive no new feedback. Existing routing and light behavior stay unchanged.

### Headlight switch item and who may switch

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, and bulldozer, including a parked camper's wheel deck seat.
- Rule: The single-stack item `yasu_ride:headlight_switch` has an icon and translated name and an always-unlocked shapeless crafting-table recipe using a lever and glowstone dust. Its `minecraft:use_modifiers` supplies `use_duration` and `movement_modifier`, so it has an item-use behavior without slowing movement. A vehicle's selected driver may switch its headlights. In a parked camper, the recorded rider of wheel deck seat 0 may also switch them. The adapter resolves that deck seat through its saved owner and the vehicle's saved seat list; an orphan or stale seat does not resolve. Other passengers cannot switch headlights. A successful flip changes the entity property and plays `random.click` at the vehicle.
- Agreement: Accepted

### Switch events and cooldown

- Applies to: The shared headlight-switch route; vehicle, camper room, and tent targets.
- Rule: `registerSwitchRouter` handles `world.afterEvents.itemUse` immediately and claimed `world.beforeEvents.playerInteractWithEntity` and `world.beforeEvents.playerInteractWithBlock` through `system.run` on the next tick, checking player validity. The first target that claims the event receives it. Claimed entity presses are cancelled; claimed block presses are cancelled on every repeat, but only `isFirstEvent` schedules a switch. The vehicle target is asked before the tent target (and the separate Gas Lantern target). A rider's entity press reaches their own vehicle, including a resolved stand-in; a camper room occupant's press reaches that room's vehicle. Direct vehicle riders do not claim block presses through this target. A headlight use by an eligible driver calls the vehicle's `SwitchCooldown` with `SPEC.headlightSwitchCooldown`, which aliases `LIGHT_SWITCH_COOLDOWN_TICKS`. Every admitted use restarts the idle interval even when it does not flip. A rejected non-driver does not consume that headlight cooldown. The cooldown is held by the vehicle aggregate, not persisted on the entity.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0020](../adr/0020-headlights.md) Decision, "that interaction is cancelled so it does nothing else"; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Decision, "A burst of repeated uses from one touch flips once; two taps need half a second between them."; [0115](../adr/0115-camper-ceiling-lights.md) Decision, "That press is cancelled, so the switch never also opens a door or the cabinet."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/shared/switchRouter.ts` (`registerSwitchRouter`, `switchNextTick`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/headlights.ts` (`vehicleSwitchTarget`); `src/infrastructure/bootstrap.ts` (`laterSwitchTargets`); `src/domain/shared/switchCooldown.ts` (`SwitchCooldown.tryUse`, `LIGHT_SWITCH_COOLDOWN_TICKS`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts` (`SPEC.headlightSwitchCooldown`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`flipHeadlightSwitch`)

### Headlight state and persistence

- Applies to: All four vehicle entities, in every driving form and while parked or docked.
- Rule: `yasu_ride:headlights` is a client-synced bool with default false in each server entity definition. The switch reads and inverts that property; the drive loop reads it to request road light cells, and client render conditions read it to select the lit overlay. A new vehicle starts off. Entity property persistence retains the state through `/reload` and a server restart; the switch cooldown itself starts fresh with a rebuilt aggregate. Headlights are independent of the camper's ceiling-light property.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0020](../adr/0020-headlights.md) Decision, "so it survives `/reload` and restarts. A placed vehicle starts with them off"; [0149](../adr/0149-route-the-parked-camper-wheel-seat-to-headlights.md) Decision, "Headlights and ceiling lights keep their separate cooldowns."

### Road light block positions and levels

- Applies to: Each loaded vehicle while its headlight property is true.
- Rule: `Vehicle.headlightCells` uses its kind's `VehicleProfile.beams` and current yaw. `headlightCells` floors the world x/z of every reach and lateral lane, with y `floor(location.y) + 1`; it follows the vehicle's height, not the terrain under a distant cell. Coincident cells are deduplicated with the brightest level. SUV and camper use their own `SUV_SPEC.headlightReach` and `CAMPER_SPEC.headlightReach` on the center lane. The sports boat uses the two lanes and reach array defined in `SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE.beams`. The bulldozer uses the center lane, `BULLDOZER_SPEC.headlightReach`, and `BULLDOZER_SPEC.headlightLevels`. Patterns without levels use `FULL_LIGHT_LEVEL`. The drive loop requests no road light cells when headlights are off. Flooring diagonal positions affects spacing; it does not adjust reach to guarantee a minimum light level between cells.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0020](../adr/0020-headlights.md) Decision, "one block above the ground it stands on."; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Consequences, "On a slope, the far cells can end up inside the ground, where no light is placed, or high above a dip."; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Consequences, "one road cell between them gets level 9, not 10."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/lighting/headlights.ts` (`headlightCells`, `FULL_LIGHT_LEVEL`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`headlightCells`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/profile.ts` (`SUV_PROFILE.beams`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` (`SUV_SPEC.headlightReach`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/profile.ts` (`CAMPER_PROFILE.beams`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts` (`CAMPER_SPEC.headlightReach`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts` (`SPORTSBOAT_PROFILE.beams`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/profile.ts` (`BULLDOZER_PROFILE.beams`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts` (`BULLDOZER_SPEC.headlightReach`, `headlightLevels`)

### Road light placement, clearing, and saved cells

- Applies to: Road light cells managed by `HeadlightBlocks`.
- Rule: Each tick, the adapter diffs requested cells and their levels against the cells it recorded. It lays `lightBlockId(level)` only into air, skips camp space, and records only successful placements. It clears an unwanted cell only if its current block is a light block, preserving player replacements. An unavailable clear (`getBlock` throws or returns undefined) remains pending for retry in the current entry. `yasu_ride:lit_cells` stores the JSON cell list with levels on the vehicle; a saved cell without a level is read as `FULL_LIGHT_LEVEL`, and malformed entries are discarded. A dimension change attempts to clear the old entry, then replaces it with a new-dimension entry, so unavailable old-dimension cells are not retained. Breaking or losing a known vehicle clears its tracked cells, with `clearGone` retrying pending clears. An entity removed before its saved cells are first read can leave lights behind. Unlike dock lights, wanted road lights are not periodically checked for loss while they remain in the recorded set. Shared light cells have no per-block ownership count. Light blocks are treated as air by movement and vehicle-space checks.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0020](../adr/0020-headlights.md) Decision, "It only places a light block in air, and only clears a cell that still holds a light block, so it never removes a block a player built"; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Consequences, "Clearing now keeps such a cell for a later tick in both cases"; [0020](../adr/0020-headlights.md) Decision, "Light blocks count as air for the step-up look-ahead (0004) and for placing a vehicle (0008)"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/headlightBlocks.ts` (`HeadlightBlocks.shine`, `update`, `clearGone`, `savedCells`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`LIT_CELLS_PROPERTY`); `src/domain/vehicle/lighting/headlights.ts` (`relight`, `litCellKey`); `src/infrastructure/shared/lightBlocks.ts` (`isLightBlock`, `lightBlockId`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts`
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/headlightBlocks.test.ts` "lights the wanted air cells and turns them back into air when the headlights go off", "clears the saved cells and their levels after a reload", "clears the light of a vehicle that is no longer loaded and keeps the light of a loaded one (ADR 0038)", "skips a wanted camp cell and lights the others (ADR 0129)". Scenario `headlights_follow_and_clear` exists in `src/test-pack/catalog.ts`; it sets headlight state without using a player's item. Not verified here: that scenario, actual restart cleanup, overlapping beams, or device light propagation.

### Ceiling lights routing in the parked camper

- Applies to: Players inside a parked camper, including riders of its vehicle and deck seats.
- Rule: The vehicle target first tries headlights for a direct vehicle rider or a resolved camper deck-seat rider, then tries that vehicle's ceiling lights if headlights refuse. Both the selected driver and recorded wheel deck-seat rider are refused ceiling lights, including when their headlight cooldown rejects the use. Other occupants can switch ceiling lights only while parked with feet inside `insideRoom`. For a player without a resolved vehicle, the target finds the room from the dock-laid cell below their floored feet, then the feet cell. `SwitchCeilingLights.canSwitch` ignores cooldown when deciding whether to take over room block or vehicle presses. The wheel deck seat's block press is also claimed for headlights. Roof, exit, and other decks do not qualify merely because a dock cell exists there. That exclusion covers block and vehicle presses only: item use is claimed wherever a dock cell gives a room, including the roof and exit, and there the use consumes the ceiling-light cooldown but switches nothing because the feet are outside `insideRoom`. The ceiling lights have a separate vehicle-wide `SwitchCooldown` using `SPEC.headlightSwitchCooldown`, shared by occupants; non-driver uses entering `switchCeilingLights` consume it before the drive checks reachability.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0115](../adr/0115-camper-ceiling-lights.md) Decision, "The room is the vehicle that laid the block under the player's feet, or the cell of their feet."; [0115](../adr/0115-camper-ceiling-lights.md) Decision, "Each use restarts the idle time, as in 0024."; [0149](../adr/0149-route-the-parked-camper-wheel-seat-to-headlights.md) Decision, "The wheel seat rider cannot switch the ceiling lights."

### Ceiling light blocks and state

- Applies to: Parked camper room cells and the `ceiling_lights` model bone.
- Rule: `CEILING_LIGHTS` defines the aisle cells and their layer; `ceilingLightCells` transforms them with the parked pose and omits cells occupied by open storage-door plates. A plate occupying a light cell carries the lights' lit state instead. With ceiling lights on, `CamperDrive` requests the remaining dock cells as type `light`, mapped to `LIGHT_BLOCK`. `DockBlocks` saves them with room cells in `yasu_ride:deck_cells`, preserves replacements, retains unavailable clears, and rechecks loss at `DOCK_RECHECK_TICKS`. Each successful switch emits `SWITCH_CLICK`. The panels hang under the ceiling liner over these cells in a `ceiling_lights` child of `body`, with `dome` lenses and opaque rims. Their appearance follows the separate render passes below.

### SUV lamp bones and detail regions

- Applies to: SUV headlights, fog lamps, front marker lenses, tail lamps, and third brake light.
- Rule: `headlights` and `tail_lights` are body-child lamp bones hidden in the base and shown in `.lit` by `yasu_ride:headlights`.
