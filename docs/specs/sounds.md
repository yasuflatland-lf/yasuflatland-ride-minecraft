## Rules

### Synthesized clips and assets

- Applies to: All sound families in this scope.
- Rule: `pnpm sounds` runs the `sounds` command in `package.json`. The generator uses only the Python standard library and synthesizes the clips without copying recordings; the two recorded resort show sounds below are the only exception and the generator does not write them. `write` writes mono 16-bit PCM WAVs at `RATE` into `packs/yasu_ride_rp/sounds/yasu_ride/`. Each family has its own seeded `random.Random` instance; engine and push share one bulldozer instance, and hover and hover start share another. Changing one family's synthesis leaves the other families' random sequences unchanged. Regeneration is reproducible with the generator and environment held fixed.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "No recording is used; the references only set the character:"; [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Decision, "`tools/gen_sounds.py` also writes `sonar`, a 4 s loop from its own seeded generator, so the other sounds stay byte for byte the same."; [0060](../adr/0060-sports-boat-engine-sound.md) Decision, "The boat's generator has its own seed, so the SUV's sounds stay byte for byte the same"; [0140](../adr/0140-bulldozer-sounds.md) Decision, "Append the new writes after the shower, with independent seeds 140 (engine and push), 141 (tracks), and 142 (hydraulics)."
- Implementation: `package.json` (`scripts.sounds`); `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`RATE`, `OUT`, `write`, family-specific `random.Random` instances); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/sounds/yasu_ride/`
- Verification: Generator, command, and committed WAV headers inspected. A one-off regeneration with `python3 -I tools/gen_sounds.py` into a scratch directory produced all 38 clips in this scope (plus the excluded shower clip) byte-for-byte identical to the committed files. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts`, `tests/packs/sportsboat.test.ts`, and `tests/packs/bulldozer.test.ts` check referenced files and durations, not regeneration equality. Not verified: byte stability across Python versions or platforms; no automated test compares regenerated bytes.

### Sound definitions

- Applies to: Every vehicle sound definition in this scope.
- Rule: `sound_definitions.json` assigns every definition category `neutral`, a per-clip volume, and `min_distance` and `max_distance`. The file defines the hearing distances for SUV/camper engine, hover and hover start, sonar, boat engine, bulldozer engine and push, tracks, and hydraulics. Sonar has the farthest maximum distance; hydraulics has the shortest and a smaller minimum distance. Boat engine volume rises with level. Bulldozer engine, push, and track volumes rise with level, and each push level is louder than its corresponding travel level.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "`sound_definitions.json` defines `mycar.engine_<n>` (volume 0.5 rising to 0.7, heard up to 24 blocks), `mycar.hover_<n>` (0.14 rising to 0.22, up to 32 blocks), and `mycar.hover_start` (0.14) in the `neutral` category"; [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Decision, "heard up to 48 blocks, farther than the engine (24) and the air (32), as sonar carries in water"; [0060](../adr/0060-sports-boat-engine-sound.md) Decision, "The volume rises from 0.5 to 0.78 across the levels, audible up to 32 blocks"; [0140](../adr/0140-bulldozer-sounds.md) Decision, "Every definition uses `"category": "neutral"`."
- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_rp/sounds/sound_definitions.json` (`sound_definitions`)
- Verification: All definition volumes, categories, and distances inspected against the source ADRs. `tests/packs/sportsboat.test.ts` "loops each level's own clip for exactly its length, louder as the revs rise" checks volume ordering; `tests/packs/bulldozer.test.ts` "increases volumes by speed and makes pushing louder than travel" checks ordering, exact volumes, categories, and distances for bulldozer sounds. Not verified: perceived loudness or hearing distance in game; the tests do not validate Bedrock attenuation.

### Loop animations last as long as their clip

- Applies to: Engine, hover, sonar, push, and track sound animations.
- Rule: Each looping sound animation has `loop: true`, emits its matching effect at keyframe `0.0`, and sets `animation_length` to its WAV duration. Durations come from `ENGINE_LOOP_S`, `BOAT_LOOP_S`, `SONAR_LOOP_S`, `BULLDOZER_ENGINE_LOOP_S`, `BULLDOZER_TRACK_LOOP_S`, and the local hover length in the generator. The animations repeat the complete clip. Hover start and hydraulics are one-shots. Switching a level changes which animation starts clips; it does not provide a stop command for an already playing sound.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "Each level is a looping animation as long as its clip: 1 s for the engine and the hover levels"; [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Decision, "a loop as long as the clip (4 s), as the engine plays only with a rider."; [0060](../adr/0060-sports-boat-engine-sound.md) Decision, "`tools/gen_sounds.py` writes `boat_engine_0` to `boat_engine_7`: 1 s loops at 6, 9, 13, 17, 22, 27, 32, and 38 engine cycles per second (720 to 4560 rpm)"; [0140](../adr/0140-bulldozer-sounds.md) Decision, "Each sound animation fires its matching effect at 0.0; engine and push animations loop for 4.0 s and track animations for 1.0 s, matching their clips; the hydraulics animation runs once for 0.9 s."
- Implementation: `tools/gen_sounds.py` (loop duration constants, `hover`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/suv.anim.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/camper.anim.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/sportsboat.anim.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/bulldozer.anim.json`
- Verification: All four animation files and WAV durations inspected. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "loops each sound animation exactly as long as its clip" checks the SUV; the sports boat test "loops each level's own clip for exactly its length, louder as the revs rise" and bulldozer test "matches sound animation lengths to their clips" cover those kinds. Camper sound durations were inspected directly; these tests do not independently compare every camper loop to a WAV. Not verified: audible seams or overlap after a level change in game.

### Ground engine of the SUV and camper

- Applies to: SUV and camper in `car` form with at least one rider.
- Rule: Each client entity's `scripts.animate` selects exactly one of six `engine_sound_0` through `engine_sound_5` entries by `query.ground_speed`. Each entry requires `query.has_rider && query.property('yasu_ride:form') == 'car'`. The speed ranges in the client files are contiguous from rest to infinity; no engine level starts without that guard. Both kinds map `engine_0` through `engine_5` to `yasu_ride.engine_0` through `yasu_ride.engine_5`. `engine` synthesizes single-cylinder diesel pulses using `ENGINE_FIRING_RATES`, `EXHAUST`, `KNOCK`, and `CLANK`; separate clips make pitch step with speed rather than glide continuously.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "The client entity plays exactly one engine level while `query.has_rider` and `mycar:flight` is `ground`, picked by `query.ground_speed`: below 1, 1–4.5, 4.5–8, 8–11.5, 11.5–15, and from 15 blocks/s."; [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Consequences, "The pitch steps between levels instead of gliding; when the speed crosses a bound, the old clip finishes under the new one for up to its length"; [0073](../adr/0073-hover-sound-on-a-riderless-vehicle.md) Context, "The engine levels are not in the form controller: the client entities `suv.ce.json` and `camper.ce.json` play `engine_sound_0` to `engine_sound_5` only while `query.has_rider` and `yasu_ride:form` is `car`."
- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/suv.ce.json` (`scripts.animate`, `sound_effects`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/camper.ce.json` (`scripts.animate`, `sound_effects`); `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`engine`, `ENGINE_FIRING_RATES`, `EXHAUST`, `KNOCK`, `CLANK`)
- Verification: Both clients' six conditions, mappings, and bands inspected. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "rumbles the engine on the ground at a pitch that rises with the speed" reads only the SUV and uses `tests/support/soundLevels.ts` (`speedRanges`, `expectContiguous`); it checks guards, continuity, and effect names, not measured pitch. `tests/packs/camper.test.ts` "resolves every client sound effect to an existing sound definition" checks camper mappings, not its engine conditions. Not verified: the SUV/camper engine mix in game; no manual row names this check except the general regression check B22 in the Bulldozer section of `docs/manual-test.md`.

### Hover start and hover rush

- Applies to: SUV and camper while their form controllers fold the wheels and hold the hover state.
- Rule: `wheels_deploy` plays `hover_start` at keyframe `0.0` as the wheels start folding. The generator's `hover_start` swells in using the slowest `HOVER_AIR_BANDS` band. The `hover` state plays `wheels_folded`, `hover_glow`, and exactly one of five `hover_sound_0` through `hover_sound_4` levels selected by contiguous `query.ground_speed` bands in the form controllers. Each level maps to the shared `yasu_ride.hover_N` clip. `hover` synthesizes rushing air from whole-cycle sine noise using `HOVER_AIR_BANDS`; it has no engine howl or turbo layer.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "`hover_start`: 1 s of the slowest level's air swelling in"; [0030](../adr/0030-engine-and-hover-sounds.md) Decision, "`wheels_deploy` plays `hover_start` as the wheels start to fold, and the `hover` state of the flight controller (0022) plays exactly one hover level: below 2, 2–6, 6–10, 10–14, and from 14 blocks/s."
- Implementation: `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`hover`, `hover_start`, `HOVER_AIR_BANDS`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.ac.json` (`hover`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/camper.ac.json` (`hover`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/suv.anim.json` (`wheels_deploy`, `hover_sound_*`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/camper.anim.json` (`wheels_deploy`, `hover_sound_*`)
- Verification: Controllers, animation keyframes, and generator inspected. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "swells as the wheels fold, and rushes while hovering at a pitch that rises with the speed" checks SUV effects and contiguous bands. `tests/packs/camper.test.ts` compares `camperForm` with `suvForm` and checks the deploy effect at `0.0` in "folds the wheels with the SUV's rotation and lifts them 2.7 px below the floor". Not verified: perceived pitch steps, air balance, or the start swell in game; no dedicated SUV/camper hover listening row is recorded.

### Hover sound without a rider

- Applies to: SUV and camper in their controllers' `hover` state.
- Rule: Hover level conditions read speed only and have no `query.has_rider` guard. They continue selecting a level when the last rider leaves. The state persists until `yasu_ride:gear_down` sends the controller to `retract`; no new hover clip starts from that state afterward. Gear-down timing comes from `SUV_SPEC.gearDownHeight` and `CAMPER_SPEC.gearDownHeight`, rather than a sound-specific distance. Engine and sonar retain their rider guards. A passenger also makes `query.has_rider` true. The sports boat has no hover sound.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0073](../adr/0073-hover-sound-on-a-riderless-vehicle.md) Decision, "The `hover` state of the SUV and camper form controllers plays its hover level whether or not anyone rides. Its conditions do not test `query.has_rider`"; [0073](../adr/0073-hover-sound-on-a-riderless-vehicle.md) Decision, "The hover sound ends as before: the gear comes down and the controller leaves `hover` for `retract` (0033)."
- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.ac.json` (`hover`, `retract`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/camper.ac.json` (`hover`, `retract`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` (`SUV_SPEC.gearDownHeight`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts` (`CAMPER_SPEC.gearDownHeight`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/sportsboat.ce.json` (`sound_effects`)
- Verification: Conditions and transitions inspected. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "%s keeps the hover sound on a riderless vehicle (ADR 0073)" checks the SUV, camper, and bulldozer for absence of `has_rider`; the bulldozer's separate controller is described below. Not verified: riderless descent audibility or completion of an already playing clip in game.

### Sonar under water

- Applies to: SUV and camper in the `submarine` controller state.
- Rule: Only `submarine` selects `submarine_sound`, guarded by `query.has_rider`. It loops the shared `sonar` effect independently of speed; dive and surface animations have no sound effects. `sonar` synthesizes a ping at the start, detuned reflections, a later return, and low rumble using the `SONAR_*` constants. It folds a two-loop ping tail onto one loop. The ping begins near `SONAR_PING_HZ` and fades before the loop ends. No add-on underwater filter is applied. Dismounting prevents new sonar clips; the controller does not stop a clip already playing.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Decision, "The `submarine` state of the form controller plays `submarine_sound` while `query.has_rider`, a loop as long as the clip (4 s), as the engine plays only with a rider. The `dive` and `surface` transitions stay silent"; [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Consequences, "The ping repeats every 4 s whatever the speed; nothing in the sound follows the speed under water"; [0041](../adr/0041-sonar-sound-under-water.md) Consequences, "The add-on applies no underwater filter of its own"
- Implementation: `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`sonar`, `SONAR_*`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/suv.ac.json` (`submarine`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animation_controllers/yasu_ride/camper.ac.json` (`submarine`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/suv.anim.json` (`submarine_sound`, `dive`, `surface`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/camper.anim.json` (`submarine_sound`, `dive`, `surface`)
- Verification: Generator and both controllers inspected. `tests/packs/resourcePack.test.ts` "loops the sonar only in the submarine form while someone rides (ADR 0041)" checks the SUV state and other entries; "starts the sonar loop with a ping that fades before the loop ends (ADR 0041)" compares Goertzel magnitudes at the ping frequency. `tests/packs/camper.test.ts` compares form controllers and checks sound-free dive/submerged-pose/surface animations in "dives, stays submerged, and surfaces in the deploy, folded, and retract poses without sound"; the separate sonar animation supplies the submerged sound. Not verified: in-game sonar listening and dismount behavior; manual-test row 40 has empty result cells.

### Sports boat engine

- Applies to: Sports boat with at least one rider.
- Rule: `scripts.animate` selects exactly one of eight `engine_sound_0` through `engine_sound_7` levels with `query.has_rider` and contiguous speed bands in `sportsboat.ce.json`. Effects `engine_0` through `engine_7` resolve to `yasu_ride.boat_engine_0` through `yasu_ride.boat_engine_7`. `boat_engine` synthesizes cross-plane V8 bank pulses, wet exhaust bubbles, and water noise using `BOAT_CYCLE_RATES`, `V8_FIRING_ORDER`, `V8_PIPES`, `BUBBLE_SHARE`, `BUBBLE_PITCH`, `WATER_BAND`, and `WATER_GAIN`. Both clip selection and the resulting volume steps follow speed, not throttle; pressing forward against a shore selects idle while speed stays in the idle band. The boat has neither hover nor sonar effects.
- Agreement: Accepted

Holding full throttle against a shore sounds like idle"
- Implementation: `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`boat_engine`, boat synthesis constants); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/sportsboat.ce.json` (`scripts.animate`, `sound_effects`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/sportsboat.anim.json` (`engine_sound_*`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/sounds/sound_definitions.json` (`yasu_ride.boat_engine_*`)
- Verification: Synthesis parameters, conditions, mappings, and definitions inspected against 0060. `tests/packs/sportsboat.test.ts` describe "sports boat engine sound (ADR 0060)" checks eight levels, rider guards, contiguous ranges, clip mappings, durations, and rising volume. ### Bulldozer engine, push, and track

- Applies to: Bulldozer in `car` form with at least one rider.
- Rule: All engine, push, and track entries start with `query.has_rider && query.property('yasu_ride:form') == 'car'`. Idle `engine_sound_0` plays below the first moving band regardless of blade position. In the five moving bands, exactly one travel `engine_sound_1` through `engine_sound_5` requires blade >= 0.05, or one `push_sound_1` through `push_sound_5` requires blade < 0.05; one matching `track_sound_1` through `track_sound_5` also plays, independently of the blade. The bands are defined in `bulldozer.ce.json` and tested against 0136's rescaled bands. Push tests blade and speed, not whether anything was cut, drive direction, or steering; a stationary pivot stays in idle without tracks. The animate order is form, drive, hydraulics, engine levels, push levels, then track levels. Dedicated clips use `BULLDOZER_IDLE_CYCLES`, `BULLDOZER_TRAVEL_CYCLES`, `BULLDOZER_PUSH_CYCLES`, and `BULLDOZER_TRACK_LINKS`. The engine has a lead-cylinder thump, low exhaust resonances, and two low-pass stages; push cycles are slower than travel at the same level. Tracks combine clank, thud, squeal, and roller rumble. `quiet_seam` rotates engine, push, and track samples to a quiet start.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0136](../adr/0136-bulldozer.md) Decision, "Halve the engine and hover sound bands: engine edges 0.5/2.25/4/5.75/7.5 and hover edges 1/3/5/7 blocks/s."; [0140](../adr/0140-bulldozer-sounds.md) Decision, "Exactly one engine level plays: idle below 0.5 blocks/s, otherwise one of five travel levels when blade >= 0.05, or five push levels when blade < 0.05."; [0140](../adr/0140-bulldozer-sounds.md) Decision, "Exactly one track level plays in each moving speed band, independently of the blade."
- Implementation: `packs/yasu_ride_rp/entity/yasu_ride/bulldozer.ce.json` (`scripts.animate`, `sound_effects`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/animations/yasu_ride/bulldozer.anim.json` (`engine_sound_*`, `push_sound_*`, `track_sound_*`); `tools/gen_sounds.py` (`bulldozer_engine`, `bulldozer_track`, `quiet_seam`, `loop_lowpass`, `BULLDOZER_*`)
- Verification: Conditions, order, synthesis numbers, and WAV sizes inspected against 0136's sound clause and 0140. `tests/packs/bulldozer.test.ts` "rumbles the engine at levels rescaled to a 9 blocks/s top speed" checks bands, guards, blade clauses, and order; "maps bulldozer sounds to dedicated clips while preserving hover and sonar" checks mappings; "keeps the engine low and its exhaust tone at twice the firing rate (ADR 0140)" checks spectral relationships; "increases volumes by speed and makes pushing louder than travel" checks volume ordering. Not verified here: audible seams, sound character, or overlap in game. ADR 0140 records owner listening approval, but Bulldozer rows B18-B20 and regression row B22 in `docs/manual-test.md` have empty result cells.

### Bulldozer blade hydraulics

- Applies to: Bulldozer blade motion, including without a rider and outside `car` form.
- Rule: The unconditional `hydraulics` animate entry runs its own controller. The controller has no rider, speed, or form condition.
- Agreement: Proposed

- Verification: Controller, animation, and synthesis values inspected. `tests/packs/bulldozer.test.ts` "plays hydraulics once when the blade leaves an endpoint" checks the complete controller; "matches sound animation lengths to their clips" checks the one-shot and compares its length with the blade travel time. ### Recorded resort show sounds

- Applies to: The Toilet flush and the Washstand running water.
- Rule: `toilet_flush.ogg` and `washstand_water.ogg` in `packs/yasu_ride_rp/sounds/yasu_ride/` are recordings the owner made and confirmed on 2026-11-11 may be shipped (source not stated), not synthesis; `tools/gen_sounds.py` stays synthesis-only and never writes or overwrites them. Each is mono Ogg Vorbis, the first 5.0 s of its source with a 0.4 s linear fade-out baked in, because the engine cannot fade a sound. The flush comes from `recording-00_08.mp3` (8.21 s) and the running water from `recording-00_06.mp3` (6.84 s); the source files are not committed.
