### Switching and persistent state

- Applies to: Driver use of the Icebreaker Switch and reconstruction of the sports boat drive.
- Rule: `Vehicle.switchTool` accepts only the crew driver and a declared monitor tool supported by the drive. A valid use advances to the next declared state, wrapping from `on` to `off`. All monitor tools on that vehicle share an idle cooldown `SPEC.toolSwitchCooldown`, separate from the light switch; even a cooldown-rejected use restarts the idle interval. `yasu_ride:icebreaker` is a bool, default false, with `client_sync: false`. The drive restores only literal true as ON; fresh or other restored values mean OFF. State changes emit a property command, and resync resends it. The entity contract lists `icebreaker` among restored properties. The server-only `forceTool` hook bypasses driver and cooldown checks but still rejects unknown items and invalid states.
- Agreement: Proposed

### Cut trigger, probe strip and recut limit

- Applies to: A driving sports boat tick before producing icebreaker cut columns.
- Rule: All five conditions must hold: icebreaker ON; defined driver input; `afloat` true for water or lava; `toDriveInput(raw).throttle > 0`; and a blocked bow. In ordinary play input comes from the driver; the server-only test input can substitute without one. A sideways creep, reverse, neutral input, beached hull or clear bow does not cut. `bowBlocked` checks cell centers under the yaw-rotated strip from `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.probeNear` to `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.ramFront + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.probeAhead`, with half width `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.ramHalfWidth`, at either height in `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.probeHeights`. Touching cells count. A first blocked tick cuts immediately, without a speed gate. A still-blocked bow cuts again only after horizontal movement reaches `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.recutDistance` or elapsed ticks reach `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.recutTicks`. Any tick failing the combined trigger clears the last-cut record.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0171](../adr/0171-sports-boat-equipment-and-icebreaker-mode.md) Decision 6, "Cut on a tick only when all five conditions hold"; [0171](../adr/0171-sports-boat-equipment-and-icebreaker-mode.md) Decision 6, "Sideways creep, reverse and an unblocked bow do not trigger a cut."; [0171](../adr/0171-sports-boat-equipment-and-icebreaker-mode.md) Decision 6, "A speed gate like the bulldozer's would never cut a boat already stopped against the ice, so the repeat is limited instead"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`icebreakerCut`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/icebreaker.ts` (`bowBlocked`, `recutDue`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/cutArea.ts` (`rectCells`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`tick`)

### Cut volume and kept blocks

- Applies to: Columns emitted by `icebreakerColumns` and applied by `BladeCuts`.
- Rule: The yaw-rotated rectangle extends from `-SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.sternReach` to `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.ramFront + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.margin`, with half width `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.ramHalfWidth + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.margin`. The stern reach bounds the shipped geometry, including rotation and inflation, rounded up to a hundredth of a block; there is no extra stern margin. Each column begins at `hullFloorRow(feetY) = floor(feetY + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.floorEpsilon)` and ends below the exclusive upper boundary `ceil(feetY + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.height + SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.margin)`. No row below that floor is cut. Shared `rectCells` includes touching boundary cells; `boxColumns` supplies a fixed height. `BladeCuts` clips columns to world height and fills with air, excluding `KEPT_BLOCKS` (air, both forms of water and lava, and the listed item holders and other protected blocks) and `LAID_BLOCK_TAG`. Other blocks, including ice, become air. The shared drop sweep records successful fills and removes matching recent drops through the cut-drop subscription. Per-column failures are caught so other columns can continue.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0171](../adr/0171-sports-boat-equipment-and-icebreaker-mode.md) Decision 7, "Reuse the blade's rectangle cell selection, including its touching-cell boundary convention."; [0171](../adr/0171-sports-boat-equipment-and-icebreaker-mode.md) Decision 7, "Preserve water, flowing water, lava, flowing lava, item holders and air through `KEPT_BLOCKS`, and laid blocks through `LAID_BLOCK_TAG`."; [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 1, "Nothing below the hull floor row is removed, so the bed under and around the hull stays. ### Surfacing lift

- Applies to: Afloat sports boat steps, independently of icebreaker mode and driver presence.
- Rule: The drive combines `ctx.waterAt` and optional `ctx.lavaAt` into `liquidAt`, then samples contiguous liquid depth using `waterDepthAt` bounded by the profile body height plus its query allowance. `surfaceLift(depth)` is zero at or below `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.surfacing.maxDepth`; above it the lift is `(depth - maxDepth) * gain`, capped at `maxLift`, with all three values from `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.icebreaker.surfacing`. The impulse is continuous at the threshold. Beached steps have zero vertical impulse; already held dock steps return `heldStep` with zero impulse. A step entering docking still uses the afloat lift expression. Other buoyancy and gravity remain engine behavior.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 3, "the script adds an upward impulse `surfaceLift(depth)`, capped at `maxLift` and continuous at the threshold."; [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 3, "A trench can flood a hull after the mode was switched off."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/surfacing.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`stepHull`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/water.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/dock.ts` (`heldStep`)

### Dry channel head and beached behavior

- Applies to: The sports boat reaching a freshly cut channel, with either mode selected.
- Rule: `isAfloat` uses positive sampled liquid depth or liquid below the feet at the boat's current center. It does not test the channel head ahead. If both checks are dry, the beached input clamps throttle to reverse or neutral, disables creep and never steps up; forward input cannot cut. Reverse and steering remain available. When those liquid checks become true again, afloat movement and eligible cutting resume. The cutter creates a level tunnel starting at the hull floor, does not build a ramp and does not place water. OFF emits no cut, so an unremoved wall remains a collision obstacle.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 2, "The icebreaker digs a tunnel at hull height."; [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 4, "**Mode OFF** cuts nothing; the wall stops the boat."; [0175](../adr/0175-keep-the-icebreaker-cut-above-the-hull-floor.md) Decision 5, "When water floods the cell it is afloat again. Reverse always works (0080)."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts` (`isAfloat`, `beachedInput`, `stepHull`, `icebreakerCut`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/icebreaker.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/water.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.test.ts` covers beached forward blocking, reverse and steering; `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/icebreaker.test.ts` checks no cut while beached or OFF. ## Open issues

Where an ADR and the implementation differ, this spec states the implementation and lists the difference here. Explicit supersessions by 0174, 0175 and 0178 resolve the old group, box and downward-margin rules; they are not unresolved differences.

- ADR 0178 Decision 16 says `MonitorRule.group` becomes the fully qualified group ID; the implementation still sets the sports boat's group to bare `sportsboat_equipment` (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/equipment.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0171 Decision 4 says the group test compares monitor tools with the group minus placement-gift items, and ADR 0178 Decision 16 instead calls for tool containment; the implementation checks the sports boat catalog against a literal single-switch group without comparing it with `SPORTSBOAT_MONITOR.tools`, while the containment test exists only for the bulldozer (`tests/packs/sportsboatEquipment.test.ts`, `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/monitor.test.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0171 Decision 3 says the monitor uses the same pixel map and its dash-placement addendum says the atlas uses the shared item icon; the implementation shares the map but changes the near-black deck and rail colors and the alpha for emissive display (`tools/gen_sportsboat.py`, `MONITOR_ICON_COLORS`; `tests/packs/sportsboatMonitor.test.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR to record the palette adjustment.
- ADR 0171 Consequences / Dash monitor placement gives rideable seat 0 as `[-0.38, 0.52, 0.35]` blocks and the driver's eye as `[-6.08, 34.24, 5.6]` px; the implementation has seat 0 at `[-0.38, 0.52, 0.1625]` (ADR 0176 moved the front seats) and the generator derives the eye from that entity file (`packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json`, `tools/gen_sportsboat.py` `driver_eye`), so the ADR's z and eye coordinates are out of date; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0171 Decision 6 describes limiting repeats while the bow remains blocked and says a clear bow resets the limit; the implementation also resets the last-cut record when mode, driver input, afloat status or positive throttle is absent, allowing an immediate cut when the full trigger resumes (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`, `icebreakerCut`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR to state all reset conditions.
