### Furniture placement and recovery

- Applies to: Dining Table, Dining Chair, single sofa, and double sofa items and entities once their assets exist.
- Rule: One kind-table-driven pipeline accepts top faces outside add-on blocks, requires solid support below every footprint cell, and checks all clearance cells for air outside vehicle and camp space. Validation precedes payment. Survival consumes one matching item; Creative consumes none; failed spawns refund payment. Placement and non-dye hits are deferred and deduplicated per player per tick. Three hits within the per-kind reset window return an item to the hitter (dropping overflow), remove the entity, and show feedback, in order. Occupied or obstructed-top refusals leave counters unchanged. The adapter checks the shared host registry and refuses recovery while a registered furniture top holds hosted contents. Kinds without a registered top remain unobstructed. Recovery also refuses while a non-air or unavailable cell lies directly above a recorded or wanted tabletop cell. Successful recovery clears recorded tabletop blocks before removing the entity.

### Rain ceiling and recovery

- Applies to: Loaded parasols and their recorded ceiling cells.
- Rule: A parasol lays invisible `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` blocks in every column overlapping its octagonal canopy, in the first cell above the canopy top (`PARASOL_SPEC.canopyTop`, 4.1875 blocks). Only air outside vehicle space is filled. The entity dynamic property `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` records cells as JSON. Every `PARASOL_SPEC.ceilingSyncTicks` ticks, loaded parasols restore their saved record and missing cells, preserving the fractional base position when rebuilding the canopy. Nearby-base queries preserve that position for spacing checks. Taking back or removing a parasol clears only its recorded cells still holding the ceiling block; replacements remain. Removal is checked on the next tick, and an unloaded base chunk preserves the ceiling. Unloaded recorded cells remain pending for later cleanup after a confirmed removal. A shared laid-block guard cancels Creative breaks of recorded ceilings.

### Beach Chair form switching

- Applies to: Non-dye taps on a Beach Chair entity in either form.
- Rule: A chair tap cancels the default interaction and opens an exclusive per-player menu in `CHAIR_MENU_CHOICES` order: Lie down, then Fold into a table, with titles from `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.title`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.lie_down`, and `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.fold` in both locales. Lie down seats the player as described in Beach Chair lying down. Folding rechecks the current state and refuses with the localized `occupied` hint while `riderOf(id) !== undefined`. A table tap unfolds directly, without a menu, unless the player holds a Gas Lantern or Dutch Oven item; those taps are left to tabletop hosting and are not cancelled. Unfolding refuses with the localized `something_on_top` hint while a hosted lantern or oven stands on the table, as reported by the shared host registry. Taps are deduplicated per player per tick and deferred; wrong-form and vanished-entity requests have no effects. Folding sets the synchronized property and triggers `yasu_ride:beach_chair_fold`, which adds the table component group and removes the lounger group. The table group turns off entity gravity and collision and overrides the chair's three 1.0-wide, 1.125-high hit boxes (16 by 46 px) with two 1.0-wide, 0.5625-high boxes at pivots [0, 0.28125, -0.15625] and [0, 0.28125, 0.15625], covering only the 16 by 21 px table, 9 px high. The entity's velocity is then cleared and it is teleported to the middle cell's centre (x.5/z.5) at floor height, re-derived from its position and quarter-turn yaw so the low table cannot sink into the floor. Unfolding clears the property and triggers `yasu_ride:beach_chair_unfold`, which removes the table group and adds `yasu_ride:beach_chair_lounger`, explicitly restoring the chair's physics and three hit boxes. Fresh chairs keep the unchanged base components without either group. The table lays no blocks and ordinary blocks cannot be placed on it; taps and hits on the table reach the chair entity through its own hit boxes, so table hits count toward the same take-back as chair hits.

### Beach Chair tabletop hosting

### Dining Table assets and hosting

- Applies to: Dining Table placement, recovery, model and tabletop hosting.
- Rule: Resort Gear lists Dining Table directly after Beach Chair. Following the owner's photos, a 0.4 px steel frame (x and z within +-7.5 px, y [11.3, 12]) encloses a 0.4 px woven panel (+-7.1 px, y [11.6, 12]) whose top face repeats the `wicker.PALETTE_BLACK` weave eight times across a 128 px wide atlas. Four straight 0.5 px square steel legs splay from (+-2.8, 11.5, +-2.8) px under the top to feet at (+-6.0, 0.15, +-6.0) px, and an octagonal 0.4 px tube ring at y=6.3 px touches the legs' inner edges. All geometry stays within one cell. The entity is persistent, damage immune, unpushable, fixed in place with collision and gravity disabled, and has no seat; its 15/16 block wide collision box reaches the 0.75 block top and its custom hit box covers the full model. A shaped planks/sticks/iron recipe produces one item. Model generation also writes the icon and item preview. `installResort` registers an always-active `dining_table` host from `FURNITURE_SPEC.dining_table.top`: half extents 7.5 px on each axis (the whole visible top), height 12 px, limit four, clearance base one. Up to four Gas Lanterns fit in a two-by-two grid, or one Dutch Oven alone; an oven and a lantern cannot share the one-cell top, because their footprints need 9.5 px of separation on x or 8.25 px on z and the top allows at most 5.5 and 6.75. Existing entity-tap handlers place either kind. Recovery refuses while the registry reports hosted contents and resumes after removal. The shared hosted-items guard removes orphan contents when the missing host area is loaded. The tabletop block behavior is specified below.

Its initial half extent of 0.4 was not in the pixel units of the shared surface API and is replaced by 7.5 px, the whole visible top; the initial limit of four is kept. Tabletop blocks extend this behavior under [0241](../adr/0241-dining-table-top-blocks.md).
- Implementation: `tools/gen_dining_table.py`; `src/domain/resort/furniture.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockFurnitureWorld.ts`
- Verification: `tests/packs/diningTable.test.ts` pins generator constants, bounds, hosting capacity and pack contracts; resort adapter and installation tests cover recovery obstruction and registration; shared guard tests cover table loss. `dining_table_place_take_back`, `dining_table_lantern`, and `dining_table_oven` are authored but have not been run. ### Dining Table tabletop blocks

- Applies to: Furniture whose spec declares a top; currently Dining Table.
- Rule: Placement immediately lays `yasu_ride:dining_table_top` in the occupied cell, only into air outside vehicle space. The invisible block has a full-cell footprint, collision and selection height `FURNITURE_SPEC.dining_table.top.topPx` (12 px), no light dampening, mining or explosion destruction, and the laid-block tag. The adapter records cells as JSON in `yasu_ride:top_cells`, remembers them on entity load, and restores missing blocks every `FURNITURE_TOP_SYNC_TICKS` (20 ticks). Clearing preserves replacement blocks and retries unavailable cells after removal. Entity removal cleanup is deferred until the origin is loaded and the entity is absent. The laid-block guard cancels owned Creative breaks. Block hits and Creative break attempts share the entity-hit counter, tick deduplication, and dye exclusion. Recovery refuses while hosted items or ordinary blocks above the tabletop remain, then clears the block before entity removal. Engine interactions place ordinary blocks in the cell at y+1; there is no tap forwarding yet. Hosting clearance begins above the laid block, with the hosted item still positioned at the model top.

## Open issues

- Ordinary blocks begin at the next integer cell, 16 px above the floor, leaving a 4 px gap above the 12 px tabletop despite the design's flush-block goal. Mobile placement, table stability, and in-game cleanup require verification. Block-tap forwarding and preventing hosted gear from sharing the top with ordinary blocks remain for issue #1470.

- Beach Chair tabletop contents are reported by the shared host registry, and hosted lanterns and ovens prevent unfolding and recovery. The hosting scenarios await BDS verification. Device menu behavior and the folded table's resting height need manual verification in game.

- ADR 0236 says the rain block is laid under the canopy; the implementation lays it in the first cell above the canopy top, following the linked design (`src/domain/resort/parasolPose.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- Canopies may overlap. Shared ceiling cells are not jointly owned, so taking one parasol back can leave a temporary hole until the other parasol's next scan. The rain and snow acceptance check and fallback from ADR 0236 are recorded in [Camping gear](camping-gear.md#open-issues).

- The Dining Table's model awaits the owner's visual approval against the product photos. Its top holds one Dutch Oven or up to four Gas Lanterns, never an oven with a lantern.
