## Rules

### Feature installation and placement

- Applies to: Using `yasu_ride:beach_parasol_item` on a block.
- Rule: Bootstrap installs resort after camp, supplying vehicle and camp space checks. Resort imports shared helpers without importing camp. First block interactions are cancelled, deduplicated per player per tick, and deferred. Only a top face outside the add-on's blocks is accepted. The canopy centers over the pressed cell; the base lies `PARASOL_SPEC.baseOffset` (3.25 blocks) farther along the player's quarter-turn heading. A solid top must support the block below the base centre; support is not required below every tray cell. The fractional base position is preserved for spawning and spacing. Model `footCells` translate with that position, and every integer world cell overlapped by a translated cell must be air outside vehicle and camp space. Another base at a Chebyshev distance below `PARASOL_SPEC.minBaseDistance` refuses placement. Refusal shows an action-bar hint before payment. Survival consumes one matching held item; Creative pays nothing. Failed spawn refunds a paid item. Successful spawn lays its ceiling. All parasol tuning lives in `src/domain/resort/spec.ts`.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, placement follows the design document.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/placeParasol.ts`; `src/application/resort/ports/parasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/placement.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockParasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/bootstrap.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/resort/parasolPose.test.ts` checks integer clearance coverage and centre support for fractional bases at all quarter turns. `tests/application/resort/placeParasol.test.ts` checks individual footprint obstructions, missing centre support, Creative exclusion, payment failure, spawn refunds, and ceiling order. `tests/infrastructure/resort/handlers.test.ts` checks deferred deduplication. Live device placement has not been run.

### Rain ceiling and recovery

- Applies to: Loaded parasols and their recorded ceiling cells.
- Rule: A parasol lays invisible `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` blocks in every column overlapping its octagonal canopy, in the first cell above the canopy top (`PARASOL_SPEC.canopyTop`, 4.1875 blocks). Only air outside vehicle space is filled. The entity dynamic property `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` records cells as JSON. Every `PARASOL_SPEC.ceilingSyncTicks` ticks, loaded parasols restore their saved record and missing cells, preserving the fractional base position when rebuilding the canopy. Nearby-base queries preserve that position for spacing checks. Taking back or removing a parasol clears only its recorded cells still holding the ceiling block; replacements remain. Removal is checked on the next tick, and an unloaded base chunk preserves the ceiling. Unloaded recorded cells remain pending for later cleanup after a confirmed removal. A shared laid-block guard cancels Creative breaks of recorded ceilings.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, rain shelter follows the design document; [0235](../adr/0235-share-convex-rain-ceiling-geometry-and-placement.md) Decision, shared footprint geometry and rain ceiling layer.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/syncParasolCeilings.ts`; `src/domain/resort/parasolPose.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockParasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/lifecycle.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/rainCeiling.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/laidBlockGuard.ts`
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/resort/bedrockParasolWorld.test.ts` checks saving, fresh-adapter restoration, replacement preservation, and unloaded-cell retry. `parasol_rain_ceiling` verifies ceiling placement, periodic repair, and cleanup on the dev BDS. The block's ability to stop actual rain and snow remains unverified.

### Taking the parasol back

- Applies to: Player hits on a Beach Parasol base.
- Rule: Hits count once per player per tick through shared hit counters. Three hits within the reset window clear the ceiling, give the hitter one plain parasol item (dropping inventory overflow), remove the entity, and show the taken hint, in that order. Earlier hits show the remaining count. A gap beyond `PARASOL_SPEC.hitResetTicks` restarts counting. Server test hooks use the same take-back action, dropping at the base when no player resolves.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, pick-up follows the design document.

### Beach Chair assets

- Applies to: Beach Chair pack assets.
- Rule: Resort Gear lists Beach Chair immediately after Beach Parasol. The unpainted teak entity has a 1 by 2 footprint, one player seat at [0, 0.4375, 0.125] blocks without interaction text, and client-synchronized `yasu_ride:folded` (default false). The controller selects the saved static form on load and animates later changes over 10 ticks. The chair bed outline is x [-6, 6], z [-16, 16] px, with top y=7, hinge [0, 7, 5.5], and backrest angle 37 degrees. The chair uses eight straight square posts and the table uses four, all 1.15 by 1.15 px, with their outside faces flush with their own rails. Every chair post ends at y=5.65 and every table post at y=5.22; a 0.018 px dark seam separates it from the rail above. Near-black ungrained pads occupy y [0, 0.2], inset 0.08 px on each side. The exposed chair post wood is 5.432 px high, about 4.7 post widths; table posts expose 5.002 px. Main and rear posts have outside faces at x=+-6; stub posts at x=+-5.3. Main legs begin at z=-9 and 9.35, stub legs at z=-15.6, and rear legs at z=14.85. Lengthwise, the stub runs z [-16, -11.5], the pale band z [-11.5, -9], the main bed z [-9, 5.5] to the hinge, and the main rails z [-9, 10.5], past the hinge; the short open rear frame runs z [10.5, 16]. All apron planks are 0.4 px thick through the wood. Main aprons span y [5.65, 7] (1.35 px deep); stub and rear aprons span y [5.65, 6.82] (1.17 px deep). The inset stub rails slide 1.15 px under the main rails at the junction so an oblique view cannot expose an open side gap. The rear is an open rectangle with a single head cross rail and no middle rail. Thirteen bed and backrest slats are 0.52 px wide with 0.36 px gaps; twelve half-pitch stub slats run between them. Stub, band, and bed tops are all exactly y=7, with 0.55 px slat thickness so the gaps read as deep slots; the inset stub rail alone sits lower. The pale band uses 0.4 px main fingers and 0.16 px stub fingers. Backrest slats are 10.5 px long, so the lowered backrest ends at z=16 during the fold, and 0.06 px thick, with only the downward head crossbar. Two steel supports are 0.12 px wide and 0.08 px thick, joining the backrest at 40 percent of its length to the rear rail center at y=6.235, z=15.4. Their slope is 26.493846881381685 degrees and length 7.27346467207446 px. There is no steel crossbar. The 144 by 32 px atlas takes its colors from the studio reference photo, divided by the preview face shading so rendered faces match the photo means (frame side about a38164, legs 9b7052, slat tops d0b79c, band e6d6be): honey-brown legs (236, 172, 126), lighter apron rail sides (255, 202, 158), yellow-tan slat and rail tops (240, 212, 178), a clean cream band (252, 240, 214) whose finger sides keep the band color, light grey steel (206, 210, 210), a mid-brown teak table top (196, 156, 116), near-black pads (34, 26, 21), dark slat walls (70, 44, 26) on the inner bed slat sides and undersides so the gaps read deep and dark, and darker end grain (190, 138, 96) on the board ends. Rows 0-15 hold grain running along v and rows 16-31 the same grain running along u; every face takes the copy whose lines follow its longer side, so rails, slats, and legs show fine lengthwise grain (one-texel lines, figure lines, darker arrises) instead of blocky patches. Cross rails stop at the side rails' inner faces so no coplanar faces z-fight at the leg joints. The outermost slats use the rail color on their sides. The folded table is a low coffee table, as in the folded reference photo: its top is x [-6, 6], z [-7.25, 7.25] px, y=7 (`TABLE_TOP_PX`, the same height as the chair bed), with underside y=6.82. Its width stays 12 px. It is centered on the entity origin (`TABLE_Z_CENTER` 0), so tabletop hosting needs no center offset, and it spans portions of both original cells, z [-16, 0] and [0, 16]. ### Beach Chair placement and recovery

- Applies to: Using `yasu_ride:beach_chair_item` on a block and hitting a Beach Chair entity.
- Rule: A top-face press outside add-on blocks places the chair form across the pressed cell and the next cell along the player's quarter-turn heading, with the backrest at the far end. Both cells require solid full-height support and two layers of air outside vehicle and camp space. Validation precedes payment; Survival consumes one matching item, Creative consumes none, and a failed spawn refunds payment. Block presses and hits are deduplicated per player per tick and deferred. Dye hits do not count. Three hits within `BEACH_CHAIR_SPEC.hitResetTicks` return one item to the hitter (dropping overflow), remove the entity, and show a recovery hint; earlier hits show the remaining count. A longer gap restarts counting. Recovery refuses while `riderOf(id) !== undefined`, including when the hitter is the rider. Refused recovery leaves the counter unchanged. Action-bar hints use the localized keys `yasu_ride.beach_chair.blocked`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.hits_left` (with the remaining count), `yasu_ride.beach_chair.taken`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.occupied`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.folded`, and `yasu_ride.beach_chair.something_on_top` in both English and Japanese. Taps are covered by Beach Chair form switching.

### Beach Chair form switching

- Applies to: Non-dye taps on a Beach Chair entity in either form.
- Rule: A chair tap cancels the default interaction and opens an exclusive per-player menu in `CHAIR_MENU_CHOICES` order: Lie down, then Fold into a table, with titles from `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.title`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.lie_down`, and `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.fold` in both locales. Lie down seats the player as described in Beach Chair lying down. Folding rechecks the current state and refuses with the localized `occupied` hint while `riderOf(id) !== undefined`. A table tap unfolds directly, without a menu, unless the player holds a Gas Lantern or Dutch Oven item; those taps are left to tabletop hosting and are not cancelled. Unfolding refuses with the localized `something_on_top` hint while a hosted lantern or oven stands on the table; the adapter reports nothing on the table until hosting is wired. Taps are deduplicated per player per tick and deferred; wrong-form and vanished-entity requests have no effects. Folding sets the synchronized property and triggers `yasu_ride:beach_chair_fold`, which turns off entity gravity and collision; the entity's velocity is then cleared and it is teleported to its placement pose origin so the low table cannot sink into the floor. Unfolding clears the property and triggers `yasu_ride:beach_chair_unfold`, which restores the chair's physics. The table lays no blocks and ordinary blocks cannot be placed on it; taps and hits on the table reach the chair entity through its own hit boxes, so table hits count toward the same take-back as chair hits.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0238](../adr/0238-a-beach-chair-that-folds-into-a-table.md) Decision, chair menu, folded table and recovery.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/beachChairMenu.ts`; `src/application/resort/switchBeachChairForm.ts`; `src/application/resort/ports/beachChairForm.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockBeachChairWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairInteraction.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/beach_chair.se.json`
- Verification: `tests/application/resort/beachChairMenu.test.ts` covers routing and transition refusals. `tests/infrastructure/resort/beachChairInteraction.test.ts` covers deferred menus, exclusive forms, direct unfolding, and hosted items left alone. `tests/infrastructure/resort/bedrockBeachChairWorld.test.ts` covers state, events, and holding the table at its pose origin. `beach_chair_fold` is registered for later BDS verification and has not been run.

### Beach Chair lying down

- Applies to: Choosing Lie down on a chair, loaded chair riders, and normal dismounts.
- Rule: Recheck chair form and the current rider before seating. Table form refuses with the localized action-bar hint `yasu_ride.beach_chair.folded`; another rider refuses with `yasu_ride.beach_chair.occupied`. Teleport the player near the seat before `addRider`; failed seating does not play the pose. One player occupies the chair, keeping their own skin. Play `animation.yasu_ride.player.lounge` with controller `yasu_ride.lounge` and `stopExpression: "!query.is_riding"`. Every `BEACH_CHAIR_SPEC.poseRefreshTicks` (20 ticks), scan loaded chairs in all dimensions and resend the pose only to current riders, including riders found after reload or restart. No occupant property is saved. Normal dismount (PC sneak or touch dismount button) stops the pose. On the next scan, place the previously observed rider beside the chair exactly once, including a dismount before the first interval. Search from the side of the bed, within the spec's horizontal and vertical limits, excluding the chair footprint, vehicle and camp space, unavailable terrain, liquid and decoration floors, and cells without two layers of air. If no safe cell exists, leave the player at the engine's dismount position. Do not teleport disconnected players, players in another dimension, or players already on another ride. Forget unloaded chairs without inferring a dismount. Folding and taking back use the same live rider occupancy.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0238](../adr/0238-a-beach-chair-that-folds-into-a-table.md) Decision, chair menu and occupancy; [0237](../adr/0237-lounge-pose-by-overriding-the-player-client-entity.md) Decision, player pose and late-viewer resend requirement.

## Open issues

- Beach Chair tabletop contents are always reported as empty by the adapter in this stage, so a table always unfolds and can always be taken back; hosting lanterns and ovens (issue #1449) supplies them. Device menu behavior and the folded table's resting height need manual verification in game.

- ADR 0236 says the rain block is laid under the canopy; the implementation lays it in the first cell above the canopy top, following the linked design (`src/domain/resort/parasolPose.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- Canopies may overlap. Shared ceiling cells are not jointly owned, so taking one parasol back can leave a temporary hole until the other parasol's next scan. The rain and snow acceptance check and fallback from ADR 0236 are recorded in [Camping gear](camping-gear.md#open-issues).
