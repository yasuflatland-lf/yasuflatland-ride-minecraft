## Rules

### Furniture domain groundwork

- Applies to: Domain geometry for Dining Table, Dining Chair, single sofa, double sofa, Toilet, and Washstand; placement and recovery are installed for the first four kinds, whose assets are implemented. Toilet assets and both bathroom pieces' engine wiring are pending later issues; Washstand assets are implemented below.
- Rule: A deeply frozen per-kind table defines footprint offsets, two clearance layers, seats, recovery parameters, and an optional tabletop surface. Shared geometry faces the player and centres the origin over the occupied floor cells. The double sofa extends from the pressed column to the player's right. Toilet and Washstand each occupy only the pressed cell, require two clearance layers, have no tabletop surface, and use three-hit recovery with a 100-tick reset window. Toilet has one seat at `(0, 0.264, -0.1)` blocks; its y is the 8.06 px seat top divided by 16 minus the 0.24-block rider offset, rounded to three decimals. Seat z awaits in-game tuning. Washstand has no seats. Existing Beach Chair behavior is unchanged. Every list that depends on the furniture kinds is derived from one source, so adding a kind needs only the kind in `FurnitureKind` and `FURNITURE_SPEC`, its `FURNITURE_IDS` entry, one entry in `tools/resort_gear.json`, one generator in `tools/generators.json`, and the pack files. `tools/resort_gear.json` is the ordered list of Resort Gear members (`name`, `item`, `geometry`, `textured`, `cutout`); the creative catalog group and the preview renderer read it. The renderer uses `cutout: true` to discard texels with alpha below 128 after UV lookup, while alpha 128 through 254 remains translucent; other entries retain their existing alpha behavior. Named photo-angle review renders use the same flags and look up pieces by `name` (see [model and previews](model-and-previews.md#resort-alpha-cut-outs-and-photo-angle-review-renders)). `tools/generators.json` is the ordered generator list and the Biome format paths, run by `tools/run_generators.py` for `pnpm model` and `pnpm model:check`. The in-game test impact map keeps literal furniture pack stems, because importing `FURNITURE_IDS` would turn the resort sources into harness imports that select every scenario; `tests/test-pack/impact.test.ts` fails when a `FURNITURE_IDS` stem is missing.

### Toilet show domain rules

- Applies to: Pure Toilet interaction, rider-gone, and tick transitions; assets and engine wiring are pending later issues.
- Rule: Interacting while empty seats the interacting `PlayerId` and emits `seat`. While seated, every interaction, including from the same rider or a second player, preserves the rider and emits `refuse_occupied`. While flushing, interaction preserves the deadline and emits `refuse_flushing`. A rider-gone event changes only a seated Toilet to flushing, sets `endsAt` to the current tick plus 100, and emits `start_flush`; empty and flushing states stay unchanged with `none`. A tick at or after `endsAt` changes flushing to empty with `end_flush`; all other ticks leave the state unchanged with `none`. Frozen `TOILET_SPEC` defines a 100-tick flush, an 8-tick lid opening to 91 degrees, a 2-tick rider scan, and sound `yasu_ride.toilet_flush`. Durations are `Ticks` and the sound is `SoundId`. Detecting eligible dismounts, seating players, playing the lid and flush animations and sound, and synchronizing engine properties remain later work.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: Issue #1567 and the owner's Resort Toilet and Washstand design (2026-11-11, retained outside this worktree); [0012](../adr/0012-ddd-layered-architecture.md), pure domain rules; [0240](../adr/0240-resort-furniture-family.md), separate show modules.
- Implementation: `src/domain/resort/shows.ts`; `src/domain/resort/toiletShow.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/resort/toiletShow.test.ts` property-tests every transition, occupied and flushing refusals, repeated rider-gone events, deadline boundaries, unchanged states, and reuse after flushing. `tests/domain/resort/shows.test.ts` pins tuning values, duration and sound brands, and deep freezing. These tests do not establish engine or asset behavior.

### Washstand show domain rules

- Applies to: Pure Washstand interaction and tick transitions; client assets are implemented below, while engine wiring remains pending.
- Rule: Interacting while idle changes the state to running with `endsAt` equal to the current tick plus 100 and emits `start_run`. Interacting while running preserves the state and deadline with `none`, even if the deadline has passed before a tick processes it. A tick at or after `endsAt` changes running to idle with `end_run`; earlier ticks and idle ticks leave the state unchanged with `none`. Frozen `WASHSTAND_SPEC` defines a 100-tick run as `Ticks` and sound `yasu_ride.washstand_water` as `SoundId`. The client controller plays the tap animation from `yasu_ride:running`; starting the show from interactions, playing sound, and synchronizing engine properties remain later work.

### Furniture placement and recovery

- Applies to: Dining Table, Dining Chair, single sofa, and double sofa items and entities once their assets exist.
- Rule: One kind-table-driven pipeline accepts top faces outside add-on blocks, requires solid support below every footprint cell, and checks all clearance cells for air outside vehicle and camp space. Validation precedes payment. Survival consumes one matching item; Creative consumes none; failed spawns refund payment. Placement and non-dye hits are deferred and deduplicated per player per tick. Three hits within the per-kind reset window return an item to the hitter (dropping overflow), remove the entity, and show feedback, in order. Occupied or obstructed-top refusals leave counters unchanged. The adapter checks the shared host registry and refuses recovery while a registered furniture top holds hosted contents. Kinds without a registered top remain unobstructed. Recovery also refuses while a non-air or unavailable cell lies directly above a recorded or wanted tabletop cell. Successful recovery clears recorded tabletop blocks before removing the entity.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0240](../adr/0240-resort-furniture-family.md); Dining set design, Placing and Taking back.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/placeFurniture.ts`; `src/application/resort/takeBackFurniture.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockFurnitureWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`
- Verification: Property tests run the same placement and recovery checks for all six domain kinds; handler tests cover deferred execution, deduplication, and dye exclusion. Server-only hooks `yasu_ride_test:test_furniture_place` accept `kind x y z yaw`; `yasu_ride_test:test_furniture_hit` accepts an entity id. Dining Table, Dining Chair, Sofa (Single), and Sofa (Double) assets and their scenarios are included. Toilet assets and both bathroom pieces' engine wiring are pending later issues; Washstand assets are implemented below. Live-game verification remains pending.

### Feature installation and placement

- Applies to: Using `yasu_ride:beach_parasol_item` on a block.
- Rule: Bootstrap installs resort after camp, supplying vehicle and camp space checks. Resort imports shared helpers without importing camp. First block interactions are cancelled, deduplicated per player per tick, and deferred. Only a top face outside the add-on's blocks is accepted. The canopy centers over the pressed cell; the base lies `PARASOL_SPEC.baseOffset` (3.25 blocks) farther along the player's quarter-turn heading. Survival consumes one matching held item; Creative pays nothing. Failed spawn refunds a paid item. Successful spawn lays its ceiling. All parasol tuning lives in `src/domain/resort/spec.ts`.

### Rain ceiling and recovery

- Applies to: Loaded parasols and their recorded ceiling cells.
- Rule: A parasol lays invisible `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` blocks in every column overlapping its octagonal canopy, in the first cell above the canopy top (`PARASOL_SPEC.canopyTop`, 4.1875 blocks). Only air outside vehicle space is filled. The entity dynamic property `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` records cells as JSON. Every `PARASOL_SPEC.ceilingSyncTicks` ticks, loaded parasols restore their saved record and missing cells, preserving the fractional base position when rebuilding the canopy. Nearby-base queries preserve that position for spacing checks. Taking back or removing a parasol clears only its recorded cells still holding the ceiling block; replacements remain. Removal is checked on the next tick, and an unloaded base chunk preserves the ceiling. Unloaded recorded cells remain pending for later cleanup after a confirmed removal. A shared laid-block guard cancels Creative breaks of recorded ceilings.

### Beach Chair form switching

- Applies to: Non-dye taps on a Beach Chair entity in either form.
- Rule: A chair tap cancels the default interaction and opens an exclusive per-player menu in `CHAIR_MENU_CHOICES` order: Lie down, then Fold into a table, with titles from `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.title`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.lie_down`, and `yasu_ride.beach_chair.menu.fold` in both locales.
