## Rules

### Feature installation and placement

- Applies to: Using `yasu_ride:beach_parasol_item` on a block.
- Rule: Bootstrap installs resort after camp, supplying vehicle and camp space checks. Resort imports shared helpers without importing camp. First block interactions are cancelled, deduplicated per player per tick, and deferred. Only a top face outside the add-on's blocks is accepted. The canopy centers over the pressed cell; the base lies `PARASOL_SPEC.baseOffset` (3.25 blocks) farther along the player's quarter-turn heading. A solid top must support the block below the base centre; support is not required below every tray cell. The fractional base position is preserved for spawning and spacing. Model `footCells` translate with that position, and every integer world cell overlapped by a translated cell must be air outside vehicle and camp space. Another base at a Chebyshev distance below `PARASOL_SPEC.minBaseDistance` refuses placement. Refusal shows an action-bar hint before payment. Survival consumes one matching held item; Creative pays nothing. Failed spawn refunds a paid item. Successful spawn lays its ceiling. All parasol tuning lives in `src/domain/resort/spec.ts`.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, placement follows the design document.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/placeParasol.ts`; `src/application/resort/ports/parasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/placement.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockParasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/bootstrap.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/resort/parasolPose.test.ts` checks integer clearance coverage and centre support for fractional bases at all quarter turns. `tests/application/resort/placeParasol.test.ts` checks individual footprint obstructions, missing centre support, Creative exclusion, payment failure, spawn refunds, and ceiling order. `tests/infrastructure/resort/handlers.test.ts` checks deferred deduplication. Live device placement has not been run.

### Rain ceiling and recovery

- Applies to: Loaded parasols and their recorded ceiling cells.
- Rule: A parasol lays invisible `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` blocks in every column overlapping its octagonal canopy, in the first cell above the canopy top (`PARASOL_SPEC.canopyTop`, 4.1875 blocks). Only air outside vehicle space is filled. The entity dynamic property `yasu_ride:parasol_ceiling` records cells as JSON. Every `PARASOL_SPEC.ceilingSyncTicks` ticks, loaded parasols restore their saved record and missing cells, preserving the fractional base position when rebuilding the canopy. Nearby-base queries preserve that position for spacing checks. Taking back or removing a parasol clears only its recorded cells still holding the ceiling block; replacements remain. Removal is checked on the next tick, and an unloaded base chunk preserves the ceiling. Unloaded recorded cells remain pending for later cleanup after a confirmed removal. A shared laid-block guard cancels Creative breaks of recorded ceilings.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, rain shelter follows the design document; [0235](../adr/0235-share-convex-rain-ceiling-geometry-and-placement.md) Decision, shared footprint geometry and rain ceiling layer.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/syncParasolCeilings.ts`; `src/domain/resort/parasolPose.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockParasolWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/lifecycle.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/laidBlockLayer.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/laidBlockGuard.ts`
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/resort/bedrockParasolWorld.test.ts` checks saving, fresh-adapter restoration, replacement preservation, and unloaded-cell retry. `parasol_rain_ceiling` verifies ceiling placement, periodic repair, and cleanup on the dev BDS. The block's ability to stop actual rain and snow remains unverified.

### Taking the parasol back

- Applies to: Player hits on a Beach Parasol base.
- Rule: Hits count once per player per tick through shared hit counters. Three hits within the reset window clear the ceiling, give the hitter one plain parasol item (dropping inventory overflow), remove the entity, and show the taken hint, in that order. Earlier hits show the remaining count. A gap beyond `PARASOL_SPEC.hitResetTicks` restarts counting. Server test hooks use the same take-back action, dropping at the base when no player resolves.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0236](../adr/0236-a-beach-parasol-in-a-new-resort-gear-group.md) Decision, pick-up follows the design document.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/takeBackParasol.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/hits.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/testParasol.ts`
- Verification: `tests/application/resort/takeBackParasol.test.ts` checks effect order, reset gaps, and loaded-ceiling synchronization. `parasol_place_take_back` verifies fractional spawn position, hit counting, item return, and ceiling cleanup on the dev BDS.

### Beach Chair assets

- Applies to: Beach Chair pack assets.
- Rule: Resort Gear lists Beach Chair immediately after Beach Parasol. The unpainted teak entity has a 1 by 2 footprint, one player seat at [0, 0.4375, 0.125] blocks without interaction text, and client-synchronized `yasu_ride:folded` (default false). The controller selects the saved static form on load and animates later changes over 10 ticks. The table top is 16 px high with half extents [6, 16] px and bed outline x [-6, 6], z [-16, 16] px. The table stands taller than the 7 px chair bed, so ordinary blocks placed in the next integer cell sit flush on its visual top. Chair form has eight 2 by 2 px legs rising 6.2 px to the slat underside. Main side rails are 1.5 px wide and 1.6 px deep, leaving 4.6 px clear below them (2.875 times rail depth); inset foot rails are 1.1 px deep. The open rear frame has 1 px wide, 1.1 px deep rails with tops at 6.2 px, 0.5 px wide short posts below the hinge ends, and one 2 by 2 px leg at each far corner. Twelve main bed slats meet twelve aligned backrest slats directly at [0, 7, 3] px, without hinge pins, extra blocks, or a lower crossbar. The backrest rises 37 degrees above the bed; its slats are 0.765 px wide with 0.18 px gaps and are 0.06 px thick. Nine inset foot slats retain their 0.72 px width and 0.44 px gaps. Interleaved pale lengthwise segments (0.5 px main fingers and 0.18 px foot fingers) occupy a 4.5 px foot overlap zone with 0.24 px air gaps. The foot-end stub frame and its two legs remain in chair form. Only the outward/downward head-end crossbar remains behind the backrest ends. A separate bright steel strip on each side is 0.25 px wide and 0.12 px thick, from the backrest underside at 40 percent of its 12.8 px length to y=5.65, z=15.4 px on the rear rail. The required endpoints within the footprint give a 27.91 degree downward slope and about 9.364 px length, rather than the photograph's approximate 20 degrees. There is no steel crossbar; the strips stow flat and hide with the chair group. Table form is one plain rectangle filling the same outline, with exactly four 2 by 2 px corner legs rising from ground level to the 15.2 px slat underside, no stub frame or pale band, and a continuous slatted surface with thin edge-to-edge joints and one middle row of nine 0.36 px square holes separated by wood. Its side rails are 1.5 px wide and its end rails are 1 px wide; all rails run from 13.6 to 15.2 px high, retaining a 1.6 px depth just below the 0.8 px thick top. It has no grooves, black gaps, or dark strip. The required root/frame/backrest/prop bones remain; chair parts descend from `chair_form`, while `table_form` carries the rectangular frame and a `table_top` child. Static poses use complementary zero/one group scales; fold/unfold rotate the backrest and prop, then switch group scales with pre/post keys at the end of the 10-tick transition. The renderer hides scaled-out descendants too. The shaped recipe uses planks, sticks, and iron. The item, recipe, and entity exist with scripted placement and recovery; form switching and invisible table support blocks are wired; player poses and hosting are pending.

### Beach Chair placement and recovery

- Applies to: Using `yasu_ride:beach_chair_item` on a block and hitting a Beach Chair entity.
- Rule: A top-face press outside add-on blocks places the chair form across the pressed cell and the next cell along the player's quarter-turn heading, with the backrest at the far end. Both cells require solid full-height support and two layers of air outside vehicle and camp space. Validation precedes payment; Survival consumes one matching item, Creative consumes none, and a failed spawn refunds payment. Block presses and hits are deduplicated per player per tick and deferred. Dye hits do not count. Three hits within `BEACH_CHAIR_SPEC.hitResetTicks` return one item to the hitter (dropping overflow), remove the entity, and show a recovery hint; earlier hits show the remaining count. A longer gap restarts counting. Refused recovery leaves the counter unchanged. Action-bar hints use the localized keys `yasu_ride.beach_chair.blocked`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.hits_left` (with the remaining count), `yasu_ride.beach_chair.taken`, `yasu_ride.beach_chair.occupied`, and `yasu_ride.beach_chair.something_on_top` in both English and Japanese. Non-dye chair interactions open the form menu described below.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0238](../adr/0238-a-beach-chair-that-folds-into-a-table.md) Decision, placement and recovery follow the design.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/placeBeachChair.ts`; `src/application/resort/takeBackBeachChair.ts`; `src/application/resort/ports/beachChairWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockBeachChairWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairPlacement.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairHits.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/install.ts`

### Beach Chair form switching and tabletop recovery

- Applies to: Non-dye taps on Beach Chair entities, tabletop block interactions and hits, and loaded chairs.
- Rule: A chair tap cancels the default interaction and opens an exclusive per-player menu in `CHAIR_MENU_CHOICES` order: Lie down, then Fold into a table. Lie down currently does nothing. Folding rechecks the current state and refuses while the rideable component has riders. A table tap unfolds directly. Tabletop block interactions leave placeable block items, Gas Lanterns and Dutch Ovens to their placement handlers; other items and bare hands request unfolding. A non-air cell directly above either tabletop cell refuses unfolding and recovery with the existing localized message, without advancing recovery hits. Missing above cells conservatively refuse too. Wrong-form and vanished-entity requests have no effects. Folding sets the synchronized property and disables entity collision and gravity before laying two invisible `yasu_ride:beach_chair_top` blocks at the foot and head cells. Their collision and selection boxes fill the cell width and depth from the floor to `BEACH_CHAIR_SPEC.topPx` (16 px), filling a full cell with their top flush with the visual table top; they draw nothing, dampen no light, and resist mining and explosions. The shared `LaidBlockLayer` fills only air outside vehicle space. Cells are saved as JSON in `yasu_ride:top_cells`. Every `BEACH_CHAIR_SPEC.topSyncTicks` ticks, tables repair missing blocks and chair forms clear recorded blocks. Unfolding clears blocks before restoring entity physics. Recovery clears before entity removal. Only matching recorded blocks are cleared; replacements remain. Removal cleanup waits one tick to distinguish an unloaded entity from a removed one and retries unavailable cells after confirmed removal. The shared laid-block guard cancels Creative breaks of owned tabletop blocks. Tabletop block hits and Creative break attempts forward to the same take-back action as entity hits, with one count per player per tick across all three routes; dye swings are ignored.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0238](../adr/0238-a-beach-chair-that-folds-into-a-table.md) Decision, chair menu, table blocks and recovery.
- Implementation: `src/application/resort/beachChairMenu.ts`; `src/application/resort/switchBeachChairForm.ts`; `src/application/resort/ports/beachChairForm.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/bedrockBeachChairWorld.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairInteraction.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairLifecycle.ts`; `src/infrastructure/resort/bedrock/handlers/beachChairHits.ts`
- Verification: `tests/application/resort/beachChairMenu.test.ts` covers routing and transition refusals. `tests/infrastructure/resort/beachChairInteraction.test.ts` covers deferred menus, exclusive forms and item routing. `tests/infrastructure/resort/bedrockBeachChairWorld.test.ts` covers all headings, persistence, repair, replacement preservation, vehicle exclusion, stale records and unloaded-cell cleanup. Pack tests tie tabletop collision and selection height to the spec. `beach_chair_fold`, `beach_chair_block_on_top`, and `beach_chair_top_restore` are registered for later BDS verification and have not been run.

## Open issues

- The Lie down menu choice is an inert entry point for issue #1448. Lantern and oven hosting, including hosted-item occupancy checks, remains for issue #1449. Device menu behavior and actual block placement require later manual verification.

- ADR 0236 says the rain block is laid under the canopy; the implementation lays it in the first cell above the canopy top, following the linked design (`src/domain/resort/parasolPose.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- Canopies may overlap. Shared ceiling cells are not jointly owned, so taking one parasol back can leave a temporary hole until the other parasol's next scan. The rain and snow acceptance check and fallback from ADR 0236 are recorded in [Camping gear](camping-gear.md#open-issues).
