## Rules

### Feature boundary and installation

- Applies to: All outdoor camping gear and its connection to vehicles.
- Rule: Domain rules live in `src/domain/camp`, use cases and ports in `src/application/camp`, and Bedrock adapters in `src/infrastructure/camp`. `bootstrap()` installs vehicles first, passing a late-bound camp space check, then calls `installCamp({ isVehicleSpace: vehicles.isVehicleSpace, vehicleHost: vehicles.vehicleHost })`. Camp returns its space check and switch targets; the vehicle switch target precedes the tent and Gas Lantern targets. Features exchange these functions through bootstrap. Shared inventory helpers, hit counters, switch cooldowns, bed registration, and laid-cell machinery stay in `shared/`.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0122](../adr/0122-a-low-chair-to-sit-on-outside-the-vehicles.md) Decision, "so only the composition root knows both features."; [0173](../adr/0173-remove-the-inside-lantern-and-add-the-gas-lantern.md) Decision 17, "Each camp item still keeps its own actions class, port, Bedrock world and handlers in the Fire Pit's shape."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/bootstrap.ts`; `src/infrastructure/camp/install.ts`; `src/application/camp/ports`; `src/infrastructure/shared/players.ts`; `src/domain/shared/hitCounter.ts`; `src/domain/shared/switchCooldown.ts`
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/camp/install.test.ts` "wires one hit use case into both hit handlers and asks the vehicles for their space (ADR 0122)" and "returns tent and Gas Lantern targets built on the registered actions" check camp wiring. Not verified: these mocked tests do not execute bootstrap or establish the complete import graph.

### Vehicle space and camp space

- Applies to: Placement beside vehicles and registered tents, in the requested dimension.
- Rule: `isVehicleSpace` includes a vehicle's laid cell and the cell above it, plus cells reached by a loaded vehicle's per-kind `footprint` while their height relative to its floored feet is between zero and `placement.clearance.height`, inclusive. Horizontal reach uses the distance from the vehicle center to the cell's ground square. Camp refuses that space when placing gear. `isCampSpace` includes every `tentClearCells` cell of every registered tent, including unlaid corner and tarp columns, even before its entity loads. Chairs and portable lights claim no camp space. Vehicle placement, dock laying, and headlights receive the camp check through bootstrap.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0122](../adr/0122-a-low-chair-to-sit-on-outside-the-vehicles.md) Decision, "A cell is vehicle space when a vehicle laid a room block in it or in the block under it"; [0131](../adr/0131-pitch-a-tent-in-the-camp.md) Registry and camp space, "so the vehicles refuse it (0129) even before the tent is loaded."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`isVehicleSpace`); `src/infrastructure/camp/bedrock/bedrockTentWorld.ts` (`isCampSpace`); `src/domain/camp/tent.ts`; `src/infrastructure/bootstrap.ts`
- Verification: `tests/packs/vehicleFootprint.test.ts` "gives the %s a footprint that covers its model at any heading" checks model reach, excluding the model-only wide blade. `tests/infrastructure/camp/install.test.ts` "claims each registered tent's footprint as camp space (ADR 0131)" checks saved tent space. Not verified: the full vehicle space query and all vehicle refusal paths by these tests; client placement beside every vehicle heading.

### Chair placement by kind

- Applies to: Low Chair and Noga Chair items, using `CHAIR_KINDS` profiles.
- Rule: The first block interaction on a top face is deferred with the captured slot, once per player per tick. A click on a `yasu_ride:` block or with vehicle space directly above is left uncancelled by the chair handler. Execution refuses, in order, own block or vehicle space, failed `CHAIR_SPEC.clearance` air checks, a nearby chair of the requested kind, and failed Survival item consumption. The duplicate query searches below the spawn cell with `SAME_CELL_DISTANCE`, so a settled chair is found; it does not query the other chair kind. Success spawns at the top-cell center and faces the player using `chairYaw`: player yaw plus a half turn snapped to `CHAIR_SPEC.yawStep`. Creative consumes nothing. Gravity settles the chair onto its support.
- Agreement: Proposed

### Tabletop aim, fit, clearance, and payment

- Applies to: An oven or Gas Lantern item used on a table entity. Both supported tabletop item kinds.
- Rule: `tableTopAim` intersects the forward view ray with the plane at `TABLE_TOP_PX`, then converts the point into table-local pixels; a missing intersection defaults to the center. The shared `placeOnTop` first refuses a full top at `TABLE_TOP_LIMIT`, then an oversized footprint, clamps the aim inside `TABLE_TOP_HALF_PX` by the item's footprint, and refuses overlap at that clamped point. Touching edges are allowed. Ovens use `DUTCH_OVEN_FOOTPRINT_PX`, lanterns `GAS_LANTERN_FOOTPRINT_PX`. Both kinds count together by scanning entities with markers naming that table. Accepted items inherit the table's yaw; no nudge searches for a different free point. After the fit checks, `placeOnTableTop` checks the item's world column from the table's floored y, using `DUTCH_OVEN_SPEC.clearance` or `GAS_LANTERN_SPEC.tableClearance`. Every checked cell must be air outside vehicle space. Missing tables, blocked columns, fit refusals, and changed Survival slots spawn nothing and consume nothing. Success consumes the correct held item in Survival, skips consumption in Creative or a playerless test hook, and spawns at the computed top point. Full, overlap, and oversized refusals have translated hints; a generic refusal is silent. Entity presses on a table do placement; presses on already placed gear operate that gear.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0185](../adr/0185-put-things-on-the-camp-table-instead-of-sitting-at-it.md) Decision 2, "Refuse a placement whose clamped footprint overlaps another item's; touching edges is allowed."; [0185](../adr/0185-put-things-on-the-camp-table-instead-of-sitting-at-it.md) Decision 5, "A refused placement consumes nothing."; [0191](../adr/0191-press-camp-gear-without-the-light-tool.md) Decision 7, "Placing an oven or lantern on a Camp Table targets the table entity"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/camp/bedrock/tableTopAim.ts`; `src/infrastructure/camp/bedrock/tableItems.ts`; `src/domain/camp/tabletop.ts`; `src/domain/camp/spec.ts`; `src/application/camp/placeOnTableTop.ts`; `src/application/shared/groundPlacement.ts`; `src/application/camp/ports/tableTop.ts`; `src/infrastructure/camp/bedrock/handlers/dutchOven.ts`; `src/infrastructure/camp/bedrock/handlers/gasLantern.ts`

### Hosted marker codec and saved spots

- Applies to: Oven and Gas Lantern dynamic property `yasu_ride:host`.
- Rule: `encodeHost` writes ground as an empty string, Fire Pit hosts as `fire_pit:<id>:<spot>`, and table hosts as `camp_table:<id>:@<x>,<z>` in local pixels. IDs may contain colons; the final separator introduces the position. Both old and current table markers participate in the same scan, overlap, limit, folding, and removal rules.
