## Rules

### Seat order and the driver seat

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, bulldozer; ground, flight, water, and parked.
- Rule: A kind's seats are numbered from 0 in `VehicleProfile.seats.positions` (x positive to the left, z positive forward), and those horizontal positions equal the x and z of the `seats` positions of the kind's `minecraft:rideable`; seat heights are defined only in the pack assets. Seat 0 (`DRIVER_SEAT`) is the driver seat and `controlling_seat` is 0. Every rider has a rider-list slot, and the rider settled in seat 0 is the driver. Without driver input, the kind's drive applies its driverless behavior, including the handover wait and landing for a hovering transforming vehicle; an empty seat 0 alone does not determine this.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0007](../adr/0007-seats-and-driver.md) Decision, "The driver seat is index 0, and `controlling_seat` is also 0"
- Basis: [0007](../adr/0007-seats-and-driver.md) Consequences, "If the driver seat is empty, nobody drives and the vehicle coasts to a stop"
- Basis: [0019](../adr/0019-passengers-move-up-to-the-driver-seat.md) Decision, "Keep the seat layout and the driver rule of 0007: four seats from `minecraft:rideable` with the driver seat at index 0"

### Camper seat layout

- Applies to: camper, all modes.
- Rule: The camper's driver (seat 0) sits at the front right and the front passenger (seat 1) at the front left, like the SUV. Seats 2 and 3 are the dinette on the left, behind the passenger, with seat 2 facing the rear. Both front seats of the camper's `minecraft:rideable` sit higher than the dinette seats, as configured in the asset. The values are in `camper.se.json` and `CAMPER_PROFILE.seats.positions`. The front cushion height stays fixed while the static steering assembly uses a 115-cube, 0.8-scale wheel (about 12.64 px) at 30 degrees from horizontal and a perpendicular 60-degree column (0070). The dark-dash shaft, steel sleeve and static 30-cube lever belong to `body`; the wheel bone has rotation [60, 0, 0] without steering animation, and compound assemblies follow 0167. The wheel clears the cushion by at least 1 px; the gear lever stays on `body`, independent of `steering`.
- Agreement: Accepted

- Basis: [0069](../adr/0069-camper-right-hand-drive.md) Decision, "Seats 2 and 3 stay a facing dinette on the left, now behind the passenger"
- Basis: [0070](../adr/0070-raise-the-camper-front-seats.md) Decision, "Both front seats of `minecraft:rideable` sit at 1.5 instead of 1.1, 0.4 blocks higher."

### Driver and seats by rider-list index

- Applies to: every rider, every kind.
- Rule: Every rider has a required `slot`, its index in the engine rider list. A rider's seat reading is that slot when it is below the seat count, otherwise their nearest seat in local coordinates. Readings are then settled uniquely by the next rule. Only the rider whose settled seat is 0 drives, even when their original slot differs from 0. With distinct in-range slots, handover follows the engine's list shift without waiting for positions. The adapter assigns slots before filtering the list to players and includes eligible players waiting for reboarding at their pending slots. Position-only selection, sticky drivers, and one-tick velocity correction are retired.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0223](../adr/0223-require-a-rider-list-slot-on-every-rider.md) Decision
- Basis: [0128](../adr/0128-settle-every-crews-seats-by-rider-list.md) Decision, unique seat settlement
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/seating.ts` (`RiderPos.slot`, `Seating.seatsOf`, `Seating.update`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`RiderView.slot`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockLoadedVehicles.ts` (`riders`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/seating.test.ts` "seats each rider by their slot wherever they stand, and the driver is exactly the rider in slot 0", "keeps doors and the indexed driver stable under shared lag (suv)" and "(camper)", "falls back to the nearest seat for an out-of-range slot". Not verified: that real players on devices are seated by rider-list index (0128 Consequences: revisit if the engine stops doing so). Mixed indexed/slot-less snapshots are excluded by the required-slot contract (0223).

### Unique seats, ties, and leaving

- Applies to: every kind, all modes.
- Rule: Every rider gets at most one seat and no seat holds two riders. Riders are settled in a fixed order: readings that match the rider's previous seat first, then ascending rider-list index, then input order. Each takes their reading if free, else their previous seat if free, else the free seat nearest them; an exact distance tie goes to the lower seat index. A rider beyond capacity stays unseated. A departing rider vacates their previous settled seat; vacated seats are reported ascending, and a rider who remains aboard is never reported as vacating. Vehicle calls the kind's `onSeatVacated` for each leaver and `onSeatTaken` for newly settled riders, with vacated-hook commands before taken-hook commands in `finalCommands`. Effects depend on the kind: the SUV returns door commands, the sports boat returns none, the camper returns none (the `TransformingDrive` default), and the bulldozer restarts its door timer through the hooks. A rebuilt crew (`/reload`, chunk reload) uses the same settlement and driver selection, so the next identical update reports no newly mounted driver.
- Agreement: Accepted

- Basis: [0128](../adr/0128-settle-every-crews-seats-by-rider-list.md) Decision, "Distance ties go to the lower seat index."
- Basis: [0223](../adr/0223-require-a-rider-list-slot-on-every-rider.md) Decision, restore uses the same settlement and driver selection as an ordinary update.
- Basis: [0052](../adr/0052-retry-camera-switches-and-break-seat-ties-low.md) Decision, "Use the lower seat index when two or more seats are exactly as close."

### Passenger moves up when the driver leaves (engine order)

- Applies to: kinds with `boarding.order` `"engine"` (SUV; the bulldozer has one seat), a driver who leaves while another rider remains.
- Rule: The script does not undo the engine's seating: the next rider in boarding order moves into seat 0 and becomes the driver, and the player who wants the wheel only has to wait for it. The SUV emits its door animation and sound commands for the driver's previous settled seat. `Crew.update` reports `awaitingDriver` only while riders remain, no driver is selected, and the number of updates since the last selected driver is within `SPEC.driverHandoverTicks`. This timer is shared by every kind, including chosen-seat kinds; it starts at infinity on construction and restore, so a rebuilt crew with no selected driver gets no wait. A hovering transforming drive uses the wait before starting driverless landing.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0019](../adr/0019-passengers-move-up-to-the-driver-seat.md) Decision, "Accept Bedrock's behavior: when the driver gets out, the next rider in boarding order moves into the driver seat and becomes the driver."

- Basis: [0126](../adr/0126-choose-a-new-driver-with-one-tick-of-motion.md) Decision, "Keep the 6-tick handover wait and all drive tuning unchanged."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/crew.ts` (`Crew.update`, `awaitingDriver`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts` (`SPEC.driverHandoverTicks`); `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/suv.se.json` (`minecraft:rideable`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/drive.ts` (`onSeatVacated`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts` (`step`)

### Touch Jump does not dismount a rider

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, bulldozer; any rider on touch or PC.
- Rule: Every drivable entity has `minecraft:input_ground_controlled`, `minecraft:can_power_jump`, and `minecraft:horse.jump_strength` with value 0, and `minecraft:movement` stays 0, so a press of Jump reaches the script and the rider stays seated. The script still sets the velocity every tick.

### Boarding by aim (sports boat and camper)

- Applies to: kinds with `boarding.order` `"chosen"` (sports boat, camper); a player who presses the vehicle or one of its seat fillers, not sneaking and not already riding it.
- Rule: For a known chosen-seat aggregate, the handler cancels the press and queues a request for the next layout. It skips sneaking players and players already riding this vehicle; it does not require the player to be on foot, so the adapter can transfer a player from another mount. The camper's bumper stand-in uses its separate boarding handler, which tries `BoardVehicle` before falling back to engine boarding. With a line of sight (mouse or gamepad), each seat is a ball of radius `aimRadius` at height `aimHeight` above the vehicle's feet (`SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.seatAimHeight` and `seatAimRadius`; `CAMPER_SPEC.seatAimHeight` and `seatAimRadius`); the player gets the first free seat whose ball the line enters, else the free seat it passes nearest, measured only in front of the eye. On touch (`inputInfo.lastInputModeUsed` is `Touch`), the player gets the free seat nearest where they stand on the ground plane. A seat held for a deck-seat rider is not free to others. A tie goes to the lower seat index. When every seat is taken, nothing happens. A press for which the repository has no aggregate is left to the engine. Repeated requests from one player before settlement replace that player's aim while retaining their request order.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0120](../adr/0120-choose-a-sports-boat-seat-by-aiming-at-it.md) Decision, "A press on the boat, or on one of its seat fillers, by a player on foot (not sneaking, not already riding this boat) is cancelled in `beforeEvents.playerInteractWithEntity`."

### Keeping chosen seats with seat fillers (sports boat and camper)

- Applies to: kinds with `boarding.order` `"chosen"`, while riders get in and out.
- Rule: Every tick the vehicle settles who sits where and requests an engine rider list: each seat's player up to the last taken seat, with an invisible `yasu_ride:seat_filler` in each free seat below it and nothing after the last player. A rider who stays keeps their seat. Unassigned riders are processed in ascending slot order (unknown last), taking their free list slot or the lowest free seat. Requests are seated last in request order. `SeatFillers.lay` preserves the matching prefix, ejects the suffix from the first mismatch, removes ejected fillers, and rebuilds that suffix. From the first eligible player who needs to wait, it defers the rest of the list and reports waiting players at their pending slots in snapshots. Waiting is bounded by `WAIT_TICKS`; players who are unavailable, fail `canBoard` (validity, health, dimension and `BOARD_REACH`), or exhaust the wait are replaced by fillers when no earlier player is waiting. A failed filler spawn or insertion is skipped, so later riders can temporarily occupy lower indices. Transfers from another mount are attempted only with capacity, and a refused transfer tries to restore that mount. The `minecraft:rideable` of both kinds accepts the filler family besides players. Fillers are removed when the vehicle is released or when they load without riding a vehicle. Only a player can drive.

### Driver when a chosen-seat vehicle's driver leaves

- Applies to: sports boat and camper, a driver who leaves while others remain.
- Rule: When a previously aboard chosen-seat driver leaves and seat 1 has a player, `settleSeats` moves that player to seat 0; riders in seats 2 and 3 retain their chosen assignments. A player who was only about to board is not counted as a driver who left. Once the requested rider list is successfully laid, a rear-only crew has no indexed driver. Another player can request the free driver seat by aim (or proximity on touch); a request from a player already aboard is ignored. While the list is being rebuilt, driver selection follows the snapshot slots, including pending slots, rather than the chosen assignments.

### Departure from the deck keeps seats (sports boat and camper)

- Applies to: sports boat and camper, when the parked or docked vehicle sets off with riders on its deck seats.
- Rule: On departure each rider of deck seats 0 to 3 asks for the vehicle seat of the same index, and fillers hold the empty seats below a taken one. The camper's toilet rider (deck seat 4) is not part of the chosen layout. After the layout, `DeckSeats.board` attempts every occupied deck seat in index order, restoring a refused rider to their deck seat. Departure handling then ejects any remaining deck-seat riders, retries them before nearby standing players, and teleports those still refused to the departure exit when one is configured. Capacity includes fillers, so a visually empty seat need not be available through `addRider`. Standing-player collection excludes players waiting for the chosen layout to reboard them. Departure is still driven by the seat 0 stick, so a rear deck-seat rider cannot start the camper.

### Bulldozer seat

- Applies to: bulldozer, all modes.
- Rule: The bulldozer has one seat, the driver seat, defined by `BULLDOZER_PROFILE.seats` and the `seats` entry of `bulldozer.se.json`. It boards in engine order and seats players only. Whoever occupies its only seat (seat 0) is the driver, with no seat-choice mechanism or passenger to move up. The shared `Seating` driver-selection rules and `Crew` handover timer still apply; with no remaining rider the timer does not report `awaitingDriver`.

### Bulldozer driver exit

- Applies to: bulldozer, a rider who leaves seat 0 in any mode; placement succeeds only when the player guards and outdoor spot check pass.
- Rule: The profile's optional `seatExits` gives seat 0 the exit `BULLDOZER_SPEC.doorExit` (x left-positive, z forward-positive from the vehicle's center, beside the left door and behind the blade). For a leaving rider whose seat has an exit, the world exit is computed from the vehicle's location before this tick's move (or its dock hold) and the drive's yaw after the step. After the final commands the application asks the world to stand the rider there, and the adapter moves only a valid living player who rides nothing, is in the vehicle's dimension and within `STAND_UP_REACH` blocks of it, and only to a spot the outdoor check accepts. The outdoor check is `seatExitSpot`: every overlapped feet/head column must pass its `passable` predicate, with `ground` under the center; it can raise the exit to the next row under its `RAISED_FLOOR` condition. It does not inspect drive form or `onGround`, so an exit while flying can succeed if the same checks pass. Otherwise the engine's spot stays. The SUV, camper, and sports boat have no `seatExits`.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0141](../adr/0141-put-a-bulldozer-driver-out-beside-the-left-door.md) Decision, "Add optional, kind-neutral `VehicleProfile.seatExits`, one local point per seat, with x left-positive and z forward-positive (0050)."
- Basis: [0141](../adr/0141-put-a-bulldozer-driver-out-beside-the-left-door.md) Decision, "A missing vehicle or player or any failed check keeps the engine's already checked spot."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/profile.ts` (`VehicleProfile.seatExits`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts` (`BULLDOZER_SPEC.doorExit`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`Vehicle.seatExits`); `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts` (`standRiderAt`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`standRiderAt`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/standUp.ts` (`standUp`, `STAND_UP_REACH`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/seatExitSpot.ts` (`seatExitSpot`, `passable`, `ground`, `RAISED_FLOOR`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleSeatExit.test.ts` "places only leaving riders at the configured exit in the post-step frame"; `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/profile.test.ts` "puts one exit beside the left track and behind the blade"; `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/seatExitSpot.test.ts`; in-game scenarios `bulldozer_exit_spot` and `bulldozer_exit_ground` (`src/test-pack/scenarios/bulldozerExit.ts`). ### Pressing a taken dinette seat with camping gear

- Applies to: parked camper, a player holding camping gear who presses a dinette deck seat.
- Rule: A press from a dinette deck seat names one slot (`PlaceDecorCommand.exact` is `true`; a press on a furniture or decor block has it `false`). The handler cancels every recognized gear press, while `SeatPresses` admits placement requests only after its idle interval. An admitted request is deferred with `system.run`; `slotForPress` considers accepting free slots in the pressed cell and chooses the nearest one there, with no fallback to another cell when `exact`. Placement also requires the vehicle to remain known, successful consumption outside Creative, and `vehicle.asRoom()?.placeDecor` to succeed (0220). When no slot is offered, the player sees "No free spot for this item", keeps the item, and nothing is saved. Other refusal paths do not necessarily show this message; a failed placement after consumption is not refunded by this use case. The press stays cancelled in both cases, so a refused press does not seat the player. With an empty hand or any non-gear item, this handler does not intervene in seating.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0104](../adr/0104-refuse-a-press-on-a-taken-seat.md) Decision, "With `exact`, the item goes on the pressed slot when that slot accepts it and is free."
- Basis: [0104](../adr/0104-refuse-a-press-on-a-taken-seat.md) Decision, "The seat press stays cancelled in both cases, so a refused press does not seat the player either."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/decorPlacing.ts` (`slotForPress`); `src/application/vehicle/placeDecor.ts` (`PlaceDecorCommand.exact`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/kinds/camper/handlers/decor.ts` (`placeGearOnSeat`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/seatPress.ts` (`SeatPresses`, `seatSlotCell`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/decorPlacing.test.ts` "with an exact press returns the pressed slot when it accepts the item and is free, else nothing, never another slot (ADR 0104)"; `tests/application/vehicle/placeDecor.test.ts` "refuses an exact press on a taken slot with the no-slot message, consuming and saving nothing (ADR 0104)". Not verified: on a device, and that an empty-handed press seats the player (no test found).
