## Rules

### Ram hit box

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat and bulldozer, moving forward or reversing.
- Rule: `selectHits` requires `abs(speed) > SPEC.ramMinSpeed`. With heading `f`, it measures `ahead = dot(target.offset, f) * sign(speed)` and `side = abs(dot(target.offset, rightOf(f)))`. A target passes the box test when `ahead` is in the inclusive interval from zero to `body.front + SPEC.targetHalfWidth`, `side <= halfWidth + SPEC.targetHalfWidth`, and its height offset is in the inclusive interval `SPEC.ramMinDy..SPEC.ramMaxDy`. Protected targets and targets still on cooldown are excluded. Reversing puts the box behind the vehicle. `Vehicle.ram` supplies the profile's `body.front` and `halfWidth = min(step.ramHalfWidth ?? body.halfWidth, ramHalfWidthLimit(profile))`. The limit is `max(body.halfWidth, profile.maxRamHalfWidth ?? body.halfWidth)`. Profiles read `SUV_SPEC.bodyFront/bodyHalfWidth` (2.68/1.43, including protruding wheels), `CAMPER_SPEC.bodyFront/bodyHalfWidth`, `SPORTSBOAT_SPEC.ramFront/ramHalfWidth`, and `BULLDOZER_SPEC.bodyFront/bodyHalfWidth`. The bulldozer reports `bladeRule().halfWidth` per step, raised or lowered, with `BULLDOZER_WIDE_BLADE.halfWidth` as its profile cap.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Decision, "The detection area is a rectangle in front of the vehicle."
- Basis: [0217](../adr/0217-derive-the-suv-spec-from-the-rubicon-body.md) Decision, "Round enclosing probes upward to `bodyFront=2.68`, `bodyHalfWidth=1.43` and `footprint=3.12`."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts` (`selectHits`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`ram`); `src/domain/vehicle/profile.ts` (`ramHalfWidthLimit`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.ts` (`bladeRule`, `step`)

### Ram damage and knockback

- Applies to: Each selected ram hit, all vehicle kinds.
- Rule: `ramDamage(speed)` is `max(1, round(abs(speed) * SPEC.ramDamagePerSpeed))`. The horizontal push is the heading multiplied by `sign(speed) * (SPEC.ramPushBase + abs(speed) * SPEC.ramPushPerSpeed)`; the vertical push is `SPEC.ramPushUp`. `DriveVehicles` forwards every `out.hits` entry to `world.damage` with `out.driverId`. `BedrockVehicleWorld.damage` skips a missing or invalid target, applies damage with cause `entityAttack` and the resolved driver as `damagingEntity` (undefined without an attacker), then calls `applyKnockback` with the horizontal and upward push.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Context, "Whatever the vehicle hits takes damage that scales with speed and is pushed aside."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts` (`ramDamage`, `selectHits`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`damage`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/ram.test.ts` "is monotonically nondecreasing with speed and at least 1 at speeds where ram occurs", "is 18 at maximum speed", and "hits a target exactly when it is inside the hit box of any body, unprotected, and off cooldown"; `tests/application/vehicle/driveVehicles.test.ts` "forwards ram hits to the world with the driver as the attacker" and "forwards ram hits without an attacker when test input drives the vehicle". ### Ram cooldown

- Applies to: Repeated hits on one target by one vehicle.
- Rule: Each vehicle owns `RamCooldowns`, recording the hit tick by target ID. A recorded target is excluded while `tick - lastHit < SPEC.ramCooldown`; it becomes eligible at equality. Before selection the vehicle prunes expired entries, and after selection it records the returned hits. Different vehicles keep separate maps.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Decision, "Do not hit the same target again within 10 ticks"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/cooldowns.ts` (`RamCooldowns`); `src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts` (`selectHits`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`ram`, `ramCooldowns`)

### Occupant protection and vehicle immunity

- Applies to: Ram targets and the four vehicle entity types.
- Rule: `isProtectedFromRam` protects only a target whose mount is `vehicle`. `ramMountOf` classifies a recognized vehicle entity as `vehicle` and a deck seat as `deckSeat`; the latter is treated like a target on foot. This protects direct riders of either the attacking vehicle or another vehicle from the scripted ram. The SUV, camper, sports boat and bulldozer each have a base `minecraft:damage_sensor` with cause `all` and `deals_damage: no`. That sensor protects the vehicle entity. Rider immunity to collision, falling and other engine damage is left to the engine.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Decision, "Always protect riders."; [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Decision, "Make the vehicle immune to all damage with `minecraft:damage_sensor`"; [0006](../adr/0006-ram-damage-and-occupant-protection.md) Decision, "Leave rider damage immunity to the engine's behavior and catch regressions with in-game tests."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts` (`isProtectedFromRam`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`ramMountOf`); `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/suv.se.json` (damage sensor, lines 446-448); `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper.se.json` (lines 522-524); `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json` (lines 604-609); `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/bulldozer.se.json` (lines 613-618)

### Core-only target sensor and world query

- Applies to: `Vehicle.tick`, kind drives and Bedrock target discovery.
- Rule: `VehicleTickInput.targets()` is lazy and called only when absolute drive speed exceeds `SPEC.ramMinSpeed`. Before calling `Drive.step`, `Vehicle.tick` strips `riders`, `testInput`, `testHeld`, `deckSeatRiders` and `targets` from the input; `TickContext` has no `targets`. `DriveVehicles` supplies the query through `world.targetsNear`. The Bedrock query searches around the vehicle within `RAM_QUERY_RADIUS`, excludes family `inanimate`, types `minecraft:item` and `minecraft:xp_orb`, and the querying vehicle itself, then returns offsets and mount classifications. Because every vehicle entity (SUV, camper, sports boat, bulldozer), deck seat, bumper stand-in, seat filler and camping furniture entity declares the family `inanimate`, none of them is ever a ram target; `ramMountOf` and the rider protection only matter for the riders of vehicles, not for the vehicles themselves. `RAM_QUERY_RADIUS` is derived from all profiles' farthest ram corners, including `ramHalfWidthLimit(profile)` and the more distant height bound.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0083](../adr/0083-ram-targets-are-a-core-sensor.md) Decision, "`TickContext` no longer has `targets()`."; [0083](../adr/0083-ram-targets-are-a-core-sensor.md) Decision, "still lazy and still called only when the speed exceeds `SPEC.ramMinSpeed`"

### Stand-in placement

- Applies to: A vehicle profile with a `standIn` rule; currently only the camper.
- Rule: `StandInRule` supplies `name`, `ahead` and `turnMargin`. `standInAxis` chooses the row perpendicular to the nearest cardinal heading and retains the existing axis until the yaw passes the diagonal by more than the margin; a cardinal heading always gets its own axis. `standInPose` places the row center `ahead` along the true heading, rounding cardinal trigonometric residue away. `Vehicle.standIn` uses the dock hold location while held and the tick's location before movement otherwise. The camper declares `name: camper_bumper`, `ahead: CAMPER_SPEC.bodyFront`, and `turnMargin: CAMPER_SPEC.bumperTurnMargin`. Its optional lift definitions are `maxLift: CAMPER_SPEC.bumperMaxLift` and `parkedMaxLift: CAMPER_SPEC.bumperParkedMaxLift`; ground lifting is the separate 0119 camper extension.

### Stand-in lifecycle

- Applies to: Loaded camper stand-ins and their owners.
- Rule: `StandIns.keep` finds a valid owned stand-in from session memory or the owner's `STAND_IN_PROPERTY`, spawns it if absent, and saves `STAND_IN_OF_PROPERTY` on it and its ID on the vehicle. It replaces the entity on row-axis or dimension changes and teleports only beyond `TELEPORT_TOLERANCE`. A failed spawn in an unloaded chunk is skipped for retry; a failed teleport is caught. Each completed keep ejects riders. The first keep after a reload sweeps other nearby stand-ins naming the same owner. An absent vehicle or pose releases the stand-in. `BedrockVehicleWorld` runs `standIns.clearGone(live)` on every snapshot, so each tick it removes session stand-ins and loaded stand-ins with no live owner. The entity-load handler in `registerFleet` (and the entity-spawn handler for the `Loaded` cause) calls `removeOrphanStandIn`, which removes a loaded stand-in unless a nearby vehicle in its dimension names it. `vehicleOfTarget` resolves a vehicle directly, a seat filler's vehicle, or a stand-in's loaded valid vehicle.

### Hit and interaction forwarding

- Applies to: Player hits and interactions targeting a vehicle or its bumper stand-in.
- Rule: `registerHits` accepts only a `Player` as the damaging entity, resolves the vehicle with `vehicleOfTarget`, and forwards a held dye to `PaintVehicle`; otherwise it calls `HitVehicle` with `fromDeck` from `standsOnDeck`. `registerStandIns` cancels every stand-in `playerInteractWithEntity` event. A headlight switch item is left to the shared switch router. Sneaking players and players already riding that vehicle do not board. Otherwise `requestBoarding` tries `BoardVehicle` first; if declined, a next-tick callback checks player and vehicle validity and calls the vehicle's `addRider`. The request uses no view direction for touch and the player's view direction for other input. Switch presses on a stand-in resolve its owner in `vehicleSwitchTarget`; the router chooses headlights or room lights from the player's riding and room context. A non-dye attack with the switch still follows `HitVehicle`.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0116](../adr/0116-a-bumper-stand-in-takes-the-camper-hits.md) Decision, "a player's hit on a stand-in goes to its camper as a hit on the camper would"; [0154](../adr/0154-keep-camper-passengers-in-their-seats-on-departure.md) Decision, "The bumper stand-in boards through `BoardVehicle` first"; [0154](../adr/0154-keep-camper-passengers-in-their-seats-on-departure.md) Decision, "When `BoardVehicle` declines, the stand-in falls back to `addRider` on the next tick, as before."

### Hint zones and eligibility

- Applies to: Players on foot near kinds opting into `profile.hints`; currently only the camper.
- Rule: `Vehicle.hint` and `hintAt` measure zones in the vehicle's frame. The camper's bumper rectangle reads `CAMPER_SPEC.bumperBoxXs`, `bumperBoxSize`, `bodyFront`, `bodyHalfWidth`, `bumperHintReach` and `bumperHintFeet`. Players inside the body's excluded region get no bumper hint. The driver-seat zone reads profile seat 0 and `CAMPER_SPEC.seatHintReach/seatHintFeet`; it requires a parked vehicle, no direct or deck-seat driver, and a player at least as near the driver seat as every other vehicle seat. Driver-seat hints win where both zones apply. At the bumper, a dye selects the paint hint even with riders aboard; otherwise direct vehicle riders suppress the pick-up hint. `ShowVehicleHints` suppresses all hints while the player rides anything, tries eligible vehicles in the same dimension nearest first, and forgets absent players. `BedrockHintWorld.hintPlayers` also excludes spectators and players whose state cannot be read.

### Hint text and input mode

- Applies to: Camper bumper, paint and parked driver-seat action-bar hints.
- Rule: `BedrockHintWorld` sends translated raw text with `setActionBar`. Touch selects `touch`, KeyboardAndMouse selects `mouse`, and other input modes or a failed input-mode read select `other`. The bumper keys `yasu_ride.hint.bumper_touch/mouse/other` say to hit the bumper to pick up the vehicle, or long-press, right-click or use it to get in. Their arguments are `SPEC.hitsToBreak` and a nested entity-name translation for the vehicle kind. `yasu_ride.hint.paint` asks the player to hit the bumper with the dye to paint the named vehicle. `yasu_ride.hint.drive_touch/mouse/other` asks the player to long-press, right-click or use the driver's seat to drive.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0086](../adr/0086-camper-hints-near-the-bumper-and-the-parked-driver-seat.md) Decision, "The hints are sent with `setActionBar` as a raw text that translates `yasu_ride.hint.<name>`"; [0086](../adr/0086-camper-hints-near-the-bumper-and-the-parked-driver-seat.md) Decision, "The count is filled with `SPEC.hitsToBreak`, so the lang text never repeats the number"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/hints.ts` (`HINTS`, `hintAt`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockHintWorld.ts` (`controlsOf`, `argText`, `showHint`); `packs/yasu_ride_rp/texts/en_US.lang`
- Verification: `tests/application/vehicle/showVehicleHints.test.ts` "hints a player in front of the camper's bumper by their input and item"; `tests/domain/vehicle/hints.test.ts` "asks a player with a dye to paint, and anyone else to hit the bumper the hits it takes unless a rider keeps it whole". Lang values and adapter translation/input mapping were inspected. Not verified: rendered translations, exception fallback and changing input devices on a real phone or PC.

### Hint timing and clearing

- Applies to: Per-player hint state maintained by `ShowVehicleHints`.
- Rule: `registerDrive` registers the hint interval after the drive interval, using `SPEC.hintCheckInterval`. `stepHint` shows a new hint name immediately and repeats the same name no sooner than `SPEC.hintRefreshTicks`, while elapsed time since its first send is less than `SPEC.hintShowTicks`; then it stays quiet until the candidate changes or disappears. `Vehicle.hint` first tries the normal zones; if none gives a hint, it retains the current name through `zoneOf` with `SPEC.hintExitMargin`. The margin widens horizontal reach, body exclusion and nearest-seat comparison, but leaves height bands unchanged. Losing a candidate on foot clears the action bar once. Mounting resets hint state without clearing the action bar, preserving the game's dismount tip.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0086](../adr/0086-camper-hints-near-the-bumper-and-the-parked-driver-seat.md) Decision, "registered right after the drive interval so it reads this tick's parked state"; [0086](../adr/0086-camper-hints-near-the-bumper-and-the-parked-driver-seat.md) Decision, "Leaving the zone on foot clears the action bar once with an empty text."; [0086](../adr/0086-camper-hints-near-the-bumper-and-the-parked-driver-seat.md) Decision, "Mounting does not clear it"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/hints.ts` (`zoneOf`, `stepHint`, `HINT_TIMING`); `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`hint`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/showVehicleHints.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/drive.ts`
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/hints.test.ts` "keeps the paint hint where a widened driver's seat zone overlaps it (issue #355)", "holds each hint by the zone that gives it", "shows a changed hint at once and clears once on leaving", and "repeats a hint no sooner than the refresh and stops after the show time"; `tests/application/vehicle/showVehicleHints.test.ts` "re-sends while the player stays, then stops, and clears once when they walk away" and "leaves the action bar alone when the player mounts". Not verified: actual action-bar lifetime, flicker on phone or PC, and engine callback order when both intervals run; the registration order is visible in source.

## Open issues

Where an ADR and the implementation differ, this spec states the implementation and lists the difference here.

- ADR 0083 Decision says `Vehicle.tick` removes `riders`, `testInput` and `targets` from its input before `Drive.step`; the implementation also strips `testHeld` and `deckSeatRiders` (`src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`, `tick`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0006 says a hit happens "at speed 0.15 or higher"; the implementation requires speed strictly greater than `SPEC.ramMinSpeed` (`src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0006 says the detection rectangle uses SUV-center depth 1.85 and half-width 1.1 plus the target half width and lies in front only; the implementation reads each kind's profile body, uses the bulldozer's per-step blade width capped by `ramHalfWidthLimit`, adds the `SPEC.ramMinDy..SPEC.ramMaxDy` height band, and hits behind when reversing (`src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/`). SUV body definitions changed through 0170, 0194 and 0217, and camper, boat and bulldozer profiles have their own definitions; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0006 says detection depth is 2.3 and distance per tick is at most 0.9; the implementation's SUV depth is now `SUV_SPEC.bodyFront + SPEC.targetHalfWidth`, which exceeds that stated depth, while `SUV_SPEC.maxSpeed` still matches the ADR's SUV speed claim. Other kinds have their own speed limits, including the faster sports boat (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR rather than presenting the old SUV figures as universal.
- ADR 0006 says never to hit occupants of your own or another vehicle; the implementation protects direct vehicle riders only and treats deck-seat riders like targets on foot (`src/domain/vehicle/ram.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts`). ADR 0063 Decision introduces separate boat deck-seat entities, and 0068 Decision uses them for camper seats, without explicitly resolving ram protection; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0006 says rider-damage regressions are caught with in-game tests; the implementation's scenarios check cows in `ram_cow`, `boat_ram`, `bulldozer_ram_cow` and `bulldozer_wide_ram`, unchanged boat health in `boat_ram`, and bulldozer health in `bulldozer_fall_survives`, but no scenario checking rider health was found (`src/test-pack/catalog.ts`, `src/test-pack/scenarios/suv.ts`, `src/test-pack/scenarios/sportsboat.ts`, `src/test-pack/scenarios/bulldozer.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0116 requires a phone check on all four headings before acceptance (#446, #448); the implementation has stand-in geometry, fake-world lifecycle and mocked event tests, while only the north-facing iPad break/rear-no-hit check is recorded in its Consequences (`tests/domain/vehicle/vehicleStandIn.test.ts`, `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/standIns.test.ts`, `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/subscriptions.test.ts`). `docs/manual-test.md` has empty PC and Mobile result cells for C4, C8, C10 and C11. The other headings, paint, boarding, room taps and driving checks remain unverified on device; the ADR needs an edit recording the outcome or a superseding ADR, and remains Proposed.
- ADR 0116 says the stand-in stands at the camper's feet height; the implementation applies the ground-lift extension of ADR 0119 using `CAMPER_SPEC.bumperMaxLift` and `CAMPER_SPEC.bumperParkedMaxLift` (`src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/profile.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR identifying that extension to its placement statement.
- ADR 0116 says the stand-in "blocks a placed block only in the one cell under its middle box"; the implementation declares a small collision box with physics collision disabled and has no explicit per-cell block-placement rule (`packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper_bumper_x.se.json`, `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper_bumper_z.se.json`). The claimed engine placement effect is not verified by an asset assertion or a recorded owner check; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR distinguishing the declaration from the unverified effect.
- ADR 0116's hidden-box occlusion claim is explicitly conditional on parking under ADR 0068. The asset pivot is `[0, CAMPER_SPEC.hiddenHitboxSize / 2, 0]` in effect, and `tests/packs/camperRoom.test.ts` checks the box fits inside the center floor block at an example parked pose (`packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper.se.json`). This verifies geometry, not the absence of reachable taps on every client; device verification remains open.
- ADR 0116 leaves the sports boat's world-axis hit-box flaw for follow-up. The implementation still has the three boxes at the pivots described there and no sports-boat `standIn` profile rule (`packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts`). This matches the recorded deferral; actual yaw-dependent tap behavior was not rechecked here.
- ADR 0116 says a rider holding the headlight switch switches the camper's headlights through the stand-in; the implementation distinguishes attack hits from switch presses: a non-dye attack reaches `HitVehicle`, while switch presses resolve the stand-in owner through `vehicleSwitchTarget` and route to headlights or ceiling lights according to rider and room context (`src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/hits.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/standIn.ts`, `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/headlights.ts`, `src/infrastructure/shared/switchRouter.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR clarifying the event and rider conditions.
- ADR 0086 says players on foot get hints without specifying a game-mode exclusion; the implementation also excludes spectators and players whose state reads throw (`src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockHintWorld.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0086 says a shown hint keeps showing until the exit margin past every edge of its zone; the implementation applies the margin horizontally but keeps both feet-height bands unchanged (`src/domain/vehicle/hints.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0086 describes bumper text joining pick-up and boarding with "and"; the implementation's English bumper variants join them with "or" (`packs/yasu_ride_rp/texts/en_US.lang`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR if its wording is intended literally.
- ADR 0086 says hint geometry reads the same values as the hit-box test so they cannot drift apart; the implementation's hints remain in the camper's local frame and feet-height bands, while the stand-in uses a world-axis row with hysteresis and ground lift from 0116 and 0119 (`src/domain/vehicle/hints.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/standIn.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts`). They share bumper definitions but need not cover the same reachable space while turning or lifted; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR qualifying that claim.
- ADR 0086 asks for measurement of action-bar lifetime on phone and PC; the implementation uses `SPEC.hintRefreshTicks` with an explicit unmeasured-lifetime comment, and no measurement was found in the cited ADR or manual results (`src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`, `docs/manual-test.md`); the ADR needs an edit recording the check or a superseding ADR.
