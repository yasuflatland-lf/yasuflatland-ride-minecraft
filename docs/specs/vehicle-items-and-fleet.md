## Rules

### Crafted vehicle items

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, and bulldozer items.
- Rule: Each kind has a craftable item `yasu_ride:<kind>_item`, as mapped by `ITEM_TYPE`. The SUV uses a shaped `crafting_table` recipe; crafting remains available alongside starter items.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0008](../adr/0008-vehicle-item-and-breaking.md) Decision, "The vehicle item is crafted. Using it on the ground consumes one item and places one vehicle"; [0023](../adr/0023-starter-vehicle-on-first-join.md) Decision, "Crafting (0008) stays, so more vehicles can still be crafted"

### Placement from the before-event

- Applies to: recognized vehicle items used on blocks.
- Rule: `registerPlacement` listens to `world.beforeEvents.playerInteractWithBlock`. It accepts only `isFirstEvent` with an item recognized by `kindOfItem`, and cancels the interaction only when `PlaceVehicle.cellFor` returns a placement cell. A rejected surface leaves the interaction uncancelled. An accepted interaction stays cancelled even when clearance later refuses placement. The handler schedules placement through `system.run` for the next tick. `oncePerPlayerPerTick` separately admits at most one request and one execution per player per tick. The sports boat's additional `itemUse` ray over liquids belongs to the sports boat topic and is outside this rule. The headlight switch cooldown and reach in 0024 belong to the lights topic.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Decision, "Placing a vehicle listens to `world.beforeEvents.playerInteractWithBlock` instead of the after-event, with the same filters (first event, vehicle item, top face). Before-events cannot change the world, so the placement runs on the next tick through `system.run`, as painting does (0017)"; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Decision, "The before-event is cancelled, so a tap with the vehicle item on a chest, crafting table, door, or lever does not also open or toggle it"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/placement.ts` (`registerPlacement`, `oncePerPlayerPerTick`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`kindOfItem`); `src/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.ts` (`PlaceVehicle.cellFor`).
- Verification: `tests/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.test.ts` covers the placement use case only. Not verified: handler subscriptions, cancellation, deferral, and per-player event deduplication (no handler test). 0024 Consequences says "The item handling is still covered only by unit tests and the manual test"; no new device check is recorded here.

### Consumption from the original slot

- Applies to: `PlaceVehicle.execute`, all kinds and game modes.
- Rule: Placement checks clearance before consuming or spawning. Outside Creative it takes one item of the command's kind from the command's original hotbar slot through `world.consumeVehicleItem`, then spawns; if that slot no longer holds the item, nothing spawns. Switching the selected slot does not change which slot is checked. Creative skips consumption and can place without an item remaining in that slot. There is no game-mode restriction other than the Creative exemption, so Adventure can place too.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Decision, "The command carries the hotbar slot the item was used from. Outside Creative, the placement takes one item from that slot before it spawns the vehicle, and spawns nothing if the slot no longer holds one."; [0024](../adr/0024-item-use-on-mobile-and-longer-headlights.md) Decision, "Adventure mode players can place vehicles too; only Creative skips taking the item"
- Implementation: `src/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.ts` (`PlaceVehicle.execute`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockPlacementWorld.ts` (`consumeVehicleItem`, `isCreativePlayer`); `src/infrastructure/shared/players.ts` (`consumeHeld`, `isCreative`).
- Verification: `tests/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.test.ts` "consumes one item of the command's kind from the slot it was used from, then places a vehicle on the block and saves the aggregate", "does not consume the item in creative mode", "does not place the vehicle or consume the item when there is no room, such as under a low ceiling", "does not place a vehicle when the slot no longer holds the item, such as after switching slots or dropping it (ADR 0024)", "places a vehicle in creative mode without needing the item in the slot", and "consumes the item unless the placing player is in creative mode".

### Clearance box

- Applies to: placement of every kind.
- Rule: Every cell at offsets `-halfX..halfX`, `-halfZ..halfZ`, and `1..height` above the placement cell must pass `world.isAir` and must not be `isCampSpace`. The Bedrock adapter accepts air and light blocks as air, and rejects undefined or throwing block lookups. Each profile defines its clearance; `clearanceHeight(body)` is the ceiling of its body height. Every current profile uses `halfX` and `halfZ` of 1, the shared horizontal footprint. The bulldozer blade and tracks and the camper's body ends are model only for this check and may overlap nearby blocks. The clearance promises room for the collision box rather than every visible model part.
- Agreement: Proposed
- Basis: [0142](../adr/0142-place-the-bulldozer-in-the-suvs-space.md) Decision, "Set the bulldozer's placement clearance to `halfX` 1, `halfZ` 1, and height 3, the SUV's 3 x 3 footprint."; [0142](../adr/0142-place-the-bulldozer-in-the-suvs-space.md) Decision, "Leave the blade and tracks, which are model only, out of the check."; [0143](../adr/0143-place-the-camper-in-the-suvs-space.md) Decision, "Set the camper's placement clearance to `halfX` 1, `halfZ` 1, and height 4, the SUV's footprint with the camper's own height."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/placement/placement.ts` (`canPlaceAt`, `clearanceHeight`); `src/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.ts` (`isFree`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockPlacementWorld.ts` (`isAir`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/profile.ts`.
- Verification: `tests/packs/placementClearance.test.ts` "fits every %s collision box inside its placement clearance"; `tests/domain/vehicle/placement/placement.test.ts` `canPlaceAt` tests, including "checks only distinct clearance cells and stops at the first obstruction" and "uses every dimension of a kind's clearance"; `tests/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.test.ts` "refuses any camp cell inside the clearance before reading its block or consuming or spawning". ### Starter items once per kind

- Applies to: players joining a session, including Creative players and players who joined before starters were introduced.
- Rule: On `playerSpawn` with `initialSpawn`, `GiveStarterVehicle` gives one item of each kind in `STARTER_KINDS` that the player has not received. The per-kind player dynamic property `STARTER_RECEIVED_PROPERTY` records receipt only after giving, so a give that throws is retried on the next join. `giveOrDrop` adds the item to inventory and drops any remainder at the player's feet when full. A received flag prevents another grant on later joins, even if the player loses the item. Respawning after death does not trigger the grant. The starter list is defined by `STARTER_KINDS`; the second starter's choice belongs to the bulldozer topic.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0023](../adr/0023-starter-vehicle-on-first-join.md) Decision, "When a player joins the world for the first time, put one vehicle item in their inventory. If it is full, drop the item at their feet"; [0023](../adr/0023-starter-vehicle-on-first-join.md) Decision, "Players who joined before this change get the item on their next join, and players in creative mode get it as well"; [0023](../adr/0023-starter-vehicle-on-first-join.md) Consequences, "A player who loses the item (for example by dying with it in a lava pit) does not get another one; they craft it or ask an operator for `/give`"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/starter.ts` (`registerStarter`); `src/application/vehicle/giveStarterVehicle.ts` (`GiveStarterVehicle`); `src/domain/vehicle/placement/starter.ts` (`STARTER_KINDS`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`STARTER_RECEIVED_PROPERTY`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockInventory.ts`; `src/infrastructure/shared/players.ts` (`giveOrDrop`).

### Repeated hits pick up an empty vehicle

- Applies to: player hits on any vehicle or its hit-taking stand-in.
- Rule: `Vehicle.receiveHit` calls `registerHit` with `SPEC.hitsToBreak` and `SPEC.hitResetTicks`. A gap longer than the reset window restarts the count on the next hit. An occupied vehicle neither counts the hit nor breaks. On reaching the threshold, `HitVehicle` drops one item of the vehicle's kind at its location, then, when the vehicle carries placed decor, gives every decor item to the hitting player through `giveDecorItems`, then calls `retireVehicle` to remove the entity, discard the aggregate, and forget it in the fleet. Hits arrive through `world.afterEvents.entityHitEntity`; every drivable entity retains `minecraft:damage_sensor` cancelling all damage. Hits with a dye repaint instead and hits from a boat deck are ignored; see the paint and sports boat topics.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0008](../adr/0008-vehicle-item-and-breaking.md) Decision, "When a player punches a vehicle three times, remove the vehicle and drop one vehicle item. The count is a tuning value"; [0008](../adr/0008-vehicle-item-and-breaking.md) Decision, "Detect punches with `world.afterEvents.entityHitEntity`. Keep the vehicle's `damage_sensor` cancelling all damage"; [0008](../adr/0008-vehicle-item-and-breaking.md) Decision, "While the vehicle has occupants, punches are not counted (it does not break). So an occupant punching their own vehicle does not break it"; [0008](../adr/0008-vehicle-item-and-breaking.md) Decision, "Reset the count to 0 if the vehicle is not punched for a while (100 ticks)"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (`receiveHit`); `src/domain/shared/hitCounter.ts` (`registerHit`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/hitVehicle.ts` (`giveDecorItems` call); `src/application/vehicle/retireVehicle.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockFleetWorld.ts` (`dropVehicleItem`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/hits.ts`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/suv.se.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/camper.se.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/sportsboat.se.json`; `packs/yasu_ride_bp/entities/yasu_ride/bulldozer.se.json`.
- Verification: `tests/application/vehicle/hitVehicle.test.ts` "on the third hit, drops the item, removes the vehicle, and discards the aggregate" and "never counts hits from the deck toward breaking the vehicle"; `tests/domain/shared/hitCounter.test.ts` "breaks after 3 hits in quick succession with the vehicle rule", "ignores occupied hits and breaks exactly on the n-th free hit within the reset window", and "never breaks when every hit comes after more than the reset window". Not verified: player attack event wiring on a device by these unit tests.

### Fleet groups and limits

- Applies to: all vehicle kinds, players, and dimensions.
- Rule: A vehicle placed from an item records the placing player's `Player.id` in entity dynamic property `OWNER_PROPERTY`. Only `BedrockPlacementWorld.spawnVehicle` writes it. Owned vehicles form one group per owner and kind, limited to that kind's `profile.fleetLimit` through `fleetLimitOf`. Placing one more evicts that group's oldest vehicle. Vehicles without an owner share one group across kinds and dimensions, limited to `SPEC.maxUnownedVehicles`; another player's or kind's owned group does not consume that allowance. Restoration trims every group to its current limit. The current per-owner limits are SUV 10, sports boat 1, camper 1, and bulldozer 1, as defined by each profile's `fleetLimit`.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "A vehicle placed from its item records the placing player's `Player.id` as its owner in the entity dynamic property `yasu_ride:owner`. Nothing else writes it, and it never changes"; [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "Vehicles with an owner are limited per owner and per kind."; [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "Vehicles with no owner, whether summoned by command or placed before this change, share one world-wide group across all kinds, limited to 10 (`SPEC.maxUnownedVehicles`)."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockPlacementWorld.ts` (`spawnVehicle`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`OWNER_PROPERTY`, `fleetEntryOf`); `src/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.ts` (`Fleet`, `groupOf`, `trim`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/index.ts` (`fleetLimitOf`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/profile.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/profile.ts`.
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.test.ts` "evicts an owner's first SUV when their eleventh SUV arrives", "evicts an owner's first boat without evicting their SUVs or another owner's vehicles", and "trims each group on restoration while sharing one unowned group across kinds"; `tests/application/vehicle/placeVehicle.test.ts` "records the placing player as the owner of each vehicle (ADR 0066)"; in-game scenario `vehicle_limit`, `vehicleLimit` in `src/test-pack/scenarios/suv.ts`, registered in `src/test-pack/catalog.ts`, covers the unowned group. Not verified: `Player.id` stability across leaving and rejoining (0066 Consequences asks for a manual test row); owned groups in game, because the test pack cannot set their owner.

### Joining the fleet and eviction

- Applies to: spawned, discovered, and evicted vehicles.
- Rule: `LimitVehicles.spawned` admits an entity spawned with any cause except `Loaded` as newest. `found` admits a vehicle whose spawn the fleet never saw as oldest, at script start, `entityLoad`, or `entitySpawn` with cause `Loaded`. Known ids keep their order; invalid entities removed by an earlier load event are skipped. Fleet order is oldest first. Loaded evictions are removed through `retireVehicle`, with riders dismounted by `Entity.remove`, no vehicle item dropped, and each successful removal logged by `log.warn`. `VehicleReleaser` releases lights, dock blocks, deck seats, fillers, stand-ins, and the hold pose. An evicted vehicle in an unloaded chunk waits for a later load. The world-wide evicted list keeps the latest `SPEC.maxEvicted`; an id dropped from it rejoins as oldest when found again. A failed removal leaves its aggregate and fleet entry for retry rather than silently discarding them.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "Everything else in 0038 still holds: how vehicles join (spawn as newest, found as oldest), eviction of unloaded vehicles, and a vehicle broken by hand leaving the fleet"; [0038](../adr/0038-keep-at-most-ten-vehicles-in-a-world.md) Decision, "A vehicle created from the item or by a command (`entitySpawn` with any cause but `Loaded`) joins as the newest."; [0038](../adr/0038-keep-at-most-ten-vehicles-in-a-world.md) Decision, "A vehicle the fleet never saw spawn joins as the oldest."; [0038](../adr/0038-keep-at-most-ten-vehicles-in-a-world.md) Decision, "An evicted vehicle is removed as soon as it is loaded, even with riders; `Entity.remove()` dismounts them. It drops no item, and each removal is logged."; [0038](../adr/0038-keep-at-most-ten-vehicles-in-a-world.md) Decision, "The list keeps the latest 64 (`SPEC.maxEvicted`); a vehicle dropped from it joins again as the oldest when it loads"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/fleet.ts` (`registerFleet`); `src/application/vehicle/limitVehicles.ts` (`start`, `spawned`, `found`, `removeLoadedEvicted`); `src/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/retireVehicle.ts`; `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockFleetWorld.ts` (`removeVehicle`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/vehicleReleaser.ts` (`VehicleReleaser.release`).
- Verification: `tests/application/vehicle/limitVehicles.test.ts` "removes the oldest loaded vehicle when a new one spawns over the limit", "removes nothing else when several spawn in one tick before their events arrive", "removes an evicted vehicle in an unloaded chunk when its chunk loads", "counts a vehicle it never saw spawn as older than the ones it did", and "keeps removing the others and saves the fleet when one removal fails"; `tests/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.test.ts` "keeps only the latest evictions once the evicted list is full". Not verified: event wiring and rider dismounting by these unit tests.

### Stored fleet and older saves

- Applies to: fleet persistence across reloads and server restarts.
- Rule: The fleet is JSON in world dynamic property `FLEET_PROPERTY`. `serializeFleetState` writes `FLEET_VERSION`, currently version 3, with oldest-first order entries containing id, kind, and optional owner; current eviction entries retain kind and optional owner too. Version 2 keeps its order and converts evictions to id-only entries, which can never be taken back. An unversioned save keeps its id-only evictions and drops its order; found vehicles rejoin as oldest with their real kind and owner. Id-only evictions remain id-only when saved again. A malformed or unsupported value is logged by `DynamicPropertyFleetRepository.load`, and the fleet starts over empty.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0038](../adr/0038-keep-at-most-ten-vehicles-in-a-world.md) Decision, "A malformed value is logged and the fleet starts over"; [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "The fleet stays in the world dynamic property `yasu_ride:fleet`, oldest first, and stores each vehicle's id, kind, and owner if any"; [0066](../adr/0066-limit-vehicles-per-player-and-kind.md) Decision, "A fleet stored by an earlier build (`{\"order\": [...], \"evicted\": [...]}` with plain ids) keeps its evicted list and drops its order."; [0124](../adr/0124-take-back-an-eviction-when-a-slot-frees.md) Decision, "The evicted list stores the kind and owner of each vehicle, so the fleet can tell its group. The stored fleet becomes version 3. A version 2 or older save keeps its evictions by id only, and those are never taken back"
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/persistence/fleetJson.ts` (`FLEET_VERSION`, `parseFleetState`, `serializeFleetState`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/persistence/dynamicPropertyFleetRepository.ts` (`DynamicPropertyFleetRepository.load`, `save`); `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/identifiers.ts` (`FLEET_PROPERTY`).
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/persistence/fleetJson.test.ts` "drops the legacy order and keeps its evictions (ADR 0066)", "keeps a version 2 order and its evictions by id only", and "round-trips every valid state with optional owners through version 3"; `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/persistence/dynamicPropertyFleetRepository.test.ts` "loads back the order and the evicted list it saved" and "starts over with an empty fleet and warns when the stored fleet is malformed".

### Taking back an eviction when a slot frees

- Applies to: a tracked vehicle leaving the fleet, including pickup and confirmed removal.
- Rule: `Fleet.forget` removes the vehicle from order and evictions. If it held a slot, the latest eviction of its group leaves the evicted list and returns as oldest of its group. Groups are owner and kind, or the shared unowned group. An id-only legacy eviction is never taken back. Removing an already evicted vehicle on load frees no slot and takes nothing back; forgetting an unknown id changes nothing.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0124](../adr/0124-take-back-an-eviction-when-a-slot-frees.md) Decision, "When a vehicle that holds a slot leaves the fleet (a break by hand, 0008), the latest vehicle evicted from the same group, if any, leaves the evicted list and goes back into the order as the oldest of its group."; [0124](../adr/0124-take-back-an-eviction-when-a-slot-frees.md) Decision, "Only a slot freed in the same group takes an eviction back. The group is the owner and kind, or the one unowned group (0066)"; [0124](../adr/0124-take-back-an-eviction-when-a-slot-frees.md) Decision, "Removing an evicted vehicle when it loads frees no slot, so it takes nothing back"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.ts` (`Fleet.forget`, `recall`, `groupOf`); `src/application/vehicle/retireVehicle.ts`; `src/application/vehicle/limitVehicles.ts` (`removed`).
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.test.ts` "takes the latest eviction back into the slot a vehicle broken by hand frees", "takes back only an eviction of the freed group", "never takes back an eviction stored with its id only", and "matches a per-group model through random admissions, discoveries, and removals".

### Forgetting removal from a loaded chunk

- Applies to: vehicle removal and death events.
- Rule: `registerFleet` captures id, dimension, and location in `world.beforeEvents.entityRemove`, then defers the probe and fleet write through `system.run`. `chunkLoadedAt` treats a defined block as loaded and an undefined result or exception as unloaded. `LimitVehicles.removed` forgets the vehicle, including any eviction take-back, when the chunk is loaded or when `LimitVehicles.died` previously recorded its death. Death records exist only in memory and are consumed by one removal; death alone does not free a slot. An unloaded removal without a recorded death preserves the vehicle's age. Unknown ids change nothing, and a later remove event for a vehicle already retired by pickup or eviction has no further effect. After a confirmed removal the use case also retries loaded evictions. An engine-raised removal's deferred read can run in the same tick; it is not necessarily a next-tick read.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0125](../adr/0125-forget-a-vehicle-removed-from-a-loaded-chunk.md) Decision, "When `world.beforeEvents.entityRemove` fires for a vehicle type, capture its id, dimension, and location while the entity is still readable."; [0125](../adr/0125-forget-a-vehicle-removed-from-a-loaded-chunk.md) Decision, "A defined block means the chunk remains loaded and the vehicle is gone: forget its id from the fleet, including the take-back of the latest eviction from its group under 0124"; [0125](../adr/0125-forget-a-vehicle-removed-from-a-loaded-chunk.md) Decision, "An undefined block or a thrown block lookup is treated as unloaded: keep the vehicle in the fleet with its existing age"; [0125](../adr/0125-forget-a-vehicle-removed-from-a-loaded-chunk.md) Decision, "a vehicle that died before its removal is forgotten even if the block read fails."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/handlers/fleet.ts` (`registerFleet`, `chunkLoadedAt`); `src/application/vehicle/limitVehicles.ts` (`LimitVehicles.removed`, `died`, `dying`); `src/domain/vehicle/fleet/fleet.ts` (`forget`).
