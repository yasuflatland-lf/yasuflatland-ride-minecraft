## Rules

### The script sets the velocity every tick

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, bulldozer; every form and medium.
- Rule: The engine does not move a vehicle. Each tick the script computes the vehicle's velocity from its internal speed and heading and gives the engine the difference as an impulse. The horizontal velocity it aims for never exceeds the kind's `maxSpeed` (`targetVelocity` caps its length). The cap bounds the speed the vehicle aims for, not the speed it reaches: right after it leaves a slippery surface where the friction estimate sat at `MOTION.frictionMin` (0.2) for a normal surface, the estimate has not caught up for some ticks, and one tick's velocity can reach `maxSpeed / MOTION.frictionMin` (4.5 blocks/tick at a `maxSpeed` of 0.9). In the SUV simulation, switching from a surface that keeps 0.2 to one that keeps 0.69 moves 3.1 blocks on the first tick and exceeds 1.0 on 9 ticks. The values that shape handling (speeds, acceleration, braking, drag, turn rate, steering full speed, grip) are not scattered through the logic; each kind declares them in its spec table and passes them to the motion functions as `MotionLimits` (`TyreLimits` for the two values only the ground step reads).
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0002](../adr/0002-script-driven-vehicle-movement.md) Decision, "Adopt approach 1. The vehicle entity is defined as a \"box you can ride\", and all movement is computed in the script."
- Basis: [0002](../adr/0002-script-driven-vehicle-movement.md) Decision, "Cap the magnitude of horizontal velocity at the max speed every tick."
- Basis: [0002](../adr/0002-script-driven-vehicle-movement.md) Decision, "Keep the tuning values in one place"
- Basis: [0082](../adr/0082-learn-strongly-slowing-surfaces.md) Consequences, "Leaving a slow surface, the estimate catches up through the moving average, so for some ticks the engine keeps more of the pre-divided velocity than the target."

### Speed, braking, and steering

- Applies to: SUV, camper (car form); sports boat afloat; bulldozer; every kind that calls `updateSpeed` and `steerYaw`.
- Rule: Each tick `updateSpeed` changes the internal speed (blocks per tick, negative in reverse) from the throttle. Forward throttle at rest or while moving forward adds `accel` times the throttle. Reverse throttle at rest or while reversing adds `reverseAccel` times the throttle backward. With no throttle the speed shrinks toward 0 by `drag` and does not cross 0. A throttle against the direction of travel removes `max(drag, brake × |throttle|)` from the speed, so a light pull on the stick never slows the vehicle less than releasing it; that tick may cross 0. The result is clamped to `-maxReverse` and `maxSpeed`. `steerYaw` turns the heading by `steer × turnRate` scaled by `min(1, |speed| / steerFullSpeed)` and by the sign of the speed, so a stopped vehicle does not turn and a reversing one turns the other way. Left input (`steer > 0`) lowers the yaw.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0002](../adr/0002-script-driven-vehicle-movement.md) Context, "We want arcade-style driving: acceleration and coasting, turning as soon as you steer, and sliding sideways under the handbrake."
- Basis: [0074](../adr/0074-light-braking-at-least-coasting.md) Decision, "In one tick, braking removes `max(drag, brake × |throttle|)` from the speed: `throttle > 0` while reversing adds it, `throttle < 0` while moving forward subtracts it"
- Basis: [0074](../adr/0074-light-braking-at-least-coasting.md) Decision, "Acceleration, reverse acceleration, creep, coasting and the speed limits are unchanged. A braking tick may still cross zero, as before"
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Context, "steering is reversed when backing up (0002)"
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "it is above the speed at which steering reaches full effect (0.08), so the vehicle turns at its full rate"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`updateSpeed`, `steerYaw`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` (`SUV_SPEC`: `maxSpeed`, `maxReverse`, `accel`, `reverseAccel`, `brake`, `drag`, `turnRate`, `steerFullSpeed`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts`

### Sideways grip and the handbrake

- Applies to: SUV and camper in car form on the ground; the sports boat and the bulldozer have no handbrake.
- Rule: Each tick the target velocity keeps `1 - grip` of the sideways component of the observed velocity (except during a step jump and on a tick that reads a wall, see those rules) and sets the forward component to the internal speed. With Jump held in car form the grip is the kind's `handbrakeGrip` (`TyreLimits`), which is far lower than `grip`, so the vehicle slides. Out of car form (takeoff, hover, landing, submarine) Jump is not a handbrake.

- Basis: [0089](../adr/0089-per-kind-control-mapping.md) Decision, "The tyre values move to `TyreLimits { handbrakeGrip, creepSpeed }`, which only the ground step (`stepMotion`) takes."

### Stick to throttle, steering, and the sideways stick

- Applies to: SUV, camper, sports boat, through `toDriveInput`; PC keyboard and mobile joystick alike. The bulldozer reads only the throttle from it (see Open issues).
- Rule: The forward/back stick value becomes the throttle: 0 while its magnitude is at most `SPEC.throttleDeadzone`, otherwise `sign × min(1, |value| / SPEC.throttleFullAt)`, so pushing the stick half way gives full throttle and a hard turn on a phone keeps full throttle. The left/right value, clamped to -1..1, is the steering. When the left/right value is past the deadzone and the stick is within `SPEC.sidewaysAngle` of horizontal (`|moveY| <= tan(angle) × |moveX|`), the throttle is 0 and the input is marked `sideways` (see Creep). To reverse, the stick must point more than that angle below horizontal. A missing input, or a stick value that is not a finite number, gives the neutral control with `jump` false. The mapping does not branch on device type.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Keep 0005's mapping: when the forward/back value exceeds 0.1, the throttle is `sign × min(1, |value| / 0.5)`, and the left/right value is the steering"
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Exception: when the left/right value exceeds 0.1 and the stick is within 20 degrees of horizontal (`|moveY| ≤ tan(20°) × |moveX|`), ignore the forward/back value and creep instead"
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Do not branch on device type, as in 0005"
- Basis: [0089](../adr/0089-per-kind-control-mapping.md) Decision, "A missing input, or a stick value that is not a finite number, gives the neutral control, with `jump` false."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/driveInput.ts` (`toDriveInput`, `NEUTRAL_CONTROL`); `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts` (`SPEC.throttleDeadzone`, `SPEC.throttleFullAt`, `SPEC.sidewaysAngle`)

### Creep

- Applies to: SUV and camper in every form except takeoff (which ignores the controls); the sports boat afloat. The sports boat beached and the bulldozer never creep.
- Rule: While the control is `sideways`, the speed moves to the kind's `creepSpeed` and holds there. From a stop, or from reversing, it speeds up (or brakes) to `creepSpeed`; above `creepSpeed` it coasts down by `drag` and stops there. For the SUV, `creepSpeed` is below `SPEC.ramMinSpeed`, so a vehicle that has settled at the creep speed never damages mobs (a vehicle still coasting down from a higher speed can, because ramming reads the speed, not the creep flag), and above `steerFullSpeed`, so it turns at its full rate. Creep needs no forward throttle, so a creeping vehicle never jumps a step.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Creeping speeds the vehicle up (or brakes it out of reverse) to 0.12 blocks/tick and holds it there. Above 0.12 it coasts down with the usual drag and stops at 0.12."
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Creeping never jumps a two-block step (0004), because that needs forward throttle"
- Basis: [0089](../adr/0089-per-kind-control-mapping.md) Decision, "The sports boat: afloat, `sideways` is creep and `jump` does nothing."
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`updateSpeed`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` (`creepSpeed`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts` (`SPEC.ramMinSpeed`)

### Ground friction estimate

- Applies to: car form on the ground (SUV, camper, bulldozer, and the beached sports boat through `stepMotion`).
- Rule: The engine's ground friction scales the velocity the script sets, so `stepMotion` estimates it and divides the target velocity by the estimate before giving it as an impulse. The estimate is the fraction of last tick's given velocity that remains in the direction it was given, clamped to `MOTION.frictionMin`..`MOTION.frictionMax`. The first sample becomes the estimate; later samples are blended in with weight `MOTION.frictionEma`. Until a first sample exists the estimate is `MOTION.defaultFriction`. A sample is taken only when this tick and the last were on the ground, the given velocity was long enough (`MOTION.minExpectedForFriction`), and no wall was read on this tick. While a wall is read the estimate is frozen. A surface that keeps at least `MOTION.frictionMin` of the velocity each tick is learned and the vehicle reaches its normal speed on it (soul sand is the example). A surface that keeps `k` with `MOTION.wallKeptMax` (0.15) <= `k` < `MOTION.frictionMin` is learned as `frictionMin`, so top speed is capped at `k / frictionMin × maxSpeed`. A surface that keeps less than `wallKeptMax` is read as a wall and the vehicle nearly stops (see "Which axes are walls on the ground").
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Decision, "Estimate the decay rate from the fraction of the previous tick's velocity that remains in the direction it was given."
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Decision, "update it with an exponential moving average (weight 0.2 on the new value)"
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Decision, "While colliding, stop updating the decay estimate"
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Context, "Estimating the decay rate from the ratio of the current velocity to the velocity given in the previous tick, and adding the difference back, made the vehicle drive at the commanded speed."
- Basis: [0082](../adr/0082-learn-strongly-slowing-surfaces.md) Decision, "A tick with no wall axis takes a friction sample as before, now clamped to 0.2-1 (frictionMin 0.2)."
- Basis: [0082](../adr/0082-learn-strongly-slowing-surfaces.md) Decision, "The sample threshold (0.05) and the exponential moving average (0.2) are unchanged."
- Basis: [0082](../adr/0082-learn-strongly-slowing-surfaces.md) Consequences, "Surfaces that keep between 0.15 and 0.2 are learned as 0.2, so top speed is capped at k / 0.2 x 0.9"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`stepMotion`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts` (`MOTION.defaultFriction`, `frictionMin`, `frictionMax`, `frictionEma`, `minExpectedForFriction`)

### Which axes are walls on the ground

- Applies to: car form on the ground, when the previous tick's given velocity and the observed velocity are known and the vehicle is on the ground.
- Rule: The engine zeroes velocity on an axis that hits a wall, so `groundWallAxes` reads x and z separately. An axis is driven when the velocity given on it last tick was above `MOTION.blockedAxisMin`; an axis that is not driven is never a wall. A driven axis is a wall when it kept less than `MOTION.wallKeptMax` of the given velocity, or when it kept less than `MOTION.blockedRatio` of the friction estimate while the other axis is driven and kept at least that much by more than float rounding (`MOTION.axisRatioTie`). A uniform slowdown that keeps at least `MOTION.wallKeptMax` on every driven axis is therefore a slow surface, not a wall; one that keeps less is read as a wall.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Decision, "The engine zeroes velocity per axis when it hits a wall, so detect collisions separately on the x and z axes"
- Basis: [0003](../adr/0003-friction-compensation-and-axis-wall-detection.md) Decision, "Skip an axis when the velocity given on it is 0.03 or less"
- Basis: [0082](../adr/0082-learn-strongly-slowing-surfaces.md) Decision, "On the ground (stepMotion), an axis is driven when the velocity given on it last tick is above 0.03 (blockedAxisMin)."

### Which axes are walls in a medium

- Applies to: the medium step (SUV and camper out of car form; the sports boat afloat; the bulldozer in its hover and submarine forms).
- Rule: `blockedAxes` checks each axis on its own: a driven axis (given velocity above `MOTION.blockedAxisMin`) is blocked when it kept less than `MOTION.blockedRatio` of the medium's wall drag (the drag, or `Medium.wallDrag` when the medium sets one) of the given velocity. The medium step takes no friction sample, and the sports boat's water-drag estimator takes no sample on a tick on which the check fires.

### Speed after a wall hit: slide, never reverse

- Applies to: every kind and form that uses `stepMotion` or `stepMediumMotion`: SUV and camper in car, hover, and submarine forms, the sports boat afloat and beached, the bulldozer.
- Rule: On a tick that reads a wall the internal speed is lowered by `speedAfterWall` and never rises or changes sign (`speed = sign(speed) × min(|speed|, kept)`). With exactly one axis blocked, a heading component along the free axis of at least `MOTION.wallSlideMinCos`, and the vehicle still moving along the wall the way its heading points, `kept` is the speed whose free-axis component equals the observed along-wall velocity, so a vehicle that hits a wall at an angle keeps its along-wall speed and slides. Otherwise (both axes blocked, nearly head-on, or moving backward along the wall) `kept` is the observed forward speed, never below 0, so a wall can stop a vehicle but never turns it around: a body bounced backward ends the tick at speed 0. A blocked tick aims for no sideways velocity (`keepLat` 0). The friction estimate is frozen during contact. A kind may add its own guard after the motion step; the camper's end guard (`guardEnds`) stops it dead at a wall ahead of its nose or tail.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0077](../adr/0077-never-reverse-the-internal-speed-on-a-wall-hit.md) Decision, "This applies to the ground step and to the gravity-off step (air, water and the floating sports boat) alike"
- Basis: [0077](../adr/0077-never-reverse-the-internal-speed-on-a-wall-hit.md) Consequences, "A wall can stop a vehicle but never turns it around; a body bounced backward ends the tick at speed 0, and the next tick accelerates from 0"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Decision, "When exactly one axis is blocked and the heading's component along the free axis is at least `MOTION.wallSlideMinCos` (0.05, about 2.9 degrees from head-on), and the vehicle still moves along the wall the way its heading points, the internal speed becomes the speed whose free-axis component equals the observed along-wall velocity: |v_free| / |f_free|"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Decision, "Otherwise (both axes blocked, nearly head-on, or moving backward along the wall) it becomes the observed forward speed, never of the opposite sign, as in 0077"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Decision, "In both cases the speed never rises and never changes sign: speed = sign(speed) x min(|speed|, kept)"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Decision, "On a blocked tick the target carries no sideways velocity (the same as during a step jump, 0004), so the along-wall speed stays at its impact value instead of growing"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Consequences, "Within about 2.9 degrees of head-on the vehicle stops as before; it does not creep along the wall"
- Basis: [0081](../adr/0081-slide-along-walls.md) Consequences, "The camper's end guard (0067) runs after the motion step and still stops it dead when the cells ahead of its nose or tail are solid"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`speedAfterWall`, `stepMotion`, `stepMediumMotion`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts` (`MOTION.wallSlideMinCos`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/bodyGuard.ts` (`guardEnds`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.test.ts` "a wall tick never flips the sign of the internal speed and never raises it (ADR 0077)", "a wall that bounces a forward vehicle slightly backward stops it at 0 (ADR 0077)", "slides along a wall hit at an angle, keeping the impact along-wall speed (ADR 0081)", "stops at a wall hit nearly head-on, as before (ADR 0081)", "a blocked tick carries no sideways velocity (ADR 0081)", "speedAfterWall never raises the speed and never changes its sign", "speedAfterWall keeps the speed whose free-axis component matches the along-wall velocity", "speedAfterWall falls back to the forward speed when both axes are blocked, nearly head-on, or moving backward along the wall"; `tests/domain/vehicle/profile.test.ts` "a wall hit never turns any profile's forward speed into reverse (ADR 0077)"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/mediumMotion.test.ts` "slides along a wall hit at an angle in a medium (ADR 0081)", "a wall that bounces a body backward stops it at 0 instead of reversing (ADR 0077)"; in-game scenario `wall_diagonal` (`src/test-pack/scenarios/suv.ts`). ### Step jumps for one- and two-block steps

- Applies to: car form on the ground for the SUV and camper; the bulldozer while its blade is raised; not the sports boat beached, which passes no step probe. A kind can turn the probe off (`TransformingOptions.stepUp`, `stepUpEnabled()`); this comes from the implementation, not from an ADR quote.
- Rule: While driving with forward throttle, non-negative speed, and the vehicle on the ground, the script looks ahead for a step and jumps onto it. `stepHeight` scans from the front edge of the collision box (`hitboxFront`) out to `speed × MOTION.stepLookaheadTicks + MOTION.stepLookaheadMargin` along three lines: the centerline and `±MOTION.stepLaneOffset`. A cell with a solid block half a block above the feet is a step of height 1, or of height 2 if the cell above is solid too. The SUV uses `hitboxFront=1.34` and `carHeight=2.36`, requiring three vertical clearance cells. A step needs room for the kind's `carHeight` above it; a step with no room, or a block three or more high, is a wall and the scan returns no jump. When rays at the same distance find both heights, the higher wins. A one-block step jumps at `MOTION.oneStepVelocity`; a two-block step at `MOTION.stepVelocity`. The jump raises the internal speed to at least `MOTION.stepMinSpeed` and then, for `MOTION.stepTicks` ticks, the vehicle keeps full control in the air with no sideways velocity. Nothing is jumped with no forward throttle, so creeping, coasting, and reversing never jump.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0217](../adr/0217-derive-the-suv-spec-from-the-rubicon-body.md) Decision, "The square collision box is 2.68 blocks wide and 2.36 blocks high."
- Basis: [0004](../adr/0004-two-block-step-up-by-look-ahead.md) Decision, "The look-ahead distance is \"speed × 4 + 0.3\" blocks, measured from the front edge of the collision box"
- Basis: [0004](../adr/0004-two-block-step-up-by-look-ahead.md) Decision, "Look ahead along three lines: the centerline and the left and right edges (±0.8 from the center)."
- Basis: [0004](../adr/0004-two-block-step-up-by-look-ahead.md) Decision, "For 10 ticks after the jump, keep controlling the vehicle in the air and cancel sideways velocity"
- Basis: [0035](../adr/0035-jump-one-block-steps-in-the-script.md) Decision, "The look-ahead of 0004 reports the height of the step to jump: 1 or 2 blocks, or none. A one-block step needs room for `SPEC.carHeight` above it, as a two-block step does. When rays at the same distance find both heights, the higher one wins"
- Basis: [0035](../adr/0035-jump-one-block-steps-in-the-script.md) Decision, "A one-block step jumps at `SPEC.oneStepVelocity` (0.45, peaking about 1.4 blocks up; a player's jump of 0.42 peaks at 1.25). A two-block step keeps `SPEC.stepVelocity` (0.75). Everything else from 0004 stays: the minimum speed, the 10 ticks of air control, and the three rays"
- Basis: [0018](../adr/0018-sideways-stick-creeps-forward.md) Decision, "Creeping never jumps a two-block step (0004), because that needs forward throttle"
- Implementation: `src/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.ts` (`stepHeight`, `STEP_LANES`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts` (`stepMotion`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts` (`MOTION.stepVelocity`, `oneStepVelocity`, `stepTicks`, `stepMinSpeed`, `stepLookaheadTicks`, `stepLookaheadMargin`, `stepLaneOffset`, `stepScanStep`); `src/domain/vehicle/motion/transformingDrive.ts` (`stepProbe`, `stepUpEnabled`); `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` (`hitboxFront`, `carHeight`)
- Verification: `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.test.ts` "jumps for a step of height 2 within look-ahead range (on any ray)", "jumps a height-1 step within look-ahead range, which a controlling rider keeps the engine from stepping (ADR 0035)", "jumps the higher step whichever rays at the same distance find heights 1 and 2", "does not jump when all obstacles have a height of 3 or more", "does not jump if there is no room (SUV_SPEC.carHeight) for the vehicle above a step of height 2", "does not jump a height-1 step without room (SUV_SPEC.carHeight) above it, but does with the room", "does not jump yet for a step beyond look-ahead range"; `tests/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.test.ts` "jumps up a step only on the ground with forward throttle and non-negative speed", "drops all sideways velocity while climbing a step", "jumps a one-block step with the lower velocity (ADR 0035)", "reports an upward jump when jumping up a step and raises internal speed to the minimum"; in-game scenarios `step2_front`, `step2_diagonal`, `accel_and_step1` (`src/test-pack/catalog.ts`). Not verified: climbing a one-block step with a rider in game (0035 Consequences: a script cannot test it; manual test row 5); a step that catches only the vehicle's edge between the lanes (0004 Consequences).

### Which blocks count as solid

- Applies to: every look-ahead and ground probe that asks `solidAt` (the step probe, the landing gear probe).
- Rule: A cell counts as solid only if a ray cast down from just above it, ignoring passable blocks and liquids, hits that cell. Air, liquid, and light blocks are not solid, and flowers, grass, carpets, snow layers, and torches have no collision box, so they are not obstacles: a two-block step with a flower on top is climbed. A cell with a block directly above it that is neither air nor liquid counts as solid without a ray, because a ray starting inside a block finds nothing.
- Agreement: Accepted
- Basis: [0035](../adr/0035-jump-one-block-steps-in-the-script.md) Decision, "`solidAt` counts a cell as solid only if a ray cast down from just above it, ignoring passable blocks and liquids, hits that cell."
- Basis: [0035](../adr/0035-jump-one-block-steps-in-the-script.md) Consequences, "Flowers, grass, carpets and torches no longer count as obstacles, for steps and for the landing gear probe (0033)."
- Implementation: `src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts` (`solidAt`)
- Verification: `tests/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.test.ts` "counts a cell solid only when a downward ray stops on it (ADR 0035)", "counts a cell under another block as solid without a ray"; in-game scenario `passable_no_hop` (`src/test-pack/catalog.ts`). Not verified: that the stable `getBlockFromRay` ignores passable blocks the same way on every Bedrock version.

The script's one-block jump does not depend on it: a controlling rider turns the engine's auto step off.

## Open issues

- ADR 0002 says to keep the tuning values in one place; the implementation keeps them in several tables: `SPEC` (`src/domain/vehicle/spec.ts`), `MOTION` (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts`), and one spec table per kind (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts` and the others); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0002 says a vehicle turns to the driver's view when a player takes the driver seat while it is stopped, with no exception; the implementation skips the turn for a parked camper (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/camper/drive.ts`, `onDriverMounted`) and a docked sports boat (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`, `onDriverMounted`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0074 says every kind uses `brake` 0.04 and `drag` 0.02; the bulldozer uses a `brake` of 0.02 and a `drag` of 0.01 (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/spec.ts`), so for it `brake × |throttle|` is at most `drag` for a throttle of 0.5 or less in magnitude, and a light pull equals coasting there; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0075 says `MotionLimits` still carries `handbrakeGrip` and the sports boat still declares one; the implementation moved both into `TyreLimits` and the boat declares none (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/limits.ts`, `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/spec.ts`), as ADR 0089 decided; the 0075 Consequences needs an edit.
- ADR 0081 says the per-axis detection of 0003 stays with the same thresholds; on the ground the implementation uses `groundWallAxes`, which adds the `wallKeptMax` rule and the driven-axis rule of ADR 0082 (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts`). ADR 0082 supersedes only 0003, so 0081's Decision still reads as the old rule; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0082 says a driven axis is a wall when it kept less than half the friction estimate while the other driven axis kept at least half; the implementation also requires the other axis to exceed it by more than `MOTION.axisRatioTie` times itself, so a uniform surface whose ratios differ only by rounding is not a wall (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/motion.ts`, `groundWallAxes`; `src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts`, issue 340); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0004 says a two-block step needs at least 1.6 blocks of clear space above it, and ADR 0035 names `SPEC.carHeight`, `SPEC.oneStepVelocity`, and `SPEC.stepVelocity`; the implementation probes one cell per block of the kind's own `carHeight` above the step (three cells for the SUV, `src/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.ts`, `clearanceProbes`), and the velocities live in `MOTION` (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/constants.ts`) while `carHeight` is a per-kind spec value (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/suv/spec.ts`); `SPEC` has none of the three; the ADRs need an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0004 says not to jump if any of the three lines has an obstacle three blocks or higher; the implementation scans outward and returns the height of the nearest step it finds, so a taller wall that lies beyond that step on any line is not examined (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.ts`, `stepHeight`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0004 and ADR 0035 do not mention an overhead check; the implementation returns no jump when a block is directly above the vehicle's center at the ceiling of its body height, and starts the scan 0.1 blocks past the front edge in steps of `MOTION.stepScanStep` (`src/domain/vehicle/motion/stepUp.ts`); the ADRs need an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0035 says a cell under another non-air block counts as solid without a ray; the implementation does not count a liquid above as a block, so a cell under water or lava still takes the ray (`src/infrastructure/vehicle/bedrock/bedrockVehicleWorld.ts`, `solidAt`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0035 says that with or without a rider the script hops one-block steps; the sports boat passes no step probe and the bulldozer probes only with its blade raised (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`; `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.ts`, `stepUpEnabled`); the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- The test titled "leaves one-block steps to the engine (ADR 0004)" in `tests/packs/behaviorPack.test.ts` states what ADR 0035 superseded: the script now jumps one-block steps, and the test only checks the `variable_max_auto_step` value; the title needs an edit.
- ADR 0089 describes the mapping for the SUV, camper, and sports boat only; the bulldozer maps the stick itself (`bulldozerInput` in `src/domain/vehicle/kinds/bulldozer/drive.ts`: a stick past `pivotStick` pivots in place, and it has no handbrake or creep), which ADR 0136 extends and which this spec does not describe; the bulldozer topic must state it.
- ADR 0089 leaves open whether the sports boat should keep creeping afloat; the implementation still creeps (`src/domain/vehicle/kinds/sportsboat/drive.ts`, `afloatInput`); the product question is undecided.
- ADR 0004 calls the step look-ahead lanes (±0.8) the vehicle's edges; the current kinds are wider (the SUV collision box is 2.68 wide, a half width of 1.34; the camper's `bodyHalfWidth` is 1.5), so a one-block step only in the outer band 0.9 to 1.3 to the side is read as a wall at jump height 0. Moving the lanes outward could jump high side walls by mistake, so the code is unchanged; whether each kind needs its own lane is undecided; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
- ADR 0004 says the jump peaks about 0.9 blocks above the top of the step; no measurement backs that, and the model of ADR 0035 (gravity 0.08, drag 0.98: 0.42 reaches 1.25 and 0.45 reaches 1.42) puts a 0.75 jump higher. Two-block steps are climbed, so this is not a defect; the real peak is not measured; the ADR needs an edit or a superseding ADR.
