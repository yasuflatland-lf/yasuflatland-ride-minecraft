# Bulldozer switches

Switches the bulldozer's driver uses from the Bulldozer Switches group of the Items tab. Back to the [item groups](README.md).

| Item | Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Blade Up/Down | Shapeless, a lever and an iron ingot | Hold it while driving the bulldozer and use it (right-click on PC, long press on mobile) to raise or lower the blade; a held press switches once ([ADR 0138](../adr/0138-raise-and-lower-the-bulldozer-blade.md)). While you hold it, the cab monitor shows UP or DOWN for the blade ([ADR 0139](../adr/0139-show-the-held-tool-on-the-cab-monitor.md)). |
